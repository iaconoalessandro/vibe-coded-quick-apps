## Do not translate

- **Brand:** Admetia.
- **Degrees and tests:** MBA, MSc, MiM, MFin, MFE, EMBA, GMAT, GMAT Focus, GRE, IELTS, TOEFL, TOEIC, ECTS, GPA, CFA, CPA, ACCA, STEM.
- **Schools and universities:** every school, university and programme name as written (Bocconi, LSE, HEC Paris, ESCP, Imperial, INSEAD…). Translate only a generic description around it.
- **Companies and organisations:** Deloitte, KPMG, EY, PwC, BCG, Accenture, HSBC, Nordea, SEB, Swedbank, Aramco, Novo Nordisk, Heineken, LinkedIn, Wise, Bolt and every other company name. `Big Four` stays in English (`las Big Four`).
- **Sources and indices:** Eurostat, EURES, Startup Genome, Global Financial Centres Index, EF (English Proficiency Index), IND, BEA, Qiwa. Institutions with an official Spanish name use it: World Bank → Banco Mundial, European Central Bank → Banco Central Europeo, European Commission → Comisión Europea, United Nations → Naciones Unidas, UK Foreign Office → Ministerio de Asuntos Exteriores británico.
- **Official local names** of visas, permits, contracts, exams and offices in the source language: Employment Pass, Werkstudent, alternance, stage (Italy), Schengen, H-1B, OPT, Skilled Worker, Graduate Route.
- **Currency codes:** CHF, QAR, AED, NOK, SEK, DKK, PLN, NIS, HK$, S$.
- **Technology names:** Python, SQL, Excel, GitHub and every programming language, product or framework.
- **Formats and codes:** PDF, A4, ID, URLs, e-mail addresses, anything in `{braces}` or inside `<tags>`.
