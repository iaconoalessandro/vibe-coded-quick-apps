# Review: atlas-il-01 (Israel country guide)

Six fixes. No problems with tags, placeholders, figures or formats; all 57 ids match the source in order.

## Fixes
- atlas-il.0036: "salidas" -> "desinversiones (exits)". Glossary term for start-up exits.
- atlas-il.0046: the Nefesh B'Nefesh attribution had been extended to the contracts clause. The source attributes only the CV claim to it. Now "Los contratos ... deben redactarse en hebreo; según Nefesh B'Nefesh, un CV ...".
- atlas-il.0047, 0049, 0051 (repeated fix): "survey" had been translated "estudio"; changed to "encuesta" (in 0051 also "el estudio es antiguo" -> "la encuesta es antigua").
- atlas-il.0004: "la clasificación de su ecosistema de Startup Genome" was ambiguous about whose ecosystem. Now "la clasificación que hace Startup Genome de su ecosistema".

## Doubts for a native speaker
- atlas-il.0010: "standing" rendered "posición internacional" (adds "internacional"). The source is vague. Left as is.
- Accepted: atlas-il.0049 "servicio militar" (reasonable gloss of "service" in the Israeli context), atlas-il.0038 "Beerseba" (RAE form), "R&D Israel" kept as a unit name (0057).
- "Oficina Central de Estadística" (0008, 0038, 0048) is acceptable. The plural "Estadísticas" is also common in Spanish press about Israel.
- "Instituto Nacional de Seguros" (0008) for National Insurance Institute (Bituach Leumi): no established Spanish name found.
