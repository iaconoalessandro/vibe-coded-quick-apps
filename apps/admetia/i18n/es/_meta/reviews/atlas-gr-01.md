# Review: atlas-gr-01 (es-ES)

All 63 ids compared with the English. Figures, dates, number formats, placeholders and tags all match. 9 fixes.

## Fixes
- Public bodies and companies named in English, translated per the brief (4 ids):
  - 0025 `Athens International Airport` → `Aeropuerto Internacional de Atenas`
  - 0062 same, inside the sentence
  - 0061 `La Piraeus Port Authority` → `La Autoridad Portuaria del Pireo`
  - 0063 `La Thessaloniki Port Authority` → `La Autoridad Portuaria de Tesalónica`
- 0047 `Union of Greek Shipowners` → `Unión de Armadores Griegos`.
- 0049 `es una práctica remunerada` → `son unas prácticas remuneradas`. Glossary term is `prácticas` (plural).
- 0049 `Viotia` → `Beocia`. Spanish exonym.
- 0048 `que necesiten griego e inglés… y disposición a trasladarse` → `que necesitan … y flexibilidad para trasladarse`. The subjunctive made the requirement read as a wish; "flexibility" was rendered as "willingness".
- 0007 `y en ninguna clasificación` → `y no en ninguna clasificación`. "Rests on … and on no ranking" read as a double negative mistake.

## Left as is on purpose
Company names (National Bank of Greece, Piraeus Bank, Pfizer's Center for Digital Innovation, Deloitte entities, Motor Oil, Titan Cement, METLEN, Aegean Airlines), programme names, the Global Financial Centres Index 40, and the street addresses.
Number formats (four digits unseparated, five or more with a point; billions → millones) are correct.

## Doubts for a native speaker
- 0048 `routes` in 0019 and 0048 → `itinerarios` (glossary `track`) rather than `vías`. Consistent within the chunk.
- 0002 `public-competition portal` → `oposiciones públicas`. `concursos públicos` may be closer to ASEP's procedures.
- 0049 `Beocia` (Viotia) may be unfamiliar; a transliterated place name would also be acceptable.
