## Remaining doubts for a native speaker

- 0219 "tarjeta de papel verde", 0221: confirm that "tarjeta" is what the source means for the green paper CUE certificate (it is a paper certificate).
- 0219 "El certificado se admite durante 3 meses": mirrors the English; the real rule is probably that the certificate must be under 3 months old.
- 0222 "En los 30 días siguientes a tu alta": the legal deadline for the TIE appointment is usually counted from entry/authorisation; mirrors the English.
- 0220 "en la Policía": the CUE may be processed at an Oficina de Extranjería in some provinces; the English says only "at the police".
