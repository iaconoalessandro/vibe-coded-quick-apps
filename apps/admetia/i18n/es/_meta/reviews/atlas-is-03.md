# Review: atlas-is-03 (Spanish)

Reviewed all 33 ids against the English. Tags, placeholders, URLs, form codes and figures (18 months, 12 months, 90/180 days, 75 %, 2.000.000 ISK, dates) were checked; no figure was changed. 6 fixes.

## Fixes
- atlas-is.0111: "Tras un título de una universidad islandesa" → "Tras obtener un título de una universidad islandesa" (the bare "Tras un título" is unnatural).
- atlas-is.0115, atlas-is.0141: "la dirección de Trabajo" → "la Dirección de Trabajo" (public body name, agreed rendering of Directorate of Labour; capital D as in atlas-is-01).
- atlas-is.0115: "Tú esperas en el extranjero" → "Esperas en el extranjero" (English has no emphatic "you"; the pronoun was an addition).
- atlas-is.0121: "que no pagan tasas" → "que no pagan tasa" (English "pay no fee", singular; matches the heading in 0118).
- atlas-is.0131: "el registro en el EEE" → "el registro de los ciudadanos del EEE" (English "EEA registration" is the registration route for EEA citizens, not a registration in the EEA).
- atlas-is.0142: "una cita presencial en el banco y activar la identificación electrónica" → "… en el banco para activar la identificación electrónica" (the appointment is to activate the ID; "y activar" misread the purpose clause).

## Translator's doubts resolved
- 0111 "converts it from inside Iceland": "lo que permite cambiar de estatus desde dentro de Islandia" kept; it renders the sense (the job-search status converts into a work permit without leaving the country) without inventing the target permit.
- 0115: now "la Dirección de Trabajo" (see above).
- 0125 "with your work contract": kept in the same position as the English, so it attaches as ambiguously as in the source (documents submitted with the A-260/A-270 forms, parallel to the students' list after A-271).

## Remaining doubts (native speaker)
- "Registers Iceland" (0125, 0126) is kept in English as the body's official English brand name (Þjóðskrá Íslands); under the "public bodies are translated" rule it could be "Registro Nacional de Islandia". Kept for consistency with "Statistics Iceland" in atlas-is-01.
- "ÚTL" (0141) left as the Icelandic acronym of the Directorate of Immigration, as in the English; a reader may not connect it to "Dirección de Inmigración" in 0128.
- 0131 "los sistemas del Estado, de la universidad y de los contratos lo usan todos" follows the English literally; "contract systems" is vague in the source.
