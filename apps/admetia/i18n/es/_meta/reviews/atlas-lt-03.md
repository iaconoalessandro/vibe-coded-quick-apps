## Fixes
- atlas-lt.0113: "omite la prueba del mercado laboral" → "queda exento de la prueba del mercado laboral". "Skips" as "omite" is a literal calque; "queda exento" is the natural legal reading.

- atlas-lt.0138: "pagar tú mismo a Sodra" → "pagar a Sodra por tu cuenta". Avoids a masculine "tú mismo" addressed to any reader (style guide: gender-neutral wording).
- atlas-lt.0142: "todavía esperas el día 91, la solicitud debe denegarse ... Solicítalo con antelación" → "sigues sin respuesta el día 91, debe denegarse ... Presenta la solicitud con antelación". "Still be waiting on day 91" was ungrammatical and lost the meaning (waiting for a decision); "Solicítalo" had no referent, and "solicitud" was repeated.

## Doubts for a native speaker
- atlas-lt.0113: "hired within ten years" is rendered literally ("en un plazo de diez años"); the source does not say ten years from graduation, so it was not made explicit.
- atlas-lt.0133: "oficina de distrito" for "ward office"; a seniūnija is a sub-municipal unit, "oficina de barrio" or "de la seniūnija" are alternatives.
- atlas-lt.0142: "estatus provisional" for "bridging status"; "estatus puente" or "situación transitoria" may be closer.
- atlas-lt.0130: "trabajo habitual" for "ordinary work" is a guess at the source meaning (regular employment).
