# Review: atlas-nz-03 (es-ES)

14 fixes. All 42 ids checked against the English; ids, order, figures, dates, percentages and NZ$ amounts otherwise correct.

## Fixes
- Official visa names (rule: keep the proper-name part, translate only the generic word), 8 ids:
  - 0111 "Fee Paying Student Visa" → "visado Fee Paying Student"
  - 0117 "Student and Trainee Work Visa" → "visado Student and Trainee Work" (also in 0118, 0128)
  - 0119 "Post-Study Work Visa" → "visado Post-Study Work"
  - 0122 "Accredited Employer Work Visa" → "visado Accredited Employer Work"
  - 0132 "Working Holiday Visa" → "visado Working Holiday"
  - 0118, 0128 "un Specific Purpose Work Visa" → "un visado Specific Purpose Work"
- atlas-nz.0110: "tasa de conversión de las prácticas en un puesto para titulados" → "tasa de ofertas de contratación tras las prácticas para pasar a un puesto de titulado". Glossary term "return offer".
- atlas-nz.0123: "comprobación del mercado laboral" → "comprobación del puesto de trabajo". "Job check" was glossed as a labour market test, which the English does not say.
- atlas-nz.0128: "la investigación para un trabajo de fin de estudios en una universidad extranjera" → "la investigación del trabajo de fin de estudios para una universidad extranjera". The thesis is for the foreign university, not carried out at one. "Trabajo de fin de estudios" kept (glossary, level not stated).
- atlas-nz.0131: "7000 a 9500 NZ$ al año" → "De 7000 a 9500 NZ$ al año". The "a" range needs "de".
- atlas-nz.0138: "certificados de antecedentes policiales" → "certificados policiales". The "de antecedentes" was an addition.
- atlas-nz.0139, 0147: "quienes se acogen a vacaciones y trabajo" → "quienes tienen un visado de vacaciones y trabajo". The old wording was unnatural.
- atlas-nz.0144: "de visado de estudiante a un Post-Study Work Visa" → "de un visado de estudiante a un visado de trabajo posterior a los estudios". The English is a lower-case generic phrase, so it is translated descriptively, as in atlas-nz-01 0031. The article was also missing.

## Left unchanged on purpose
- 0118, 0139, 0147: lower-case "working holiday" in the English stays "vacaciones y trabajo" (glossary), not the visa name.
- 0125, 0131, 0114, 0115, 0134, 0137: NZ$ amounts written "N NZ$", as in atlas-nz-01; four-digit amounts without separator, "20.000" with.
- 0141, 0146: "45 %" with the space; "23,95 NZ$" uses a decimal comma (money).
- 0150: "AEWV" kept as the source acronym.

## Doubts for a native speaker
- 0144 vs 0119: the descriptive "visado de trabajo posterior a los estudios" is not the proper name "visado Post-Study Work"; confirm that readers will link the two.
- 0112, 0151: "proveedor" for "provider" (NZQA-accredited education provider) is literal; "centro de enseñanza" may read better.
- 0115, 0134, 0137: "tasa de visitante" / "tasa" for the International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy: no established Spanish name.
- 0123: "token" kept in English for the employer's token.
