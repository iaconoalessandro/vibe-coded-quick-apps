## Remaining doubts for a native speaker

- 0154 "certificado de registro de la UE": likely the certificate of registration for EU citizens; "certificado de registro de ciudadano de la UE" may be the clearer official-sounding name.
- 0135 "sin trabajar": renders the terse "with no work"; the sense is probably "gives no right to work".
- 0148, 0153 "justificante": used for "receipt" of the application; "resguardo" is a common alternative.
- 0147 "tarjeta de número personal": literal for "personal number card"; the Bulgarian document name is not known.
