# Review: atlas-at-04 (Austria country guide)

3 fixes. All 17 ids and their order checked against the English; no figure, tag, placeholder or URL issue; register is tú / impersonal throughout.

## Fixes
- atlas-at.0195: `traducción por un traductor jurado` → `traducciones de un traductor jurado judicial` (English "translations by a sworn court translator": plural, and "court" had been dropped).
- atlas-at.0198: `nueva tarifa fija` → `nueva tasa fija` (same word as the `antigua tasa` earlier in the string).
- atlas-at.0200: `La lista de ocupaciones de difícil cobertura … se publicará` → `La lista de profesiones con escasez de personal … está prevista` (glossary: shortage occupation; "is due" is a plan, not a certainty).

- atlas-at.0195: `traductor jurado judicial` for "sworn court translator" (at-03 0177 uses `traductor inscrito en los tribunales`); the two should be harmonised if a better term is found.
