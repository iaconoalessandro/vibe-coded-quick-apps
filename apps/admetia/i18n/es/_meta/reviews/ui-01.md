# Review of i18n/es/chunks/es/ui-01.json

26 ids fixed. No changes to tags, placeholders, URLs or figures (all checked, identical to source and in order).

## "&" rule (translator's doubt confirmed)
- ui.0003 `TI & informática` → `TI e informática`
- ui.0011, ui.0014 `ciencia de datos & IA` → `ciencia de datos e IA`
- ui.0025 `Datos &amp; IA` → `Datos e IA`
- ui.0026, ui.0027 `Ciencia de datos &(amp;) IA` → `Ciencia de datos e IA`
- ui.0035, ui.0042, ui.0082 `TI &amp; informática` → `TI e informática`
Reason: style guide, `&` joining ordinary words becomes y/e.

## First person (translator's doubt)
- ui.0105 is impersonal in the English source ("The weights here are designed…"), so left as is.
- ui.0107 `son de elaboración propia` → `son míos` (English: "are mine"; first person singular kept).

## Other fixes
- ui.0017 `másteres de MBA, de negocios e informática` → `MBA y másteres de negocios e informática` (an MBA is not a "máster de MBA").
- ui.0036 `escribiendo un examen` → `haciendo un examen` (natural collocation).
- ui.0040 `Evaluados frente a` → `Puntuados frente a` (consistent with "scored" = puntuar elsewhere in the chunk).
- ui.0041, ui.0044, ui.0069 `Empieza: calculadora de …` → `Empieza con la calculadora de …` (colon form was unnatural for a button).
- ui.0043 lower-case start `ciencias…` → `Ciencias…` (string begins a sentence).
- ui.0051 `No apto` → `no apto` (mid-sentence).
- ui.0055 `una divulgación FOI` → `una respuesta a una solicitud FOI` ("divulgación" was a calque of "disclosure").
- ui.0064 `repasando apuntes y portátiles juntos` → `trabajando juntos con apuntes y portátiles` (misreading: "working through notes and laptops").
- ui.0085 `Suiza, Países Bajos` → `Suiza, los Países Bajos`.
- ui.0091 `el de CS` → `el de ciencias de la computación` (CS abbreviation not used elsewhere in the Spanish).
- ui.0095 `solicitudes FOI` → `solicitudes de acceso a la información (Freedom of Information)` (the English spells it out; unexplained FOI added); `los solicitantes de los últimos cinco años` → `cinco años de candidatos` (removed added "los últimos"; glossary: applicant = candidato).
- ui.0097 `los propios solicitantes` → `los propios candidatos` (glossary).
- ui.0100, ui.0110 `¿Cuán sólida/sólido es tu …?` → `¿Qué solidez tiene tu …?` (`cuán` is literary or Latin American; ui.0110 is concatenated with ui.0111 and ui.0112 and still reads correctly).

## Remaining doubts (native speaker)
- ui.0040 and ui.0072/0075: degree names "Master in Finance / Management / Marketing" kept in English as programme-type names; Spanish might prefer "Máster en …".
- ui.0055 / ui.0095: how FOI should be glossed for Spanish readers (here: left as FOI in 0055, spelled out as Freedom of Information in 0095).
- ui.0004 to ui.0006, ui.0008: "Probabilidades de admisión en …" and "Máster de {track}" are acceptable but unverified for all {track} values.
- ui.0113 "en línea" vs "online" for online master's.
