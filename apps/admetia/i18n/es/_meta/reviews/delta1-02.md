## Rule for programme labels
- Title-case names of a specific programme or scheme stay in English. Examples: "ECB Graduate Programme", "Graduate Program (...)", "2027 Graduate Program", "Trainee Program (...)", "Group Trainee Programme", "Global Early Talent Program", "Accelerator Program Fresh Graduates", "Analyst Program", "CPA Rotational Program".
- Lower-case or descriptive labels are translated with the glossary. Examples: "Trainee program" → programa trainee, "Banking and accounting graduate program", "Student placements", "Software and engineering internships", "Baltic summer internship".
- "Summer Internship (...)" is generic, so it was translated.
- English text in brackets is translated (durations, places, tracks).
