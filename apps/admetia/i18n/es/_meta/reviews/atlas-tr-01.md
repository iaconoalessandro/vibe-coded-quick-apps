# Review: atlas-tr-01 (es-ES)

12 ids fixed. Figures, tags, placeholders and register (tú) checked id by id; no figure was altered.

## Fixes
- atlas-tr.0036 (lira figures): "13.010,7 mil millones de TRY" → "13,0107 billones de TRY"; "4.672,8 mil millones" → "4,6728 billones"; "2.562,8 mil millones" → "2,5628 billones" (style guide: a thousand billion or more becomes billones, same value). Also removed the added "(PIB)" gloss, which the English does not have.
- atlas-tr.0009, 0039: "Aegean Free Zone" → "Zona Franca del Egeo" (Turkey is not English-official; English names of public bodies are translated).
- atlas-tr.0053, 0054: "National Internship Program" → "Programa Nacional de Prácticas".
- atlas-tr.0013, 0026: "Istanbul Finance Centre" → "Centro Financiero de Estambul" (English translation of a Turkish name; 0004 and 0026 already said "centro financiero").
- atlas-tr.0010: "posición" → "relevancia" (glossary: standing).
- atlas-tr.0001, 0050: "jornadas de empleo en línea" → "Career Days en línea" (named event of Kariyer.net keeps its name).
- atlas-tr.0043: "los grandes empleadores la emplean" → "los grandes empleadores que hemos encontrado contratan así" (omitted "found"); "de las 208 universidades" → "de la totalidad de las 208 universidades" (omitted "all").
- atlas-tr.0044: "titulados del último año de estudios" → "titulados del último año" (the added "de estudios" suggested final-year students rather than graduates of the last year).

## Doubts for a native speaker
- Izmir as "Esmirna" (consistent in the chunk; "Izmir" is also used in Spanish).
- atlas-tr.0057 "titulados de ciclo corto" for associate-degree holders (Turkish önlisans); "técnicos superiores" would be the Spanish-system analogue but is not equivalent.
- atlas-tr.0055 "Experience Programme" (Koç) left in English as a programme name; may be a translation of a Turkish name and so need "Programa Experience".
- atlas-tr.0052 "28.ª feria de empleo de Bilkent" kept generic (glossary feria de empleo); if "Career Fair" is treated as the event's proper name it would be capitalised, like Career Days.
- atlas-tr.0054 "patronazgo" (Presidency's patronage) is acceptable but "patrocinio" is possible.
