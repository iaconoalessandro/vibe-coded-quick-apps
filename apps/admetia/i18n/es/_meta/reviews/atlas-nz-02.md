# Review: atlas-nz-02 (es-ES)

All 45 ids checked against the English. Ids, order, figures, dates, percentages and NZ$ amounts all match; JSON valid. 21 ids changed (the visa-name fix touches 11 of them).

## Fixes
- Visa names (0071, 0076, 0085, 0086, 0087, 0092, 0094, 0095, 0096, 0104, 0107): the lower-case English "post-study work visa", "post-study visa" and "accredited employer work visa" had been left in English inside Spanish sentences. nz-01 (0031) translates the lower-case descriptive form, so: "post-study work visa" → "visado de trabajo posterior a los estudios"; "post-study visa" → "visado posterior a los estudios"; "accredited employer work visa" → "visado de trabajo con empleador acreditado". 0107 also reworded to avoid "visado de trabajo, un visado de trabajo…".
- 0069, 0099: "Universidad de Auckland" → "University of Auckland" (university names as written, glossary). In 0069 the expo names "Negocios y Derecho / Ingeniería y Tecnología / Prácticas y Titulados" → "Business and Law / Engineering and Technology / Internship and Graduate" (named events keep their name; 0099 also changed, for consistency).
- 0065, 0090: "cuatro destinos de seis meses" → "cuatro rotaciones de seis meses" (placements = rotaciones, as nz-01 0057).
- 0070, 0102: Suncorp "streams": "líneas" → "itinerarios" (as nz-01 0048).
- 0073: "universidades prioritarias" → "universidades objetivo" (glossary: target school).
- 0074: "itinerarios de tipo aprendizaje" → "vías de tipo aprendizaje" (pathways = vías, as in the same sentence; "itinerario" is the glossary term for track).
- 0082: "muestran una banda salarial en lugar de una cifra" → "…en lugar de pedir una cifra". The English says the adverts show a band instead of asking for a figure; the old text dropped "asking for".
- 0085: "diplomas de posgrado de nivel 7" → "Graduate Diploma de nivel 7". A NZ Graduate Diploma is a level 7 qualification, not a postgraduate one (the English separates "graduate diplomas" from the bachelor's); kept as the qualification name.
- 0094: "durante toda la duración del puesto" → "mientras dure el puesto" (redundant).
- 0098: "ofrece puestos junior de tecnología" → "se centra en puestos junior de tecnología" (Summer of Tech "serves" these roles; it does not offer them).

## Doubts for a native speaker
- Official name: INZ calls it "Post Study Work Visa" and "Accredited Employer Work Visa"; the English here is lower case so I translated, but if the site wants the official names kept they should be "visado Post Study Work" / "visado Accredited Employer Work" throughout.
- 0092, 0104: "trial period" vs "probationary period" both map to Spanish "periodo de prueba"; I kept "cláusula de prueba" / "prueba de 90 días" for the trial period to keep them apart.
- 0070: "Deloitte open by October" rendered "abre su convocatoria a más tardar en octubre"; the English may mean "already open in October".
- 0083: "referees" (persons) rendered "referencias"; "personas de referencia" would be more exact but longer.
