## Doubts for a native speaker
- atlas-at.0141: `unión de estudiantes` for the Austrian student-union fee (ÖH-Beitrag); a more idiomatic term may exist.
- atlas-at.0132/0180: `matrícula` for "enrolment" collides with the glossary use of `matrícula` for tuition; `medios económicos` (0132, 0180) vs `fondos` (labels) kept as two registers of the same idea.
- atlas-at.0124: `reclutadores` (glossary prefers "selección de personal"); acceptable but slightly Latin American.
- atlas-at.0177: `traductor inscrito en los tribunales` for "court-registered translator" is understandable but could be tightened.
- atlas-at.0183: `comisaría` for "police office" (Austrian Polizeiinspektion).
