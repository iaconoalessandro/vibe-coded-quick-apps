# Review: atlas-lu-03 (Luxembourg guide), es-ES

6 fixes. No tags, placeholders or figures were altered; every figure, date and amount checked against the English.

## Fixes
- atlas-lu.0134: "corre el riesgo de una inscripción falsa" → "implica el riesgo de una inscripción falsa". Unnatural collocation.
- atlas-lu.0136: "tu ID" → "tu documento de identidad". It means an identity document, not a product name.
- atlas-lu.0139: "médico generalista" → "médico de cabecera"; "unos 56 € a 65 €" → "de unos 56 € a 65 €"; "radiografía de tórax gratuita contra la tuberculosis" → "… para descartar la tuberculosis". An X-ray screens for TB; it is not "against" it.
- atlas-lu.0143, 0149: "al CNS" → "a la CNS" (Caisse nationale de santé, feminine). "(CNS)" after "tarjeta sanitaria" unchanged.
- atlas-lu.0146: "prevé entre 6.000 € y 7.000 € en efectivo" → "prevé tener disponibles entre 6.000 € y 7.000 € …". "Cash" means funds on hand, not banknotes.

## Translator's doubts, resolved
- atlas-lu.0125 "Un doctorando": kept. The English "a PhD" means a doctoral candidate employed or funded; "doctorado" (the glossary term) would be the degree and misreads here. Generic masculine is correct per the guide.
- "ID" appears only in 0136 and is now "documento de identidad"; no product or scheme name needed it.
- "sans domiciliation" kept in French in straight-quote style as in the English.

## Remaining doubts (native speaker)
- "la CNS" vs "el CNS": feminine follows the French institution name; confirm site-wide usage.
- 0135/0136 "autorización de residencia temporal", "certificado de alojamiento del propietario": rendering of local official documents; confirm wording.
- 0141 "centro de la Seguridad Social (CCSS)": acceptable rendering of Centre commun de la sécurité sociale.
