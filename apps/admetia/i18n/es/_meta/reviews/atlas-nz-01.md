# Review: atlas-nz-01 (es-ES)

7 fixes. All 64 ids checked against the English; ids, order, figures, dates, currency formats and glossary terms otherwise correct.

## Fixes
- atlas-nz.0013: "frente a 40.900" → "por encima de las 40.900 anteriores". The English says "up from", and "frente a" lost the rise.
- atlas-nz.0029: "y los datos oficiales…" → "y porque los datos oficiales…". The second reason was left outside the "porque" it depends on.
- atlas-nz.0030: "La cuota de la Public Service en Wellington (42,6 %)" → "El peso de Wellington en la Public Service (42,6 %)". The old wording said the Public Service is 42.6 % of Wellington; the source (0027, 0036) says 42.6 % of the Public Service workforce is in Wellington. Also "criterio de interpretación" → "valoración" and "se ha leído" → "se ha interpretado".
- atlas-nz.0031: "el Accredited Employer Work Visa" → "un visado de trabajo con empleador acreditado". The English is a lower-case generic description, so it is translated descriptively; the old form mixed the English name with a masculine article.
- atlas-nz.0040: word order fixed so "algunos o todos sus puestos" follows the verb, which makes the sentence read naturally.
- atlas-nz.0044: "portal oficial de orientación profesional del Gobierno" → "portal de carreras del Gobierno". The old wording added "oficial" and "orientación profesional".
- atlas-nz.0063: "publicó 20 a 25 puestos" → "anunció de 20 a 25 puestos", consistent with 0047 ("advertised").

## Left unchanged on purpose
- atlas-nz.0023, 0028: bare "$" rendered as "dólares" (not "neozelandeses"), per the brief; "2000 millones" etc. follow the four-digit rule.
- Public Service kept in English where capitalised (0027, 0030, 0036, 0055, 0056); "función pública" where lower case (0056).
- atlas-nz.0011, 0046, 0047, 0063: NZ$ amounts are written "N NZ$", following the guide's currency pattern.

## Doubts for a native speaker
- 0030: the English "Public Service share of Wellington" is ambiguous; I read it as Wellington's share of the Public Service workforce, from 0027 and 0036.
- 0031: "entrevista en vídeo unidireccional" for "one-way video interview" is literal; "en diferido" or "grabada" may be more usual.
- 0057: "rotaciones" for "placements" (graduate rotations) and "itinerarios" for Suncorp "streams" (0048) are acceptable but unconfirmed.
