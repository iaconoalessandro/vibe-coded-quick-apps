All 34 ids checked against the English; figures, country list, tag-free strings, JSON validity and id order verified. 11 edits in 11 strings.

## Fixes

- Generic "pass" → "pase" (rule: lower-case generic pass): 0116 "ningún permiso" → "ningún pase"; 0118 "un permiso de un año" → "un pase de un año"; 0136 "solicitar el permiso" → "solicitar el pase"; 0141 "Si te cancelan el permiso" → "Si te cancelan el pase"; 0144 "un permiso" → "un pase"; 0145 "un permiso propio" → "un pase propio". "permisos de trabajo ordinarios" (0120) kept: English says "work permits".
- 0121, 0123 "Salario mínimo exigido" → "Mínimo salarial": "salario mínimo" reads as the legal minimum wage (SMI), and the label was about 80 % longer than "Salary floor". Matches "mínimos" in 0124 and "un mínimo que depende de la edad" in 0119.
- 0119 "El salario debe … obtener al menos 40 puntos" → "… la solicitud debe sumar al menos 40 puntos": the salary does not score points, the application does.
- 0126 "tesis doctoral" → "trabajo de fin de estudios": English "thesis" has no level; glossary rule for unstated level.
- 0142 "bonificación" → "prima": a pay bonus, not a COMPASS bonus (sg-02 reserves "bonificaciones" for COMPASS points).

- 0126: "non-graduating Student’s Pass" (a pass for students not taking a degree): "no conducente a título" is a descriptive rendering; check it is clear.
