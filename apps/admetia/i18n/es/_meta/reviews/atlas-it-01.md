# Review: atlas-it-01 (es-ES)

All 103 ids compared with the source. Every figure, percentage, currency amount, date and rank was checked and none had changed. Number formats follow the guide (five digits or more take a point, four digits take none, decimal comma). No HTML tags or placeholders are in this chunk. The JSON is valid, with the same ids in the same order. 16 fixes.

## Fixes

Repeated fix (7 ids: 0090, 0092, 0093, 0094, 0095, 0096, 0097)
- "La producción económica (PIB) de la región fue…" → "El PIB de la región fue…". The English says only "GDP", so the gloss "producción económica" was an addition.

Single fixes
- 0001: "un inicio tentativo" → "un inicio provisional previsto". "Tentativo" is a calque.
- 0003: "Casi en todas partes se espera el italiano, salvo…" → "Se espera italiano en casi todas partes, salvo…". Unnatural word order.
- 0015: "Milán frente al resto y por qué…" → "Milán frente al resto, y por qué…". Comma restored to match the English.
- 0041: "Sedes energéticas" → "sedes energéticas". The English is lower case, as part of the group label in 0039.
- 0066: "con moda, marroquinería e industria farmacéutica" → "por la moda, la marroquinería y la industria farmacéutica". The English "for fashion…" gives the reason Florence is the hub, not what it has.
- 0083: "…ofertas de empleo en Italia indica que" → "indican que". Agreement with the plural noun.
- 0100, 0101: "Eurostat contabiliza en 2021 los empleos en … en 147.200 en la región…" → "Eurostat contabiliza en 2021 un total de 147.200 empleos en … en la región…", and "en Italia en conjunto" → "en el conjunto de Italia". The old text had a clumsy chain of "en … en … en". Figures and order unchanged.
- 0103: "por debajo del 45.º de la edición anterior" → "tras caer desde el 45.º de la edición anterior". The old wording read as a comparison of positions, not a fall in rank.

## Checked and left unchanged on purpose
- 0014: "frente a … frente a" for "vs … vs". It is correct but a little heavy in a short label. "vs" would also be acceptable.
- 0017: "opa de MPS" for "MPS takeover". Acceptable; the actual instrument may have been an exchange offer (OPS).
- 0013: straight quotes around "presente", as in the English.
- 0102: ranks (2nd/3rd, 4th/5th, 23rd/10th) checked against the source and kept.

## Doubts for a native speaker
- 0085: "un empleado soltero" for "a single employee". The English probably means one employee with no dependants, not an unmarried one. It may need rewording once the source is clarified.
- 0086: "código tributario refundido" for "consolidated tax code". "Texto refundido" may be closer to the Spanish legal register. The "art. 225" reference is kept as is.
- 0014 and 0017, as above.
