20 fixes in 17 ids. Ids, order and figures checked against the English; all figures, dates and money conversions were correct (546.400 millones, 592.800 millones, 1,003 billones, 1 billón, 1000 with no separator).

## Fixes
- Public bodies kept in English (US is English-official): 0001, 0009, 0010 "Oficina del Censo" → "Census Bureau"; 0010 "Departamento de Vivienda y Desarrollo Urbano" → "Housing and Urban Development" (and "alquileres de mercado justos" → "fair market rent"); 0062-0064 "Comisión de Bolsa y Valores de EE. UU. (SEC)" → "Securities and Exchange Commission de EE. UU." (the "(SEC)" gloss was an addition).
- "standing" → "relevancia": 0013 "posición" → "relevancia"; 0065 "su posición en software" → "su relevancia en software".
- 0003 "convocatorias" (glossary: intake) → "vacantes ... se publicaron" (source: postings).
- 0008 "logísticos" → "especialistas en logística"; "estas cifras" → "estas valoraciones" (they are the ratings); "medidas del mercado".
- 0009 reworded the county-sum clause to follow the English order.
- 0010 "Los salarios son la media anual de todos los trabajadores" → "El sueldo es el salario medio anual de todos los trabajadores" (kept "mean annual wage").
- 0014 "derechos de trabajo" → "derecho a trabajar" (work rights).
- 0016 removed added "en ofertas" (gloss not in the English).
- 0053 "frente a unos 375.800" → "tras caer desde unos 375.800" (the English says "down from"); "en mayo" → "a mayo" for "by May".
- 0062-0064 "el Form 10-K" appositive → "(Form 10-K)" so the report is the one filed, as in the English.
- 0075 "la IA acaparó" → "con la IA concentrando" (neutral).
- 0089 "casi el doble" → "aproximadamente el doble" ("about twice", not "almost").
- 0098 "a los seis meses" → "en un plazo de seis meses" ("within six months").
- 0102 ambiguous "empatada" → "igual que Chicago".
- 0001 "se desempeñan" → "se realizan" (more natural in Spain).

- Kept in English: OPT, H-1B, F-1, J-1, L-1, E-2, DHS, USCIS, BEA, BLS (la BEA, la BLS), Census Bureau (el), Housing and Urban Development, Securities and Exchange Commission, Form 10-K, Fortune 500/Fortune 10, Greater Houston Partnership, Startup Genome, Global Financial Centres Index, venture capital, trainee, Big Tech, Big Four, MMS.
