- models.0393: "Recién titulados con prácticas de hasta unos 3 años" → "Recién titulados con entre unas prácticas y unos 3 años de experiencia". The original read as "internships of up to 3 years"; the English means experience ranging from an internship to about 3 years.
- models.0396: "no presentarlo no afecta" → "no presentar ninguno no afecta". The referent was GRE and GMAT (two tests), so the singular pronoun was ambiguous.
- models.0404: "tres cuartas partes de la promoción tiene" → "tienen". Agreement with the plural subject.
- models.0436: "Primera o 2:1" → "First o 2:1". The glossary keeps UK degree classes unchanged.
- models.0456: "no a esta" → "no aquí". "esta" had no clear referent.
- models.0481: "La prueba se exime con…" → "Se exime de la prueba con…". "Eximir" takes the person or the requirement as its object, not the test as subject.
