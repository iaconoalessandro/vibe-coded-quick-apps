# Review: atlas-at-01 (Austria country guide)

7 fixes. All ids/order/placeholders/figures checked against the English; no figure was wrong.

## Fixes
- atlas-at.0014: `Red-White-Red Card` → `tarjeta Rojo-Blanco-Rojo` (current style-guide rule: English-named permit outside English-speaking countries is translated).
- atlas-at.0016: `§1 Empleo de…` → `§1 empleo de…` (English is lower case after §1; sentence case).
- atlas-at.0001: `avisa de las vacantes que abre hasta finales de mayo` → `comunica las vacantes a más tardar a finales de mayo` ("by the end of May", not "until").
- atlas-at.0045: `en el 2.º semestre de 2023–2025` → `entre el 2.º semestre de 2023 y 2025` (the original read as "2nd half of each year").
- atlas-at.0048, 0050, 0052: `(región NUTS 3 AT312/AT221/AT323…)` → `(NUTS 3 AT…)` (added word "región" duplicated the preceding "La región de…"; English has none).

## Checked, left unchanged
Figures, "billion" → `mil millones` renderings (`13.000 millones de dólares`, `2000 millones`, `1830 millones`), 4-digit numbers without separator, percentages with `%` spaced, dates, ordinals, glossary terms (sin valorar, familia de puestos, polo, sede), tú register (none needed), straight quotes around "alle jobs" / "cuando sea necesario" kept as in the English.

## Doubts for a native speaker
- atlas-at.0045: `salidas` for VC "exits" (alternatives: `desinversiones`, `exits`); "H2 2023–2025" is ambiguous in the English itself.
- atlas-at.0001: "gives notice of openings by the end of May" is ambiguous in the English (who is notified); rendered literally.
- atlas-at.0012: `impuesto sobre nóminas` for Austria's wage-tax payroll data.
- atlas-at.0066: English sequence (October 2026 fairs, then TUday26 on 7 May 2026 "follows") is odd; kept faithfully.
