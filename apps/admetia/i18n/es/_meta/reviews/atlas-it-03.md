## Fixes

Repeated fix (3 ids: 0177, 0192, 0208)
- "stage" as an invariable plural ("Los stage", "La selección de stage", "Normas sobre los stage") → "stages", as 0213 already had. Consistency within the chunk. In 0177 also "La selección de stage" → "La contratación de stages" (glossary: hiring = contratación).

Single fixes
- 0185: "de más reciente a más antigua, enviadas en PDF" → "de la más reciente a la más antigua, y el CV se envía en PDF". The participle "enviadas" agreed with studies and experience, but the English "sent as a PDF" refers to the CV.
- 0189: "el convenio colectivo aplicable" → "el convenio colectivo". "Applicable" was an addition (it is in the English only in 0202).
- 0196: "con un inicio provisional desde julio de 2026" → "con un inicio provisional previsto desde julio de 2026". "Tentatively" needs "previsto" (same convention as atlas-it-01 id 0001).
- 0201: "Un stage es un régimen aparte" → "Las prácticas son un régimen aparte". The English says "An internship" (glossary: prácticas); "stage" was a substitution.
- 0205: "Lo que un empleador quiere saber" → "quiere oír". The English is "wants to hear".
- 0209: "Si buscas un stage después de dejar de ser estudiante:" → "Buscar un stage después de dejar de ser estudiante:". This is one of the "mistake" items (0209–0212) that open with a gerund; the others were already infinitives, so the "Si buscas" conditional broke the series.
- 0212: "con fecha y firmada" → "fechada y firmada". Agreement and idiom ("authorisation, dated and signed").

## Doubts for a native speaker
- 0201 and 0192/0197: the source alternates "stage" and "internship" for what looks like the same thing. Here "internship" became "prácticas" and "stage" stays Italian; a native check on whether this reads as two different arrangements is worthwhile.
- 0203: "oficina de empleo" for "job centre" (glossary: public employment agency); the actual Italian body is the Centro per l'impiego.
