Why this one is built differently. The business calculators lean on admitted class profiles. Computing programmes do not publish those — but they do publish hard entry rules, and in the UK their real acceptance rates are obtainable under Freedom of Information. So this track leads with the rules and says plainly where every number came from. Where five years of applicants have reported their own outcomes, that is shown too, labelled as what it is: a self-selected sample, never an acceptance rate.
