Find an internship or job

Getting hired works differently in every country and at every stage. Start from where you are now to see the route most people take, when it opens and what an application needs. The other stages stay one step away, because people move between them.

These are starting points, not profiles. Nothing is stored when you follow one, and every page lets you change the stage.

When does recruiting open?

Fifteen recruiting windows across banking, consulting, the Big 4, consumer goods and tech, in London, Paris, Frankfurt and Munich, Milan, Zurich and the United States. Many open a year or more before the job starts, which is why the first weeks of a master’s matter.

Open the full recruiting calendar: filters by sector, country and year of study, the month-by-month tables, decision rules, myths and sources.
