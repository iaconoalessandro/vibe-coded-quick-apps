Where are you trying to get to?

Admetia is a free, independent reference for four decisions: which master’s to apply to, which career it leads to, how to get hired, and how to move country for it. Start from the question you have. Nothing here asks you to sign up or choose a profile first, and you can move between the parts at any time.

    Not sure what you are looking for yet?

    You do not need to know the names of degrees or jobs to begin. Three ways to look around, none of them a commitment:

    • Take the Career Compass

      Answer 8 to 17 questions about what you studied, what you would enjoy doing and how you want to work. It ranks the role families against your answers and shows why. Optional, and you can skip it and browse instead.

    • Start from what you studied

      Pick your degree subject and see which role families are a strong fit, a possible fit or a stretch, with the reason for each.

    • Start from a place

      Open a country to see what its cities hire for, then follow the links to how hiring works there and which roles are involved.

    What this is, and what it is not

    An independent, unofficial reference built for personal use. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any university, employer or government. Nothing on it is admissions, careers, legal or immigration advice: every figure is dated and sourced so that you can check it, and the pages say plainly where something could not be verified. How the information was gathered and checked.

    ColophonRuns in your browser. No accounts, no cookies and no lead capture; visits are counted anonymously — which page, never what you type.

    Your answersStored only in this browser, and only so a refresh does not lose them.

    SourcesEvery figure is tagged by where it came from, or marked as an estimate when no school publishes it.
