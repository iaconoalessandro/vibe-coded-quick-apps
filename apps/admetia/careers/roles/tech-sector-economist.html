What you actually do

Design and test pricing, incentives and marketplace rules; estimate demand with structural models (models derived from economic theory) and causal effects from experiments (A/B tests) and observational data. Amazon listings describe causal inference for product selection, cost-to-serve and delivery speed, structural demand models for Prime Video investments, incrementality measurement for ads and labour-market analysis (Amazon.jobs, 2026). Uber's economists design pricing, incentives and subscription structures, run marketplace experiments and ML-driven optimisation; its policy-economics applied scientist role covers driver and courier policy (Uber postings, 2024-26). Airbnb's senior policy economists support regulation, housing, tourism and tax analysis (Airbnb posting, 2026). You write code in Python/R/SQL and present to product managers.

A typical day and week

Illustrative composite. Review overnight experiment results; refine an estimator; two meetings with product managers; write a one-page memo with a recommendation; weekly team read-out with engineers and data scientists.

Hours, stress and lifestyle

Typical 40-50 hours, peaks 55-60 around launches and quarterly planning (author judgement). Stress 3/5. Flexible, usually hybrid; limited travel. Layoff and reorganisation risk is real in tech (general observation, not sourced).

Human vs. quantitative profile

People/communication: 4/5. Quantitative/technical: 5/5. You must sell a model to product managers and engineers, and coding is expected.

Compensation (approximate)

US (Amazon, Levels.fyi, updated 9 October 2026, 16 submissions): L4 median total compensation about USD 211,921; L5 about USD 295,812; overall median pay mix USD 147,000 base, USD 56,000 stock, USD 44,750 bonus. A snapshot of 11 August 2026 gave L4 USD 218,600, L5 USD 295,800 (the two vary, small sample). Uber: policy-economics applied scientist base USD 131,000-138,000 in San Francisco (Uber posting, undated). Airbnb: Senior Policy Economist USD 179,000-210,000; staff-level USD 194,000-240,000 (aggregator listings, approximate). INOMICS found the PhD premium among economists was 96% in 2023 (and about 50% in 2022) (INOMICS Salary Report). UK/Europe: no reliable data found. Asia: no reliable data found.

Career path

Economist/Scientist (PhD entry, L4) -> Senior Economist (L5, about 3-5 years) -> Principal/Tech Lead (L6, 8-12 years) -> Director/Chief Economist. Levels vary by company.

Exit opportunities

After 2-3 years: other tech, consulting, central banks, tenure-track academia. After 7-10 years: chief economist, product/strategy leadership, startup founding, antitrust expert roles.

Tier list of employers

Tier 1: Amazon (the largest economist team), Uber, Airbnb, Microsoft, Meta, Google, Netflix - perception based on size of economist teams and publication culture. Tier 2: Lyft, Spotify, eBay, Etsy, Booking, Zalando, fintechs. Tier 3: startups with one economist. Tier groupings reflect industry consensus and practitioner perception, not objective fact.

How to enter

Degrees: PhD (economics or similar) is the main route; Amazon lists a PhD in economics or equivalent as basic qualification for its marketing economists; Uber's marketing applied scientist role accepts PhD, MS or bachelor's with years of industry experience; Airbnb counts a PhD as roughly two years' experience for its senior role. Calendar: PhD candidates apply through the AEA job market (Uber's "AEA/ASSA economists" postings), fall-winter, with interviews in January; experienced hires apply all year. Interview formats: job-talk-style presentation of your research, coding screen (SQL/Python), causal-inference and experimentation questions, behavioural interviews. Skills: causal inference, experimentation, structural estimation, Python/SQL, ability to communicate to non-economists. Common mistakes: pure-theory PhD with no applied data skills; weak coding. Alternative routes: master's plus industry data science, then move into economist track (rare and role-dependent), pre-doc plus PhD. Entry difficulty: 5/5 (PhD from a strong programme practically required for the best seats).

Honest downsides and who it is NOT a good fit for

Many tech economists are used as senior data scientists; impact depends on product priorities; publishing is constrained. Compensation depends heavily on stock, which moves with the share price. Not a good fit if you want policy influence or academic freedom.

A reality check on pay, hours and tiers

Every figure here is dated and approximate, copied from the research with its source and year. Pay is in local currency and never converted. Tier lists reflect industry consensus and practitioner perception, not objective fact. Three limits apply to almost every page:

  • Asia and Gulf pay across almost all branches rests on aggregators, small self-reported samples (Levels.fyi has 12-40 submissions in some cities) or recruiter blogs. Tokyo is the thinnest.
  • Hours and stress are researchers' estimates from practitioner accounts, prep sites and a few surveys (mainly M&A). No report found a systematic hours survey by role family.
  • Gated recruiter guides (Hays, Robert Walters, Michael Page tables outside Italy, Management Consulted, CIPD, PMI) could not be read. Many pay figures therefore come via secondary summaries, as the reports note.

The full list of gaps, the rating scales and the researchers’ assumptions are on the sources page.
