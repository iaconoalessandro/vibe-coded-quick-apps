How the scoring works

Hard rules come first

ECTS prerequisites, experience caps, test floors. Fail one and the school is ineligible — a 780 GMAT doesn't buy back a missing statistics module.

Every school weighs it differently

Bocconi: grades and the test are nearly the whole ranking. HEC and IE read essays and interview you. Each is scored its own way.

Every number is sourced

Tagged by origin — school page, school document, aggregator, FOI — and flagged when a school publishes nothing.
