# Landscape map

## Who this library is for (assumption)

The repo shows the audience. The site is bilingual (EN/IT) and converts the Italian 30-point grade scale. Its "national employers" list is Italian-heavy (Eni, Enel, UniCredit, Intesa, Mediobanca, Barilla). Of the ~70 master's programmes it scores, most are in Europe (UK 15, France 11+, Spain 6, Italy 4, Germany 4, Switzerland 3, NL 3, Nordics 4, Austria 2, Portugal 2), with 8 in the US. **Working assumption:** the reader is a 20–25-year-old European (often Italian) bachelor's student or recent graduate. They are choosing a pre-experience master's in Management, Finance or Marketing, may later consider an MBA, and may or may not need a visa, depending on whether they hold EU citizenship and whether the target is the UK, Switzerland or the US. Where the answer differs for non-EU readers, the files say so.

## Early signals from the broad sweep (what shaped the ratings)

- The entry-level market is tighter than the one most advisors formed their views in. The UK's ISE recorded a mean of **140 applications per graduate vacancy**, the highest since records began in 1991; FMCG/retail hit ~290 per role (ISE Student Recruitment Survey 2025, https://ise.org.uk/_userfiles/pages/files/reports/student_recruitment_survey_2025.pdf) [data].
- The French grande-école net employment rate at six months fell from a record 90.5% (2023) to about 80% (2025) and 76% (2026); a secondary source gives 85.8% for 2024. Primary: CGE Enquête Insertion 2026 PDF; the 2023 figure was corrected from 91% on 2026-10-01 (CGE insertion surveys, as reported by diplomeo.com and lexpress-education.com, 2025–26) [data].
- UK Big-4 graduate intakes were cut 6–33% by firm (reported by scottishfinancialnews.com / thefinancestory.com, 2025) [data, secondary]. Top consulting starting pay was frozen for a third year (2026 reporting), while McKinsey announced a 12% increase in North American entry hiring for 2026 (peoplematters.in, storyboard18.com) [employer-stated, secondary].
- Stanford Digital Economy Lab ("Canaries in the Coal Mine", Brynjolfsson, Chandar, Chen, Aug 2025): employment of 22–25-year-olds in the most AI-exposed occupations fell ~13% relative to others, while older workers in the same jobs held steady [data, US payroll].
- UK Graduate Route shrinks from 24 to 18 months for applications from 1 Jan 2027 (Statement of Changes, 14 Oct 2025; reported by ICEF Monitor, BUILA) [data].
- Goldman Sachs' summer intern intake was ~45% STEM majors, up from 37% four years earlier (eFinancialCareers, 2021 reporting on Goldman data) [employer-stated, secondary — old; needs refresh].
- GMAC Prospective Students Survey 2025: interest in specialised business master's rose, ROI is the most-cited decision factor, and interest in tech careers dipped (gmac.com, 2025) [data].

## Rating method

- **Impact (I)**: how much the topic can change the student's decision or outcome (1 = nice to know, 5 = can flip the choice of school, country or career).
- **Scarcity (S)**: how hard decision-grade information on it is to find in one place (1 = on every school page, 5 = scattered, contradictory, or known only to insiders).
- **Value = I × S** (max 25). ≥16 = dedicated file or major section; 9–15 = folded into a broader file; ≤8 = listed only (or handled by the calculator).
