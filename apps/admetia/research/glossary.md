# Glossary

## Degrees and programmes
- **Pre-experience master's**: a master's for people with little or no full-time work experience (typically 0–2 years). MiM, MiF and MSc Marketing are pre-experience. The MBA is post-experience.
- **MiM (Master in Management)**: a generalist business master's. In France it is called the *Programme Grande École* (PGE).
- **MiF / MFin / MSc Finance**: a finance master's. LBS calls its version the **MFA** (Masters in Financial Analysis).
- **MFE (Master of Financial Engineering)**: a quantitative finance master's for maths, engineering or physics graduates.
- **MAM / MBAn (Business Analytics)**: a data-focused business master's.
- **MBA**: Master of Business Administration, usually after 3–6 years of work.
- **Deferred MBA**: admission to an MBA while still a student, starting after 2–5 years of work (e.g. HBS 2+2, Stanford, Wharton Moelis).
- **CEMS MIM**: a one-year add-on Master in International Management, taken through a member school alongside its own master's.
- **Double degree**: two degrees from two schools, usually with an extra semester or year.
- **Gap year (année de césure)**: a year of internships taken between the two years of a continental master's.
- **Laurea triennale / laurea magistrale (LM)**: the Italian three-year bachelor's and two-year master's. **LM-77** is management, **LM-56** economics, **LM-16** finance.
- **Master universitario di I/II livello**: an Italian post-degree course. It is *not* a degree (a laurea magistrale or MSc) under Italian law.
- **ECTS / CFU**: European credit units. 60 per full-time year. CFU is the Italian name.
- **GMAT Focus Edition**: the only current GMAT, scored 205–805 (since 31 January 2024). Older "700" scores are on the retired 200–800 scale and are not equal to a Focus 700; see getting-in/admissions.md for the concordance.
- **TAGE MAGE**: the French management aptitude test (90 questions, scored 0–600), accepted by HEC and ESSEC next to the GMAT and GRE; not compulsory.
- **IELTS / TOEFL**: English-language tests. Schools set minimum overall and per-component scores, and waiver rules differ (LSE does not waive for a degree taught in English outside an English-speaking country).
- **Rolling admissions**: the school reviews applications as they arrive and fills places until the class is full, so later rounds have fewer seats and less scholarship money.
- **Bologna process**: the European 3+2 degree structure (bachelor's + master's).
- **Grade de master / visa (France)**: state recognition levels for French diplomas. Only the *grade* equals a state master's degree.

## Schools and rankings
- **FT ranking**: Financial Times rankings of MiM, MiF and MBA programmes. They are based partly on alumni surveys three years after graduation.
- **PPP (purchasing power parity)**: an adjustment for local price levels. It makes salaries in cheap countries look higher and Swiss salaries look lower than in cash terms.
- **Weighted salary**: the FT's alumni salary three years after graduation, adjusted for PPP and for sector mix. It is not a starting salary.
- **Coverage / response rate**: the share of a class that reported employment data. Low coverage makes "100% employed" meaningless.
- **AACSB / EQUIS / AMBA**: business-school accreditation bodies. Holding all three is called the "triple crown".
- **Target / semi-target / non-target school**: how much a school is actively recruited from by investment banks and consulting firms.
- **Grande école**: a selective French higher-education school. Business ones include HEC, ESSEC, ESCP, EDHEC and emlyon.
- **HAW / FH / Hochschule**: German or Swiss universities of applied sciences. **DHBW** is the Baden-Württemberg Cooperative State University (dual study).
- **Duales Studium (dual study)**: a German degree combined with paid work at a sponsoring company.

## Employers and careers
- **IB (investment banking)**: advising on mergers (**M&A**) and raising capital (**ECM** for equity, **DCM** for debt, **lev fin** for leveraged finance).
- **BB (bulge bracket)**: the largest global banks (Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley and others).
- **EB (elite boutique)**: smaller advisory-only banks (Evercore, Lazard, Rothschild, Centerview, PJT and others).
- **MM (middle market)**: banks focused on mid-sized deals.
- **S&T**: sales and trading. **AM**: asset management. **PE**: private equity. **HF**: hedge fund. **VC**: venture capital.
- **Quant / strats / e-trading**: maths- and programming-heavy finance roles.
- **On-cycle (PE)**: the US practice of PE firms hiring first-year bankers within months of their start.
- **Off-cycle internship**: an internship outside the June–August summer window, often 3–6 months. It is common in London and Paris.
- **Summer analyst / superday**: a banking summer intern; a superday is the final day of back-to-back interviews.
- **Spring week / insight programme**: short employer programmes for first- and second-year undergraduates in the UK. They are mostly closed to master's students.
- **MBB**: McKinsey, BCG and Bain. **Tier 2**: other strategy firms (Roland Berger, Kearney, Oliver Wyman, L.E.K., Strategy&).
- **Big 4**: Deloitte, PwC, EY and KPMG. Their **TS** (transaction services) and deals teams are distinct from audit.
- **Case interview**: a consulting interview where you solve a business problem live.
- **FMCG / CPG**: fast-moving consumer goods / consumer packaged goods (P&G, Unilever, L'Oréal, Nestlé and others).
- **ABM / BM**: Assistant Brand Manager / Brand Manager.
- **Graduate scheme / rotational programme**: a structured entry programme with rotations across functions or countries (e.g. Unilever UFLP, Siemens SGP).
- **Coffee chat / cold outreach**: a short informal call with someone at a firm, usually arranged by a message sent without prior contact (cold outreach). It is how many candidates get information and sometimes a referral.
- **Internal referral**: a current employee puts your application forward. It is the biggest lever candidates control (getting-in/breaking-in.md).
- **ATS (applicant tracking system)**: the software employers use to collect, parse, search and filter applications. Automatic rejections usually come from knockout questions (work authorisation, sponsorship, minimum experience), not from a hidden CV score (getting-in/applications-and-interviews.md).
- **Knockout question**: a yes/no or numeric application question that ends the application when answered a certain way.
- **Online assessment (OA) / HireVue / game-based assessment**: a timed online stage before interviews: aptitude tests, a recorded video interview (HireVue is one vendor) or short games (Pymetrics, McKinsey Solve).
- **Return offer**: a full-time offer made at the end of an internship.
- **Exploding offer**: an offer with a short deadline, often a few days, after which it lapses.
- **Reneging**: accepting an offer and then withdrawing to take another. It can damage your standing with the employer and the school's careers office.
- **TC (total compensation)**: base salary plus bonus and other cash and equity. Compare TC, not base, across banks, consultancies and tech.
- **Sign-on bonus / stub bonus**: a one-off payment on joining; a stub bonus is a pro-rata bonus for the part of the first year worked.
- **Clawback**: a clause obliging you to repay a sign-on or relocation payment if you leave within a stated period. Ask for the period and whether the repayment is gross or net.
- **Up or out**: a promotion system in which people who are not promoted on schedule are expected to leave (consulting, banking, Big 4).
- **Bench**: time between projects, common in consulting and IT services, when you work on internal tasks.
- **PIP (performance improvement plan)**: a formal plan to fix performance, sometimes a step before dismissal.
- **FP&A**: financial planning and analysis, a corporate finance function.
- **SDR / AE / OTE**: in tech sales, sales development rep / account executive / on-target earnings (base plus target commission).
- **Founder's associate**: a generalist right-hand role to a startup founder.
- **Blue Book**: the European Commission's paid five-month traineeship. **EPSO AD5**: the entry-level EU civil-service competition.
- **CFA / FRM / CAIA**: investment, risk and alternative-investment qualifications. **ACA / ACCA / CIMA**: UK accounting qualifications.
- **Dottore commercialista / revisore legale**: Italian chartered accountant / statutory auditor. **WP / StB**: German Wirtschaftsprüfer / Steuerberater. **DEC**: French diplôme d'expertise comptable.

## Visas and tax
- **Graduate visa / Graduate Route (UK)**: post-study work permission without a sponsor. It lasts 2 years for applications until 31 December 2026 and 18 months from 1 January 2027.
- **Skilled Worker visa (UK)**: employer-sponsored work visa with salary floors.
- **HPI (High Potential Individual) visa**: a UK visa for graduates of listed top global universities, with no job offer needed.
- **OPT / STEM OPT (US)**: work permission after a US degree. 12 months, plus 24 more for STEM-designated degrees.
- **H-1B (US)**: an employer-sponsored work visa allocated by lottery. Since the FY2027 round the lottery is weighted by wage level: a higher wage level gets more entries.
- **Zoekjaar / orientation year (NL), RECE (France; replaced the general post-study APS in 2019), §20 AufenthG (Germany)**: post-study job-search permits.
- **APS (Akademische Prüfstelle, Germany)**: academic certificate verification required for applicants from China, India and Vietnam applying to German universities.
- **CEFR (A1–C2)**: the European language-level scale. B2 is upper-intermediate, C1 advanced, C2 near-native.
- **Sperrkonto (German blocked account)**: an account into which a non-EU student pays the year's living funds (€11,904 for 2026) and withdraws a fixed monthly amount, used as proof of funds for a student visa.
- **Alternance (France)**: a work-study or apprenticeship contract in which the employer pays a salary and the training cost while you study (money/costs-and-funding.md section 6).
- **Werkstudent (Germany)**: an enrolled student who works up to 20 hours a week in term time and is exempt from health, care and unemployment contributions but pays pension contributions.
- **Student visa funds rule (UK)**: you must show maintenance funds held for 28 consecutive days, ending within 31 days of applying.
- **IHS**: the UK Immigration Health Surcharge.
- **Impatriati regime (Italy)**: an income-tax relief for people moving their tax residence (back) to Italy, now in art. 225 of D.Lgs. 117/2026.
- **30% facility (NL) / Beckham law (Spain)**: tax reliefs for incoming workers, with conditions.
- **ISEE**: the Italian family-income indicator used for fee waivers and DSU/ISU grants.
- **Net minus rent**: take-home pay after income tax and social charges, minus a typical one-bedroom rent. The library uses it to compare cities.

## Data sources often cited
- **AlmaLaurea**: an Italian university consortium that surveys graduates at 1, 3 and 5 years. Bocconi, Polimi, LUISS and Cattolica are not members.
- **CGE**: the Conférence des Grandes Écoles, which runs the yearly French graduate employment survey.
- **BFS / FSO**: the Swiss Federal Statistical Office.
- **ISE**: the UK Institute of Student Employers.
- **GMAC**: the Graduate Management Admission Council (GMAT owner). It runs prospective-student and corporate-recruiter surveys.
- **Nuffic**: the Dutch organisation for internationalisation in education; it publishes graduate stay rates.
- **ISTAT**: the Italian national statistics institute.
