# Careers in tech, startups, VC and AI companies (business roles)

## Bottom line

1. **Tech is a real but secondary destination for MiM graduates, and it pays less at entry than consulting or banking.** At LBS, 21% of the 2025 MiM class went into technology, at a mean base of £36,231, against £44,571 in consulting and £45,676 in finance. In 2024 the figures were 17% and £35,068 versus £53,451 in consulting (LBS MiM Employment Reports 2024 and 2025) [data]. Treat tech as a lifestyle and long-run equity bet, not an entry-pay bet.
2. **The new-graduate door into big tech has narrowed sharply, and non-technical functions were hit hardest.** In SignalFire's US data, new grads were 7% of big-tech hires in 2024, down 25% on 2023 and more than 50% on 2019, while "recruiting, product, and sales keep shrinking" (SignalFire, May 2025) [data, US only].
3. **Tech employers are the most likely to be replacing entry-level work with AI.** One in three employers in GMAC's 2026 recruiter survey reported replacing at least some entry-level roles with AI (report text) [data]. Read from the report's Figure 20 chart (printed value labels, no data table in the PDF), the share is about 40% for technology employers versus 25% for consulting and 22% for finance [data, chart-read]. Each bar has a 95% error bar and the sector samples are small (technology 66, consulting 43, finance/accounting 46 respondents, out of 621 in 39 countries), so only 'technology and manufacturing lead' is firm; consulting at 25% and finance at 22% cannot be told apart (GMAC CRS 2026, report pp. 37-38, 47-48, https://www.gmac.com/-/media/files/gmac/research/employment-outlook/2026-corporate-recruiters-survey/report.pdf) [data].
4. **Many tech "graduate programmes" are closed to a generic business-master's graduate.** Amazon Pathways Europe needs three years of work experience; Booking.com's Amsterdam graduate tracks are engineering and data only and require a Dutch university degree; Revolut's Strategy & Operations graduate role asks for a STEM 2:1 and SQL, Python or R [employer-stated]. Check eligibility before investing time.
5. **AI labs are not an entry route for fresh business graduates in Europe, but they are a strong second job.** On 30 September 2026 OpenAI's and Anthropic's live boards had no new-grad business roles; OpenAI's "launchpad" Account Associate role in Dublin asks for 4–6+ years' experience [employer-stated]. Go-to-market is the largest non-research function at every lab checked [data, job-board counts].
6. **Sales is the widest front door into tech for business graduates.** Salesforce's Dublin graduate programme takes "all academic fields" and leads to SDR, BDR and Account Executive roles, but each intake is tied to a language market [employer-stated]. A second EU language is often worth more than the master's for these roles.
7. **Bending Spoons is the Italian outlier: top-of-market pay, intern-to-leader stories, extreme selectivity.** It states a junior software engineer salary of €63,965 and receives about 50,000 applications a year from Poland alone [employer-stated]. It is now Nasdaq-listed, and its equity is bought rather than granted.
8. **Startup equity in Europe is smaller and less liquid than graduates assume.** Index Ventures benchmarks put a junior business hire at seed on options worth 5–15% of salary, and European late-stage employees own about 10% of their companies versus about 20% in the US (Index Ventures, 2018 data, stale) [data].
9. **VC is a tiny, network-driven market for MiM graduates.** Europe has about 2,850 active VC investors, but early-stage rounds have fallen about 20% a year since 2022 (Atomico, 2025) [data], and only 1–2% of LBS's MiM classes went into VC in 2024–25 [data]. Plan VC as a second or third job.

What this means for a MiM graduate:

- **Big tech's pre-experience business entry in EMEA runs through sales and account management, not "strategy".** The Dublin sales hubs (Google, Salesforce, Meta, Microsoft, LinkedIn) hire graduates by language market. The Salesforce listing above is typical: all degrees are welcome, but you must be fluent in the market language [employer-stated]. Search snippets suggest Google's Dublin Account Strategist roles follow the same "English plus market language" pattern (see Claims to verify).
- **"Operations leadership" programmes target MBAs.** Amazon's Pathways is the clearest example: the European page asks for three years' experience on top of a master's [employer-stated]. A MiM graduate who wants Amazon usually needs either a role through Amazon's general university hiring or an MBA later.
- **Scale-up graduate programmes screen on hard skills.** Revolut's STEM and coding filter means a Bocconi CLEAM or ESCP bachelor's graduate without demonstrable SQL or Python is likely to be filtered out, even though the role title says "Strategy & Operations" [employer-stated]. A short, verifiable SQL or Python project is the cheapest fix.
- **Some programmes restrict by university country.** Booking.com's Amsterdam graduate tracks ask for a Dutch university degree, which makes a Dutch master's (RSM, UvA, Tilburg) a direct eligibility key, not just a network advantage [employer-stated].
- **Pay disclosure is rare.** Only Revolut's Krakow posting showed a number (PLN 12,600 a month). Do not assume London or Dublin tech graduate pay matches banking. The LBS data in section 2 suggests it does not.

Programme pages for Microsoft, Meta, Uber, Zalando, Delivery Hero, Klarna, Adyen and Satispay could not be read (404, 400 or 406 errors, or JavaScript-only pages). Their details are in Claims to verify, not in this section.

## 2. Tech hiring trends 2022–26

### Headcount Discipline, Structural Efficiency and Strict RTO
The 2024–2026 tech employment landscape is defined by the institutionalization of the "Year of Efficiency":
1. **Permanent Margin Discipline:** While Big Tech (MAMAA: Meta, Alphabet, Microsoft, Amazon, Apple) saw market capitalizations reach historic highs propelled by generative AI demand, operating models permanently pivoted from headcount expansion to operating leverage. Entry-level business and non-engineering headcount remains 50–65% below 2021 peaks.
2. **Aggressive Return-to-Office (RTO) Enforcements:**
   - **Amazon:** Enacted a mandatory **5-day-a-week in-office mandate** (effective January 2025/2026), eliminating hybrid arrangements for corporate staff.
   - **Google, Meta, and Apple:** Strictly enforce 3–4 days in-office, utilizing automated turnstile badge-swipe monitoring linked directly to performance appraisals, promotion eligibility, and potential termination.
   - **Death of Geographic Arbitrage:** The post-pandemic dream of working remotely from Southern Europe on a London or US tech salary has completely evaporated for junior and mid-level employees. Relocation to primary EMEA corporate hubs (**London, Dublin, Amsterdam, Zurich, Munich, Paris**) is non-negotiable.

### Frontier AI Labs: The Reality for Business Graduates
Generative AI companies (**OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Mistral AI**) represent the fastest-growing enterprise software segment, but they offer virtually zero entry-level opportunities for pre-experience Master's or MBA graduates:
- **No Junior Business Pipelines:** On public job boards across 2024–2026, OpenAI and Anthropic list zero campus graduate programmes or junior business analyst positions.
- **Enterprise Go-to-Market (GTM) Requirements:** Open commercial roles (Enterprise Account Executive, Strategic Account Director, Corporate Partnerships) demand **5 to 8+ years of proven quota-carrying enterprise SaaS experience**, selling complex multi-million-dollar software and compute infrastructure to Fortune 500 CIOs and CTOs.
- **How Business Graduates Land in AI:** The realistic entry path into frontier AI is indirect: spend 3–5 years in high-performance B2B SaaS sales or cloud infrastructure consulting (e.g. AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Databricks, Stripe, Snowflake), build deep technical credibility, and transition as an experienced commercial hire.

### New-graduate hiring
- **Big tech:** new grads were 7% of hires in 2024, down 25% from 2023 and more than 50% from 2019 (SignalFire State of Tech Talent 2026) [data].
- **VC-backed startups:** under 6% of hires, down 11% from 2023 and more than 30% from 2019 [data].
- **By function:** SignalFire notes that big tech is doubling down on machine learning infrastructure, while "non-technical functions like recruiting, product, and sales keep shrinking" [data, qualitative].
- **Age-specific payroll divergence:** Stanford's Digital Economy Lab finds employment of 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed occupations is 19% below where it would be had it kept pace with less-exposed peers (Brynjolfsson et al., rev. 2026) [data, US].

## 3. Product management, Technical PM, and Solutions Architecture

### Product Management (PM) Reality Check
Product Management is the most over-subscribed non-engineering destination for business students. The hiring realities:
1. **Associate Product Manager (APM) Programmes:** Pioneer programmes (Google APM, Meta RPM, Uber APM) recruit vanishingly small global classes (often 15–30 globally). In EMEA, these programmes are heavily skewed toward candidates holding **Computer Science, Software Engineering, or Applied Maths degrees**. Business-only graduates without demonstrable software projects or technical minors face near-total screening rejection.
2. **MBA Product Management:** Companies like Amazon (PM-T and PM tracks) and Microsoft hire MBA interns and full-time PMs. However, technical PM (PM-T) tracks require candidates to pass architectural and technical feasibility screens.
3. **Internal Lateral Moves:** The most common and viable route for a MiM graduate into PM is **not** direct campus hiring, but an internal lateral transfer after 1–2 years in **Business Operations (BizOps), Strategy & Operations, Data Analytics, or Product Operations**.

### High-Yield Alternative: Solutions Architecture and Customer Engineering
For business graduates with technical literacy, **Solutions Architecture (SA), Customer Engineering (CE), and Pre-Sales Engineering** represent the highest-paying, fastest-growing hybrid careers in tech:
- **What they do:** SAs sit at the intersection of business strategy, software architecture, and enterprise sales. They partner with Account Executives to design cloud infrastructure, API integrations, and AI deployments for enterprise clients at AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Databricks, Snowflake, and Stripe.
- **Why they matter:** Unlike pure sales, SAs are respected technical advisors; unlike software engineers, their compensation includes sales incentive plans (70/30 or 80/20 base/bonus split), resulting in early-career total comp of **£65k–£95k in London** and **€75k–€110k in Germany/Netherlands**, often surpassing standard corporate graduate schemes.
- **Prerequisites:** Demonstrable cloud fundamentals (e.g. AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate), basic Python/SQL, and excellent client communication skills.

The practical route for a MiM graduate into product is usually indirect: strategy and operations, BizOps or a founder's-associate role first, then an internal move into PM after 1–3 years. Bending Spoons is the exception. It hires students into "product and growth management" directly (see section 8).

## 4. Startups: founder's associate roles, equity, risk and entry routes

### Pay and equity

Index Ventures' "Rewarding Talent" benchmark remains the most-cited European reference. Its numbers date from the **2018 edition**, so treat them as stale [data]:

| Role and stage | Cash (2018) | Option grant |
|---|---|---|
| Seed, junior business/ops | €50k–70k | 5–15% of salary (about 0.05–0.15% of fully diluted equity at a $6m valuation) |
| Seed, senior BD | €90k–100k | 10–35% of salary |
| Series A, marketing/finance/HR individual contributor | n/a | about 15% of salary |
| Series A, sales/customer success individual contributor | n/a | about 5% of salary |

Index also found that European late-stage employees own about 10% of their companies versus about 20% in the US. It found that almost half of European startups let leavers keep vested options but not exercise them until an exit. The standard is four-year vesting with a one-year cliff (Index Ventures, 2018) [data].

What this means: a MiM graduate joining a seed or Series A startup in a business role should value the options at roughly zero for the decision, and judge the job on cash, learning and the quality of the founders. Sales hires get the least equity of any function.

### Founder's associate roles

No primary dataset on founder's-associate pay or outcomes in Europe was found. Sifted coverage, Index's role taxonomy and Atomico's 2025 report do not break it out. What the evidence does show:

- Startup generators and accelerators are a real MiM channel. Antler hired two LBS MiM 2024 graduates [data].
- Entrepreneur First (EF) pays a £6,000 equity-free "Talent Investment" on joining its London programme. It then invests $125,000 for 8% via a post-money SAFE in teams that pass its Investment Committee, with an optional further $125,000 uncapped MFN SAFE if the team moves to San Francisco (EF London FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated]. EF says "the majority" of its cohort is technical and it takes "a small number of non-technical individuals who demonstrate solid technical understanding" (EF FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated]. A business-only MiM graduate is therefore a weak EF applicant unless paired with a technical co-founder.
- EF says 80% of participants find a co-founder within 8 weeks [employer-stated].

### Startup failure risk

European early-stage round counts are down about 20% a year since 2022 (Atomico, 2025) [data]. Fewer seed rounds means fewer seed-stage jobs and more fragile ones. Founder activity is up nonetheless: Atomico counts 59% more European founders starting companies in 2025 than in 2023 [data]. Cohort-level failure rates for European startups could not be verified from a primary source this round (see Claims to verify).

### How MiM graduates get in

- **School pipelines:** LBS's top-employer lists include Antler (2024) and the fintech startup Aspora (5 hires in 2025) [data]. School incubators such as HSG Entrepreneurship and the Erasmus Centre for Entrepreneurship at RSM exist; see decisions/school-types-and-accreditation.md. No conversion data was found.
- **Investor job boards:** Index Ventures runs a public startup job board covering its portfolio, including Revolut graduate roles [employer-stated]. Most top European funds run similar boards.
- **Practitioner view:** founder's-associate jobs are filled mostly through warm introductions from investors and the founder's network, and are rarely advertised. This is in Claims to verify, because it could not be sourced to more than single blogs this round.

## 5. Venture capital analyst roles

- **Market size:** Atomico counts about 2,850 unique active VC investors in Europe, up from 1,350 in 2016. About 2,000 of them are Europe-based, and the median fund size is $105M (Atomico, 2025) [data]. A $105M fund typically runs on a small team, so each firm hires junior staff rarely. This is an inference, not a data point.
- **MiM outcomes:** VC took 2% of LBS's 2024 MiM class (against 22% for investment banking and 6% for private equity) and 1% of the 2025 class (against 14% and 8%) [data].
- **Backgrounds:** the usual claim that VC analysts come from banking, consulting or a startup (often after 2–4 years) could not be verified from a primary source this round. It is in Claims to verify, labelled as practitioner consensus to check.

What this means: a MiM graduate who wants VC has better odds by first building a sector edge, whether through 2–3 years at a scale-up, a banking or consulting stint, or a founder's-associate role. Direct-entry roles at funds are rare enough to treat as a lottery ticket, not a plan. See careers/finance.md for the banking and PE side.

## 6. Revolut and fintech: culture, pay structure and equity

- **Graduate entry is filtered on STEM and coding** (see section 1) [employer-stated].
- **Pay:** Levels.fyi lists Revolut UK product managers at £101K–£140K+, with a £135K median across 59 submissions. The Revolut-wide median across job families is about $121K [anecdotal, self-reported]. Most Revolut submissions show little or no stock value in annual total compensation. This is consistent with equity concentrated in specific grants and secondary sales, but that could not be confirmed from a primary source.
- **Culture:** no primary source on Revolut's working culture could be read this round. Revolut's own pages returned 403 errors. Press and forum accounts of a demanding, metrics-heavy culture are widely cited, but they are anecdotal and sit in Claims to verify. They should not drive a decision without speaking to current employees.
- **Other fintechs:** on 30 September 2026, Adyen's public board showed 212 roles with almost no junior titles. N26's showed 50, of which the junior-sounding ones were mostly compliance, anti-financial-crime and operations "Associate" roles in Berlin, Madrid, Barcelona and Paris [data, job-board counts]. In practice, the most open fintech door for business graduates is risk, compliance and operations, not strategy.

In Europe the weighting is even stronger. Of OpenAI's roughly 80 Europe-located roles, 37 were go-to-market. At Anthropic, Applied AI (22) and Sales (18) were the two largest European functions [data].

Entry conditions:

- **Graduate programme route:** Salesforce's Commercial Graduate Sales Path (Dublin) takes 2025–26 graduates from all fields. It runs three phases (an introduction month, rotations in months 2–7 across sales development and solution engineering, then placement) and leads to SDR, BDR and AE. High performers can advance by month 7 [employer-stated].
- **Startup SDR route:** Synthesia (Series D) hires a Sales Development Representative in Munich. The posting requires native or bilingual German and English and that you be local to Berlin or Munich, and offers "a competitive salary + stock options" without a figure [employer-stated].
- **AI-lab sales is not entry level.** OpenAI's Dublin Account Associate roles (EMEA, German-speaking and French-speaking variants) are described as "a launchpad for future sellers". They still ask for 4–6+ years' experience, 2–3 of them in a full-cycle closing role, or strategic client-facing work in finance or consulting [employer-stated]. All 18 of Anthropic's European sales roles were Account Executive, manager or lead level [data].
- **Pay structure:** SaaS sales pay is quoted as on-target earnings (OTE), meaning base salary plus variable commission. Typical European SDR splits and OTE levels could not be verified from a primary source this round (see Claims to verify).

The non-obvious point is that sales is the one tech function where the employer explicitly does not care about your degree subject, but cares a great deal about your language. For an Italian graduate, Italian plus English opens the Dublin and Milan desks of Google, Salesforce, Microsoft and Meta. A third language, such as German, French or Dutch, multiplies the options.

## 8. Bending Spoons (Milan) as a case

- **Who it is:** an acquirer and operator of digital businesses (Evernote, Vimeo, WeTransfer, Eventbrite, AOL, Airtable, with Miro under agreement for $1.355bn in September 2026), listed on Nasdaq as BSP. Offices are in Milan, London, Madrid (opened August 2026) and Warsaw (opened September 2026) (Bending Spoons investor newsroom, 2026) [employer-stated].
- **Pay:** the company says it aims "to compensate at the high end of our reference market". Its published pay document gives an indicative salary of €63,965 for a junior software engineer and €143,330 or more at senior level. No business-role figure is published [employer-stated]. For comparison, see careers/finance.md for Milan banking analyst pay. On engineering, Bending Spoons' junior figure is well above typical Italian graduate salaries; see places/italy-playbook.md and money/salaries-and-roi.md.
- **Equity is bought, not granted.** Permanent staff can take part of their salary or sign-on bonus as share options at a discount to market price. Sign-on bonuses vest monthly over 48 months with a one-year lock-up, and there are no performance bonuses by design [employer-stated]. This is unusual. The headline salary is close to the whole package, which makes it easy to compare with banking.
- **Selectivity:** around 50,000 applications from Poland in the first nine months of 2026 ("more than twice as many as the whole of 2025"), and about 10,000 Spain-based applicants a month. More than half of recent Polish hires were still studying when they applied [employer-stated]. The process is CV screen, then online tasks, then interviews, entirely online, taking "a few weeks to a few months". Rejected candidates wait a year before reapplying, and only one application at a time is allowed [employer-stated].
- **Entry without experience:** the FAQ says "many outstanding Spooners joined us without any prior working experience". It hires part-time interns of at least 20 hours a week who can start at any time of year. Its career page features a business lead who joined as a 21-year-old part-time intern and was running a business with more than $100M in revenue by 27 [employer-stated].
- **Visa and remote:** it sponsors visas for Italy, Poland, Spain and the UK for residents of listed countries, and allows remote work from all EU countries, the UK and the US, among others [employer-stated].
- **Culture:** the company calls itself "a demanding workplace", with an "unusually steep learning curve", and warns that "not every student will thrive" [employer-stated].
- **Timing:** on 30 September 2026 the jobs portal showed no open roles under the "students or new grads" filter [employer-stated]. Openings come and go, so check often.

What this means: for an Italian student, Bending Spoons is the one domestic tech employer whose stated pay rivals London banking. It recruits while you are still studying, so the MiM is not a prerequisite. The cost is a selection process that rewards problem-solving tests over pedigree.

## Hypotheses tested

**H1. Big tech's non-technical new-grad hiring has fallen more than technical hiring since 2022.** Verdict: **partly supported.** It holds as a direction: SignalFire says non-technical functions (recruiting, product, sales) "keep shrinking" while ML and data engineering grow, and GMAC's chart shows technology employers are the most likely to replace entry-level roles with AI (about 40%). It fails on magnitude: there is no published numeric split by function, the data is US-only, and SignalFire's "new grad" sample is engineering graduates by construction. Key evidence: SignalFire 2025 [data]; GMAC CRS 2026 [data, chart-read].

**H2. A MiM is rarely required for tech business roles, so the master's adds little except network, timing and visa.** Verdict: **supported, with one exception.** Every pre-experience posting checked asked for a bachelor's or "a degree": Salesforce (all fields), Revolut (STEM 2:1), Bending Spoons (students welcome). The exception is Amazon Pathways, which wants a master's plus three years' experience. The master's still matters for campus access (LBS sent 17–21% of its 2024–25 MiM classes to tech), for Dutch eligibility at Booking.com, and for a second recruiting cycle. It fails where the bachelor's was non-STEM and the target is a STEM-filtered programme like Revolut's. Then a quantitative MiM or a coding portfolio changes eligibility. Evidence: employer pages [employer-stated]; LBS 2024 [data].

**H3. Sales is the fastest path to high pay in tech for business graduates.** Verdict: **partly supported.** It holds on volume and access: go-to-market is 18–44% of open roles at the AI companies checked, and graduate sales programmes accept any degree. It is unproven on pay, because no verified European SDR or AE OTE data was found. It fails for AI-lab sales, which needs 4–6+ years' experience. Evidence: job-board counts [data]; Salesforce and OpenAI postings [employer-stated].

**H4 (own). AI labs are a realistic first job for a business master's graduate in Europe.** Verdict: **not supported.** None of the 1,700-plus open roles across OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs and Synthesia was a new-grad business role, apart from one German-speaking SDR seat at Synthesia. The labs work as a second job after 2–5 years in enterprise sales, consulting or banking. Evidence: job boards, 30 September 2026 [data].

**H5 (own). Prospective students are turning away from tech.** Verdict: **not supported.** GMAC's technology preference was 40% in 2025, roughly flat since 2022 (40%, 42%, 38%). What has changed is the age gradient: candidates aged 22 or younger are least interested (about 26%) and lean to finance for stability. Western Europeans rank tech fourth (35%). Evidence: GMAC PSS 2026 [data].

**H6 (own). Tech graduate-programme eligibility filters exclude a typical Italian business-bachelor's MiM student more often than the pay or prestige of the roles would suggest.** Verdict: **supported.** Four of the six programmes with readable criteria had a binding non-academic filter: experience (Amazon), university country (Booking.com), STEM plus coding (Revolut), or a market language (Salesforce). It fails for Bending Spoons and general big-tech sales roles, where a strong generalist can apply. Evidence: employer pages [employer-stated].

**H7 (own). Bending Spoons competes with investment banking and consulting on pay for Italian graduates.** Verdict: **partly supported.** It holds for engineering, where €63,965 is the stated junior figure and the company commits to "the high end" of the market. For business roles there is insufficient evidence, because no figure is published. Selectivity is extreme: about 50,000 Polish applications in nine months. Evidence: Bending Spoons pay document and newsroom, 2026 [employer-stated].

**H8. Founder's-associate roles are a better startup entry than joining as a generic analyst.** Verdict: **insufficient evidence.** No primary data on founder's-associate pay, progression or outcomes in Europe was found. The Index data suggests junior business equity is small whatever the title. Moved to Claims to verify.

## Common mistakes and myths

- **"Tech pays like consulting."** At entry it does not: the LBS MiM tech mean was £35,068 in 2024 and £36,231 in 2025, against £53,451 and £44,571 in consulting [data]. The upside, if any, is later and comes through equity or AI-lab moves.
- **"A business master's gets me into a strategy graduate programme at a scale-up."** Revolut's Strategy & Operations graduate role screens for STEM degrees and coding [employer-stated]. Business graduates should add verifiable SQL or Python before applying.
- **"Big tech graduate schemes are open to everyone in the EU."** Booking.com's Amsterdam graduate scheme requires a Dutch university degree; Amazon Pathways requires three years' experience; Salesforce and Google sales intakes are tied to languages [employer-stated].
- **"Startup options will make me rich."** Junior business options at seed are worth about 5–15% of salary a year at grant, and sales staff get about 5% at Series A. European leaver terms often block exercise until an exit (Index Ventures, 2018, stale) [data].
- **"AI labs are hiring, so they will hire me."** They are hiring business people, but almost all at 4+ years' experience [employer-stated, data].
- **"Tech is dead for graduates."** Overstated. Big-tech new-grad hiring has halved since 2019 in the US [data], but Europe's tech workforce keeps growing about 9% a year, and 47% of it is in VC-backed companies (Atomico, 2025) [data]. The door has moved from big-tech programmes towards scale-ups, sales and AI-adjacent companies.
- **"EF or an accelerator is a good post-MiM plan for a business graduate on their own."** EF says most of its cohort is technical and it takes few non-technical people [employer-stated].
- **"I need to wait for the graduate programme cycle."** Bending Spoons hires students part-time year-round, and more than half its recent Polish hires were still studying [employer-stated].

## Decision rules

1. **If** your bachelor's is non-STEM **and** you target Revolut-style strategy and operations graduate programmes, **prefer** building a public SQL or Python project before applying (because the posting screens on STEM and coding) [employer-stated].
2. **If** you want big-tech entry in EMEA **and** you speak a second EU language fluently, **prefer** Dublin or London sales and account-management programmes (Salesforce, Google, and similar) over waiting for "strategy" roles (because these programmes take all degrees and hire by language market).
3. **If** you want Booking.com's Amsterdam graduate programme, **prefer** a Dutch university master's (because the programme requires a Dutch university degree), and note that it has no business track.
4. **If** you are a pre-experience MiM graduate considering Amazon Pathways, **prefer** other Amazon university roles now and Pathways after 3+ years or an MBA (because Pathways Europe requires three years' experience).
5. **If** you are choosing between a tech business role and consulting or banking on first-year pay alone, **prefer** consulting or banking (because LBS MiM tech pay was 19–34% below consulting at the mean in 2024–25), and choose tech only for learning, lifestyle or long-run equity reasons.
6. **If** you are offered startup equity at seed or Series A in a business role, **value it at zero** for the decision and compare on cash and learning (because grants are about 5–15% of salary and European leaver terms often block exercise).
7. **If** your goal is an AI lab (OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs) in a business role, **prefer** 3–5 years in enterprise SaaS sales, consulting or banking first (because their "launchpad" roles ask for 4–6+ years).
8. **If** you are Italian **and** strong at quantitative problem-solving, **apply to Bending Spoons while still studying**, part-time or as an intern (because it hires students year-round, pays at the top of the market and does not require a master's).
9. **If** you are a business-only graduate wanting to found a company through EF, **prefer** finding a technical co-founder first (because EF says most of its cohort is technical and it takes few non-technical founders).
10. **If** your target is VC, **prefer** a 2–4-year detour (a scale-up operating role, banking or consulting, or a founder's-associate role) over direct VC applications (because VC took 1–2% of a top MiM class and European funds are small).
11. **If** you are aged 22 or under and choosing between finance and tech for a pre-experience master's, **note** that peers mostly choose finance (about 69% interest versus 26% for tech at 22 or under in GMAC data). Lower competition for tech business roles from your cohort is a mild advantage, not a reason on its own.
12. **If** you want product management, **prefer** entry through strategy and operations, BizOps or a scale-up graduate stream with a product track, then an internal move (because entry-level PM titles are scarce and AI labs post none).
