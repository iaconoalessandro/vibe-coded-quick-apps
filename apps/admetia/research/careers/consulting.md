# Management consulting careers for pre-experience master's graduates

## Bottom line

1. **In Germany and Austria, a master's buys a higher entry title, not just a better CV.** McKinsey Germany starts bachelor's graduates as "Junior Fellow" and master's graduates as "Fellow", with PhDs entering as "Associate" (e-fellows.net McKinsey FAQ, 2026, https://www.e-fellows.net/unternehmen/mckinsey/faq-mckinsey) [employer-stated, via employer partner page]. In the US and UK, MBB treat undergraduates and non-MBA master's graduates as one entry class on one salary band (Poets&Quants / Management Consulted, 2025 data, https://poetsandquantsforundergrads.com/students/consulting-pay-what-business-graduates-earned-in-2025/) [practitioner consensus]. For a reader choosing between a UK one-year MSc and a DACH two-year MiM for consulting, this is the key structural difference.
2. **MBB is still hiring consultants in 2026, and the cuts fall mainly on support staff.** McKinsey said it would grow its North American workforce by 12% in 2026 versus 2025 (Storyboard18 citing Reuters/WSJ, Sept 2025, https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-marketing/mckinsey-counters-ai-job-fears-with-12-graduate-hiring-boost-in-2026-80845.htm) [employer-stated]. It is also cutting about 10% of non-client-facing roles over 18–24 months while saying it "would continue to hire consultants" (People Matters citing Bloomberg, Dec 2025, https://www.peoplematters.in/news/strategic-hr/as-mckinsey-marks-100-years-top-management-plans-10percent-workforce-cuts-47709) [practitioner consensus]. BCG grew revenue 7% to $14.4bn in 2025 and headcount to 33,500 (BCG press release, 23 Apr 2026, https://www.bcg.com/ja-jp/press/23april2026-bcg-revenue-22nd-consecutive-year-growth) [employer-stated].
3. **But the MBB pyramid has shrunk from its 2022 peak, and pay has stopped rising.** McKinsey went from about 45,100 staff at end-2023 to about 40,000 by May 2025 (Fortune, May 2025, https://fortune.com/2025/05/29/mckinsey-headcount-down-more-than-10-percent) [practitioner consensus]. US MBB entry salaries were flat for a third consecutive year in Management Consulted's 2026 report (2025 offers), at a $112k base at Bain and McKinsey and $110k at BCG for undergraduate and master's hires (P&Q, 26 Jan 2026, quoting Management Consulted; the data come from MC's own clients' offers, not from the firms) [practitioner consensus]. One practitioner site reports a McKinsey and Bain rise to $120k from January 2027; no press report or second source was found, so it is unverified. Expect a flat or slightly rising offer in Europe too, not the jumps of 2021–22.
4. **The Big 4 are where graduate intake is actually being cut.** PwC UK cut its entry-level intake from 1,500 to 1,300, and its UK chief blamed "the wider slowdown in investment, hiring and deal-making" (Consultancy.uk, 12 Sept 2025, https://www.consultancy.uk/news/41565/pwc-slashes-200-graduate-roles-amid-uk-slowdown) [employer-stated]. The same report says PwC in the US was weighing a cut of nearly one-third in entry-level hiring over three years [practitioner consensus]. A Big 4 consulting offer is therefore a less secure fallback than it was in 2021.
5. **The MBB pay premium over the Big 4 is roughly 1.4–1.8x in the UK and 1.2–1.6x in France and Germany, and smallest in the US (1.05–1.25x against Big 4 strategy arms).** Master's-level entry totals as quoted by one aggregator family: London about £60k at MBB against £34–39k at the Big 4 (PrepLounge UK 2026, https://www.preplounge.com/en/blog/consulting/salary/uk); Germany €80–90k against €55–60k (PrepLounge DACH, Dec 2025, https://www.preplounge.com/de/articles/consulting-salary-germany); France up to €90–100k against €42–47k (PrepLounge France 2025, https://www.preplounge.com/en/blog/consulting/salary/france); US about $130–134k against $100–110k (PrepLounge USA, Jan 2026, https://www.preplounge.com/en/blog/consulting/salary/usa) [practitioner consensus, single aggregator family]. These are indicative ranges, not firm-published pay. PrepLounge's UK MBB bases (BCG £49.5k, Bain £52.2k) are identical to Management Consulted's own page, so the two are one source, not two; the Financial Times (via eFinancialCareers, 30 Sep 2026) independently puts UK Big Four graduate pay at about £36k [practitioner consensus]. The "about 2x" for Paris compares MBB maximum totals with Big 4 base pay; base to base it is about 1.4–1.6x (about 1.3x on a Planete Grandes Ecoles grid, 13 Feb 2026), so it does not make France clearly the place where the "MBB or nothing" bet pays most.
6. **In Germany, tier 2 pays close to MBB.** One aggregator lists Kearney at €80k base, Roland Berger at €72k and Simon-Kucher at €68k, against €75–80k at MBB, while Accenture is at €58k and the Big 4 at €50–55k (PrepLounge DACH, Dec 2025, link above) [practitioner consensus, single source]. A tier-2 strategy offer in Munich is not a big financial downgrade. A Big 4 or Accenture offer is.
7. **In DACH, the internship is the front door.** BCG's "Visiting Associate" internship (8–10 weeks, up to 12 for mandatory internships) is open from the third bachelor's semester, and top performers get a binding full-time offer through its "FAST FORWARD" programme (BCG careers, 2026, https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/visiting-associate-germany-austria) [employer-stated]. McKinsey's Fellow Intern programme runs 8–12 weeks and can end in a full-time offer (e-fellows.net, 2026, https://www.e-fellows.net/unternehmen/mckinsey/fellow-intern-programm) [employer-stated, via partner page]. Interns selected for FAST FORWARD get a permanent offer without further interviews (BCG, 2026) [employer-stated]. A MiM with a built-in internship or gap semester fits this model (check each programme's rules in decisions/programme-choice.md).
8. **Without fluent German, DACH MBB is effectively closed.** McKinsey's recruiting lead says fluent German is "zwingend erforderlich" (mandatory) for Germany and Austria (e-fellows, 1 Aug 2025, https://www.e-fellows.net/unternehmen/mckinsey/bewerbungstipps-von-mckinsey) [employer-stated via partner]. BCG's second round includes one case in German and one in English (BCG, 2026, https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/bewerbungsprozess-germany-austria) [employer-stated]. Roland Berger asks for "a good command of the language local to the office" everywhere (Roland Berger FAQ, 2026, https://www.rolandberger.com/en/Join/Your-application/FAQ/) [employer-stated]. A Mannheim or St. Gallen MiM does not get around this.
9. **AI is changing selection faster than it is changing headcount.** McKinsey has piloted case interviews where graduate candidates must use its internal AI tool Lilli, and are judged on prompting, checking the output and applying it (CFO.com citing the FT, 14 Jan 2026, https://cfo.com/news/mckinsey-ai-hiring-experiment-college-graduates-puts-pressure-on-up-or-out-career-model-cfo-/809674/) [practitioner consensus]. Prepare for AI-assisted cases, not just paper cases.
10. **Dubai and Riyadh pay well, tax-free, but openness to European juniors is unproven.** Self-reported McKinsey Business Analyst pay in Dubai is about AED 276k in total (levels.fyi, updated Sept 2026, https://www.levels.fyi/companies/mckinsey/salaries/management-consultant/locations/greater-dubai-area) [anecdotal, self-reported]. That is roughly €64–65k net at about 4.3 AED per euro (our conversion). I found no primary source on the nationality mix of Middle East entry classes. Treat "the Gulf is the easy MBB door" as unproven (see Hypotheses).

**The non-obvious point for a MiM candidate:** in the US and UK the degree level does not change the entry title. Management Consulted's 2025 data gives one "Undergraduate/Master's Hires" line for MBB, with a $112k base at Bain and McKinsey ($110k at BCG) and a total of $137–140k including the maximum bonus and a signing bonus (P&Q, 2025 data, https://poetsandquantsforundergrads.com/students/consulting-pay-what-business-graduates-earned-in-2025/) [practitioner consensus]. PrepLounge's US guide also treats "Undergrad/Master's first-year" as one band (Jan 2026, https://www.preplounge.com/en/blog/consulting/salary/usa) [practitioner consensus]. In Germany, McKinsey's ladder separates Junior Fellow (bachelor's) from Fellow (master's) (e-fellows FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated via partner]. A search snippet from another e-fellows page adds that bachelor's hires move into the Fellowship after one year of consulting (unverified, snippet only). If that is right, the German master's is worth about one year of seniority.

**Pre-Experience Master's vs. Post-MBA Leveling:**
- **Pre-experience Master's (MSc / MiM):** Enters at the **analyst level** (Business Analyst at McKinsey, Associate at BCG, Associate Consultant at Bain). In the UK and US, there is zero salary or title distinction between a 21-year-old BSc graduate and a 23-year-old MSc graduate. In DACH (Germany, Austria, Switzerland), the MSc earns a higher sub-grade (Fellow vs Junior Fellow) and ~€5,000–€10,000 higher starting base.
- **Post-MBA (Full-Time MBA from top programmes):** Enters at the **post-MBA consultant level** (Associate at McKinsey, Consultant at BCG/Bain). US base pay is $190,000–$192,000 with total compensation reaching $250,000–$270,000; UK post-MBA base is £95,000–£110,000; DACH post-MBA base is €115,000–€130,000. Master's candidates cannot apply for post-MBA roles unless they possess 3–5+ years of pre-master's full-time corporate experience.

### Tier-2 Strategy and Boutique Positioning
The "Tier 2" umbrella covers firms with distinct industry moats and selectivity:
- **Oliver Wyman:** The undisputed global leader in **Financial Services and Risk Management**. Oliver Wyman's FS practice advises the largest global central banks, sovereign wealth funds, and bulge-bracket banks. It also boasts a fast-growing Actuarial, Health & Life Sciences, and Payments franchise. Pays £55k+ base in London and ~$110k in the US.
- **Roland Berger:** The leading European-headquartered global strategy firm. Heavyweight market share in **DACH, Automotive, Capital Goods, and Restructuring**. German offices are considered peer-equivalent to MBB on prestige and corporate alumni placement across DAX 40 boardrooms.
- **Kearney:** Renowned for **Operations, Procurement, Supply Chain, and Manufacturing**. During cost-cutting and margin-compression macro cycles, Kearney's operations practice wins major Fortune 500 mandates. Base pay in Germany (€80k) matches MBB.
- **Strategy& (PwC) and EY-Parthenon:** Dedicated strategy houses distinct from their parent accounting networks. Strategy& (legacy Booz & Company) focuses on corporate strategy and deals. EY-Parthenon is a market leader in **Private Equity Commercial Due Diligence (CDD)**, evaluating target assets under tight 2-to-4 week sprints for mega-fund and upper-middle-market PE sponsors. Both firms run independent recruiting processes with starting bases (£50k–£55k in London) far above parent Big 4 audit/advisory schemes (£32k–£36k).
- **Alvarez & Marsal (A&M) and AlixPartners:** The premier **Restructuring, Turnaround, and Performance Improvement** firms. In the 2023–2026 high-interest-rate environment, these firms experienced explosive revenue growth as debt refinancing stresses and corporate distress triggered turnaround mandates and Chief Restructuring Officer (CRO) appointments. A&M's Private Equity Performance Improvement (PEPI) practice is also a major destination for former consultants and corporate finance graduates.
- **L.E.K. Consulting:** Highly regarded boutique specializing in Healthcare, Life Sciences, MedTech, and Private Equity due diligence. Strong UK and US presence with very quantitative, data-driven casework.

### Which offices hire pre-experience master's graduates?
- **Germany, Austria, Switzerland:** the default entrant holds a master's. PrepLounge publishes DACH entry pay for master's graduates only (Dec 2025, https://www.preplounge.com/de/articles/consulting-salary-germany) [practitioner consensus]. McKinsey's structure also treats the master's as the normal entry level [employer-stated via partner]. Volume is meaningful: BCG says it plans "a high three-digit number" of new hires in Central Europe in 2026, most of them in Germany (BCG Germany/Austria FAQ, 2026, https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/faq-germany-austria) [employer-stated]. That figure covers all levels, not only graduates. Roland Berger does not split by degree: anyone who has "completed an undergraduate degree" is "on track to becoming an Analyst or Junior Consultant" (Roland Berger FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated].
- **UK:** MBB and the Big 4 run undergraduate graduate schemes. Most of the PrepLounge UK MBB figures are labelled "Master's graduates" but sit on the same band as undergraduates (PrepLounge UK 2026) [practitioner consensus]. A UK MSc gives no pay premium at entry. Its value is access: school brand and a second recruiting cycle.
- **France and Italy:** the grande école Master in Management (bac+5) is the normal entry degree. Consultor lists HEC, ESSEC, ESCP, Polytechnique, CentraleSupélec, Mines, Ponts, Télécom, emlyon and Sciences Po as target schools for entry packages (Consultor, 2019, https://www.consultor.fr/articles/quels-salaires-pour-les-consultants-en-strategie) [practitioner consensus; pre-2023, possibly stale]. For Italian offices I could not fetch a primary source in this session (see Claims to verify; see places/italy-playbook.md).

## 2. Hiring trends 2023–26

### Macro Realities: Delayed Start Dates, Intake Contractions, and Managing-Out
The 2023–2026 consulting hiring cycle has been defined by three major structural shifts following the 2021–2022 post-pandemic hiring bubble:
1. **Widespread Start-Date Deferrals:** In 2023 and 2024, McKinsey, BCG, and Bain instituted unprecedented start-date deferrals across their incoming full-time classes. Bain offered incoming hires $30,000–$40,000 stipends to delay start dates by up to a year to work at non-profits, pursue language study, or take sabbaticals. McKinsey pushed start dates into 2024 and 2025. While deferral backlogs normalized in late 2025 and 2026, intake class sizes were recalibrated downward by 20–30% from peak levels.
2. **Aggressive Up-or-Out and "Counselled to Leave" (CTL):** When client demand slowed and voluntary attrition to tech and private equity dropped, firms accelerated internal performance evaluations. At McKinsey, global headcount dropped from ~45,100 to ~40,000 between late 2023 and May 2025 through Project Magnolia cuts and stringent semi-annual performance reviews. The traditional "up-or-out" runway was compressed: underperforming analysts and associates were given 2–3 month "search phases" (CTL) rather than the historic 6–9 month transition cushion.
3. **Flat Starting Compensation:** In response to margin compression and excess junior capacity, MBB starting salaries were frozen flat for three consecutive years (2023–2025/2026) at $110,000–$112,000 for US undergraduate/master's hires, breaking a multi-decade trend of steady annual increases.

### How to read this
- **The generalist MBB entry class is stable to slightly up. The support and tech layers are being cut.** Both McKinsey statements (12% North American growth; cuts aimed at non-client-facing roles) point the same way [employer-stated / practitioner consensus]. Note that the 12% figure is for North America. I found no equivalent statement for European offices.
- **BCG's growth is tilted towards technical hires.** The 2025 hiring emphasis was "AI engineers, data scientists, IT architects, and deep-industry specialists" (BCG press release, Apr 2026) [employer-stated]. A search snippet mentions BCG hiring "Forward Deployed AI Engineers" from campus (Management Consulted, 2026; unverified, snippet only). For a MiM graduate this means a quantitative or technical profile widens the door. It does not mean the generalist Associate role has gone.
- **The Big 4 and Accenture carry more intake risk.** A snippet reports UK graduate postings at the Big 4 down 44% in 2025, with KPMG's scheme down from 1,399 (2023) to 942 (unverified, snippet only; see Claims to verify). Only PwC's cut is confirmed on the record here.

### AI and junior work
- **McKinsey Lilli:** snippets report that over 75% of about 43,000 staff use it monthly, and that it saves about 30% of research and synthesis time (Fortune/others, June 2025; unverified, snippet only). What is confirmed is that McKinsey is testing candidates on how well they use it (CFO.com, Jan 2026) [practitioner consensus].
- **BCG Deckster / GENE:** a snippet says Deckster had been used over 450,000 times since March 2024, saving heavy users two to three hours per deck (Business Insider, 2026; unverified, snippet only). BCG's own page on GENE exists (BCG, 27 Sept 2024, https://www.bcg.com/news/27september2024-introducing-gene-bcgs-ai-chatbot; not fetched).
- **Why this matters (non-obvious):** the tasks AI is absorbing (slide formatting, desk research, first drafts) are the first-year analyst's training ground. The CFO.com piece argues this may push performance expectations earlier, making "up or out" bite sooner (CFO.com, Jan 2026) [practitioner consensus, single outlet]. Expect to be judged on synthesis and client judgement in year one, not year three.

The premium is largest where the comparator is the audit-led graduate scheme rather than a Big 4 strategy arm. ## 7. Exit options and "up or out"

- McKinsey attributed its 2023–25 headcount fall partly to "performance review firings" (Fortune, May 2025) [employer-stated]. This is up-or-out working as designed. Lower voluntary attrition is one reason the firm needs fewer new hires, and Management Consulted names low attrition as a driver of flat pay (MC 2026 salary report, snippet via search; unverified, snippet only).
- The CFO.com piece argues AI may bring the up-or-out filter forward, so fewer juniors survive to the promotion point (CFO.com, Jan 2026) [practitioner consensus, single outlet].

- BCG's FAST FORWARD offer can be held while you finish your degree, take a gap year or do further internships, which lets a MiM student lock in consulting and still test other options (BCG binding programmes page, 2026, https://careers.bcg.com/global/en/germany-austria-bindungsprogramme) [employer-stated].

## 8. Culture: hours, travel, burnout

- **Travel model, Europe:** Roland Berger states that "in general, you'll be working four days on-site and spending Friday at the office", and that over 75% of its work is cross-border (Roland Berger FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated]. Projects run from a few weeks to a year, with teams from one or two consultants up to 20 [employer-stated]. So the four-day client-site week is not only a US model. It is the stated default at Germany's largest home-grown strategy firm.
- **Hours: one quantified source, old and North American.** Perlow and Porter's study of about 1,600 BCG managers and professionals in North America found 92% working at least 50 hours a week and a third more than 65; after "predictable time off" teams were introduced, the average week fell from 64.5 to 57.7 hours (HBR Oct 2009; Wharton Knowledge, 3 Jul 2012, https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/article/remaking-the-workplace-one-night-off-at-a-time/) [data: single firm, 2000s, stale]. I found no current figure for any European MBB office, Bain or McKinsey, and no firm discloses consultant hours.
- **Official pay statistics hide this.** UK ONS data show full-time employees in management consultancy (SIC 7022) at a mean 38.0 paid hours a week in 2025, below the 38.5 all-industry mean, because ASHE counts paid hours and salaried overtime is unpaid (ONS ASHE Table 16.9a, 2025 provisional, https://www.ons.gov.uk/employmentandlabourmarket/peopleinwork/earningsandworkinghours/datasets/industry4digitsic2007ashetable16) [data]. Do not read that as evidence of short hours.
- **Burnout:** no consulting-specific survey was found. The nearest quantified UK profession is accounting (caba 2024, see careers/accounting-and-corporate.md section 8) [data: self-reported]. The claim that European MBB offices staff more locally than US offices is unconfirmed (see Claims to verify).
## Hypotheses tested

**H1. "In DACH, MBB hires more pre-experience MiM graduates than bachelor's graduates."**
Verdict: **partly supported** (structure, not counts).
- Holds: McKinsey Germany's ladder treats the master's as the standard entry degree and gives bachelor's hires a separate, lower "Junior Fellow" title (e-fellows FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated via partner]. PrepLounge publishes DACH entry pay for master's graduates only (Dec 2025) [practitioner consensus].
- Fails or unknown: BCG's VA internship is open from the third bachelor's semester (BCG careers, 2026) [employer-stated], so bachelor's students are clearly recruited. No firm publishes the degree mix of its DACH entry class.
- Practical reading: a bachelor's is enough to get in, but the master's is the normal level and pays a higher title.

**H2. "A Big 4 consulting offer now carries more risk of deferral or layoff than an MBB offer."**
Verdict: **partly supported**.
- Holds: PwC UK cut its graduate intake by 200 (employer-stated). PwC US was weighing a one-third cut over three years (Consultancy.uk, Sept 2025). Accenture's 11,000+ cuts (unverified, snippet only). McKinsey, by contrast, is aiming its cuts at support staff and still hiring consultants (People Matters/Bloomberg, Dec 2025), and plans 12% North American growth (Storyboard18/Reuters, Sept 2025).
- Fails: MBB were the firms that deferred MBA start dates in 2023 (unverified, snippet only), and McKinsey cut about 10% of total headcount through 2023–25 (Fortune, May 2025). The evidence covers intake size more than rescinded offers. No rescission data was found for either group.
- Condition: most true for audit-adjacent advisory and deals teams tied to deal volume, which PwC's chief named as the driver.

**H3. "The Middle East is the most open MBB door for European graduates in 2025–26."**
Verdict: **insufficient evidence**.
- For: high tax-free pay (McKinsey BA about AED 276k, levels.fyi, Sept 2026 [anecdotal]); a fast-growing Saudi market (PrepLounge cites 18.2% annual growth from $3.2bn in 2023 [practitioner consensus]).
- Against or unknown: no source on the nationality mix of Gulf entry classes, on Saudization quotas in consulting, or on Arabic requirements was fetched. The Saudi pay data is post-MBA only.
- Do not build a decision rule on this until verified.

**H4. "AI will cut analyst intake at MBB within three years."**
Verdict: **not supported for MBB on current firm statements; partly supported for the Big 4**.
- Against: McKinsey (+12% North America 2026, 15–20% growth over five years, per Storyboard18/Reuters) and BCG (headcount up to 33,500, press release April 2026) are both still growing client-facing staff [employer-stated]. BCG says AI work is over 40% of revenue, which creates demand for staff as well as replacing it.
- For: PwC's reported one-third entry-level cut over three years, explicitly tied to AI (Consultancy.uk, Sept 2025). McKinsey's shrink from 45,100 to about 40,000 (Fortune, May 2025).
- Condition: firm statements are forward-looking PR and could reverse in a downturn. The more likely MBB change is in the **mix**, with more technical and AI hires (BCG press release), rather than fewer total juniors.

**H5 (own). "A master's raises your entry level and pay in Germany but not in the UK or US."**
Verdict: **supported**.
- Evidence: Junior Fellow vs Fellow at McKinsey Germany [employer-stated via partner]; one "Undergraduate/Master's" band at US MBB (P&Q/MC) and in PrepLounge's US guide [practitioner consensus].
- Where it fails: a UK or US master's can still pay off by giving access (a second recruiting cycle, a target-school brand), just not a higher title.

**H6 (own). "The MBB pay premium over the Big 4 differs sharply by country, and is largest in France."**
Verdict: **partly supported (single source family; the France 2x does not survive a like-for-like check)**.
- Evidence: like-for-like, about 1.4–1.8x in the UK, 1.4–1.6x in France, 1.2–1.5x in Germany and 1.05–1.25x in the US (our calculation from PrepLounge, Management Consulted, FT via eFinancialCareers and Planete Grandes Ecoles, 2 Oct 2026). Only the US gap is clearly the smallest.
- Caveat: one source family (PrepLounge UK and Management Consulted share data). The French Big 4 figures may be for general advisory rather than strategy arms (e.g. Strategy&, Monitor Deloitte), which pay more.

**H7 (own). "Tier 2 strategy pays close to MBB in Germany."**
Verdict: **supported (single source)**. Kearney at €80k base matches or beats BCG and Bain at €75k; Roland Berger is at €72k. Oliver Wyman (€60.5k) is the exception (PrepLounge DACH, Dec 2025) [practitioner consensus]. Tier-2 bonuses are lower, so total-comp gaps are larger than base gaps.

**H8 (own). "An English-only EU MiM graduate from a DACH school can get into DACH MBB."**
Verdict: **not supported**.
- Against: McKinsey Germany/Austria treats fluent German as mandatory (e-fellows, Aug 2025) [employer-stated via partner]. BCG runs a German-language case in round 2 and limits some programmes to German-speaking students (BCG, 2026) [employer-stated].
- Where it may fail: specialist tracks (e.g. data or AI engineering) and non-DACH offices of the same firms. Not tested here.
- Implication: an English-only graduate of St. Gallen, Mannheim or WU should aim for London, Amsterdam, Dubai or the home-country office, or start German to C1 during the MiM.

**H9 (own). "The four-day client-site week is a US habit; European consultants mostly work from the office."**
Verdict: **not supported for Roland Berger; untested for MBB**. Roland Berger states that consultants generally work "four days on-site" and Friday in the office, with over 75% of work cross-border (Roland Berger FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated]. No MBB Europe statement was fetched.

**H10 (own). "AI is changing how juniors are selected before it changes how many are hired."**
Verdict: **partly supported**. McKinsey's Lilli-in-interview pilot (CFO.com/FT, Jan 2026) and its liberal-arts recruiting push come alongside stable or growing intake statements [practitioner consensus / employer-stated]. It is too early to know whether the pilot becomes standard.

## Common mistakes and myths

1. **"My master's will get me a higher level everywhere."** True in Germany (Fellow vs Junior Fellow), false at UK/US MBB, where undergrads and master's graduates share one band (e-fellows 2026 [employer-stated via partner]; P&Q/MC 2025 [practitioner consensus]).
2. **"Big 4 consulting is a safe fallback."** Graduate intake is being cut at PwC UK, with bigger cuts under discussion in the US (Consultancy.uk, Sept 2025) [employer-stated]. Apply broadly across the Big 4 and tier 2, and do not count on one Big 4 scheme.
3. **"MBB is in decline because of AI."** Firm statements say the opposite for consultants in 2026 (McKinsey, BCG), even as support roles are cut [employer-stated]. The real change is flat pay and a smaller pyramid than at the 2022 peak.
4. **"levels.fyi or Glassdoor tells me what I'll be offered."** These are self-reported, mix levels and give no per-city sample size (levels.fyi shows the same global count of 904 on each city page) [anecdotal]. Use them for ratios, not offers.
5. **"Internships are optional extras."** In DACH, the Visiting Associate internship is the Associate job, and the binding full-time offer comes through it (BCG careers, 2026) [employer-stated].
6. **"Tier 2 is a big financial step down."** Not in Germany: Kearney and Roland Berger base pay sits close to MBB (PrepLounge DACH) [practitioner consensus].
7. **"BCG accepts English applications, so German isn't needed."** The form may be in English, but round 2 includes a case in German, and McKinsey calls fluent German mandatory (BCG 2026; e-fellows Aug 2025) [employer-stated].
8. **"Only my university grades matter."** McKinsey Germany asks for transcripts starting with the Abitur (school-leaving certificate) (e-fellows, Aug 2025) [employer-stated via partner].
9. **"If I miss the internship, I've missed MBB."** BCG says the door stays open without an internship (BCG FAQ, 2026) [employer-stated]. The internship is the easiest route, not the only one.

## Decision rules

1. If you want MBB in Germany or Austria and hold only a bachelor's, prefer a DACH MiM with an internship semester over a one-year UK MSc (because the German master's lifts you from Junior Fellow to Fellow, and the internship is the conversion route).
2. If you target London MBB, do not pay for a master's for the title alone (because UK/US MBB put undergrads and master's graduates on one band). Choose the master's for school access and a second recruiting cycle.
3. If you are choosing between a Paris MBB offer and a Paris Big 4 offer, weight the pay gap at about 1.4–1.6x base to base (up to about 2x only if you compare MBB maximum totals with Big 4 base), against about 1.2–1.5x in Germany.
4. If you hold a German tier-2 offer (Kearney, Roland Berger, Simon-Kucher) and a Big 4 or Accenture offer, prefer tier 2 on pay (base €68–80k vs €50–58k) and on exit signalling.
5. If your only offer is a Big 4 graduate scheme tied to deals or transaction services, keep interviewing until your start date (because intake at these arms tracks deal volume and has been cut).
6. If you have a quantitative or technical background (engineering, data science), apply to MBB's technical and AI tracks as well as the generalist track (because BCG's 2025 hiring growth went mainly to AI engineers and data scientists).
7. If you are preparing for McKinsey in 2026–27, practise cases where you use an AI tool and must check its output (because McKinsey is piloting Lilli-assisted interviews).
8. If you study on a French or DACH programme with a gap year or a mandatory internship, use it for an MBB/tier-2 internship of 8–12 weeks (because BCG explicitly accepts gap-year and mandatory internships up to 12 weeks).
9. If you compare salary offers across cities, use aggregator ratios, not absolute figures, and check against a firm source or an offer letter.
10. If you want a German or Austrian MBB office and your German is below C1, prefer a MiM where you can reach C1 before the internship year, or target London, Amsterdam or your home-country office instead (because McKinsey says fluent German is mandatory and BCG tests it in a live case).
11. If you are an Italian (or other non-German) native speaker choosing between offices, weight your home-country office higher than its size suggests (because Roland Berger requires the local office language and McKinsey and BCG enforce German in DACH, which narrows the field of competitors; whether MBB Milan or Paris enforce the local language as strictly is unverified).
12. If you win a BCG FAST FORWARD offer, use the deferral option to finish your MiM or take a gap-year internship elsewhere (because the offer holds without further interviews and the start date can be postponed).
13. If you are considering Dubai or Riyadh as your main route, verify nationality and language requirements with the office's recruiters before committing (because no primary evidence confirms openness to European juniors).
