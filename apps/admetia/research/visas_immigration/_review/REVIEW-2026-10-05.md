### The two weakest guides (Italy, France)

They were the first two runs and have no "Red Flag" section; Portugal, Spain, Iceland and Belgium do.

France, not in the guide:
- **F-01** EU citizens: art. L233-1 sufficiency rules and the OQTF risk after 3 months.
- **F-02** CEAM/EHIC is enough only for a student who does not work. Any paid job or stage means French social security and a NIR. The guide says the opposite for EU students (section 4.2), and says the DPAE assigns a "codice fiscale/previdenziale".
- **F-04** Visa-free passports (UK/US/CA) cannot convert a 90-day entry into a student or work permit. The word "conversione" appears only for the working-holiday visa.
- **F-10** Translations by a French sworn translator, apostille and the multilingual birth certificate.
- Also absent: the €180 late-filing penalty (confirmed on service-public F2231, see section 2) and the rule that RECE must be requested before the student permit expires.

Italy, not in the guide:
- **Falla 1** Visa-free nationals cannot convert an entry into a study permit.
- §4 gives no limit on the postal receipt, although `italy_open_questions.md` (Q3) records the 9-month expiry printed on the 22AO receipt since Nov 2024. The guide's section 4 therefore reads as if the receipt has unlimited validity.
- The Reality items "permit issued already expired" and PrenotaFacile.
