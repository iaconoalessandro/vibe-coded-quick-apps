# Italy pay addendum

Compiled 9 October 2026. Fills the "no reliable data found" gaps on Italian pay across the 13 career-branch reports. Every figure is dated and sourced; where it rests on a weak source, the row says so.

## 1. How to read Italian pay

**RAL (retribuzione annua lorda)** is gross annual salary before income tax and employee social contributions. It is the figure Italian job ads and recruiter guides quote. It normally includes the 13th month (and the 14th where the contract has one) but excludes bonus unless stated. Always compare RAL with RAL, never with a US/UK "total compensation" number.

**Net pay (netto)** is what reaches the bank account. At typical graduate levels, roughly 75-80% of RAL survives at about EUR 30k, roughly 68-71% at EUR 40k, and about 64% at EUR 50k. These ratios come from calculator estimates for 2026 (13 monthly payments, employee INPS contribution 9.19%, IRPEF brackets 23% up to 28k, 33% to 50k, 43% above, plus local surcharges that vary by town): Gi Group puts EUR 30k RAL at about EUR 1,730-1,770 net per month, and QualcheRisposta gives EUR 1,882 (30k), EUR 2,103 (40k) and EUR 2,478 (50k). Treat these as approximate; the sources differ by up to about EUR 150 per month at 30k.

**13th/14th month (tredicesima, quattordicesima).** The 13th is paid in December by law. The 14th (usually July) exists only in some contracts, notably Commercio/Terziario. A "RAL of 36k" can be 12, 13 or 14 payments, so the monthly net differs.

**CCNL (contratto collettivo nazionale di lavoro)** is the national collective agreement for an industry. It sets minimum pay by level (livello/area), the number of monthly payments, seniority steps (scatti di anzianità) and leave rules. Companies can and often do pay above the minimum ("superminimo"). Three examples:
- **Credito/ABI (banks).** Under the November 2023 renewal, the minimum for the 3rd professional area, 1st level reaches EUR 2,742.34 gross per month from 1 March 2026 (FirstCisl table via QuiFinanza). Over 13 payments that is about EUR 35.65k a year, before seniority steps and bank-specific pay. This matches the bottom of a Deutsche Bank Milan M&A analyst ad (EUR 35,650-45,000, June 2026) that cites the credit CCNL, so a bank's entry salary can simply be the contract minimum. The contract expired on 31 March 2026; unions asked for about EUR 518 per month more and a 35-hour week, and I did not confirm an outcome.
- **Metalmeccanico (engineering industry, Federmeccanica).** Minimum gross monthly pay from 1 June 2026: D1 EUR 1,784.94, D2 EUR 1,979.37, C3 EUR 2,211.43 (13 payments, so about EUR 23.2k, 25.7k and 28.7k a year before superminimi and bonuses).
- **Commercio/Terziario (Confcommercio).** 14 payments, levels from 7 up to Quadri. The tables I found conflict with each other on amounts and dates, so I quote no figure here; use the contract text.

**Variable pay.** Bonus is usually "MBO" (management by objectives) or "premio di risultato", expressed as a percentage of RAL. Recruiter guides give bonus ranges separately from RAL; I note them where useful. Banks and finance firms pay far more of their total package as bonus than industrial firms.

**Benefits and welfare aziendale.** Italian employers add meal vouchers, supplementary health insurance, pension contributions and tax-advantaged "flexible benefits". These are not in RAL and are worth a few hundred to a few thousand euro a year. The tax rules on them change often, so check the offer letter.

**Milan premium.** JobPricing's Salary Outlook 2026 (data for 2025) gives average private-sector RAL of EUR 32,991 nationally, EUR 35,137 in Lombardy (first among regions), EUR 34,119 in the North, EUR 32,746 in the Centre and EUR 29,777 in the South and Islands. For tech and product roles, Levels.fyi's self-reported data shows a bigger Milan gap (software engineer median EUR 50.5k in Milan vs EUR 39.3k for Italy overall; product manager EUR 62.7k vs EUR 51.2k), but with small samples.

**Why Italian graduates compare with Milan offices of foreign firms.** Foreign banks, consultancies and tech firms in Milan set pay against group or European grids, work in English and offer a path to London, Paris or Frankfurt, while many Italian employers pay close to contract minimums. AlmaLaurea finds that, all else equal, Italian master's graduates working abroad earn about EUR 673 net more per month than those in the South (see section 3). Recruiter guides such as Michael Page do not split by city, so national ranges blur the Milan/foreign-firm premium.

**Scope notes:** All figures are gross RAL in EUR thousands (k), base pay unless a bonus is stated. "Entry" is the recruiter's under-5-years band, "~5 years" is the 5-10 years band, and "Senior" is over 10 years; Michael Page bands are wide, so the low end suits 0-2 years and the high end 4-5 years. Confidence: **high** = official contract table, gazette or public-sector notice; **medium** = recruiter survey or a real job ad; **low** = aggregator, forum, undated page or a proxy role. Where no source exists I write "no reliable data found". Currency amounts of other countries are not converted.

## 3. Graduate outcome benchmarks

**AlmaLaurea Rapporto 2026** (XXVIII survey of outcomes, presented 11 June 2026; interviews in 2025 with graduates of 2024 at one year and graduates of 2020 at five years). Net monthly pay:

| Level | 1 year | 3 years | 5 years |
|---|---|---|---|
| First-level (bachelor's) | EUR 1,491 | EUR 1,684 | EUR 1,796 |
| Second-level (master's) | EUR 1,495 | EUR 1,695 | EUR 1,903 |

- Real (inflation-adjusted) entry pay fell slightly: about -1.4% for first-level and -0.9% for second-level graduates (as reported by PMI.it).
- Master's graduates working in Italy earned EUR 1,452 at one year against EUR 2,290 abroad, and EUR 1,840 at five years against EUR 2,941 abroad (Open, 12 June 2026). Living costs abroad are higher.
- By degree group, AlmaLaurea's regression (equal conditions, one year after graduation) says, against the politics/social and communication group: medical-pharmaceutical +EUR 310 net per month, industrial and information engineering +256, computing and ICT +222, economics +128, science +105, law -117. Working in the North pays +68 and the Centre +50 versus the South; working abroad +673. A job in the credit sector adds +287 compared with the social-services branch, and ICT sector jobs add +184.
- Group averages for master's graduates one year out (survey 2024, as summarised by Il Sole 24 Ore Infodata on 24 May 2026 from AlmaLaurea): industrial and information engineering EUR 1,783; computing and ICT EUR 1,777; medical-pharmaceutical EUR 1,660. I did not find a clean 2026 figure for the economics/statistics group (the AlmaLaurea page lists "economico" without a number in the text I could read). For first-level graduates in the same survey: engineering EUR 1,703, ICT EUR 1,653.
- The 2026 report also gives five-year employment rates of about 96% (engineering), 94.8% (ICT) and 91.6% (economics/statistics) according to a secondary site (tesify.it); these are not pay.

**JobPricing (Salary Outlook 2026, University Report July 2026).** Average RAL of graduates across all ages is EUR 42,378, versus EUR 30,999 for those with only a diploma; bachelor's EUR 34,640, master's EUR 45,877. Average RAL for all private-sector workers in 2025 is EUR 32,991.

**ISTAT.** One summary cites an average gross annual pay of EUR 31,856 for 2025 (secondary source; I did not open ISTAT's own table).

**How these compare with the role tables.** A master's graduate five years out earns EUR 1,903 net a month; at 13 payments that is about EUR 24.7k net, or very roughly EUR 31-33k RAL at the typical net share. That sits at the low-to-middle end of the "<5 years" bands for finance, data and software roles (35-55k), and well below the MBB and bank-M&A ranges. The gap has three likely causes: AlmaLaurea is an average across all degrees and employers, including public sector, part-time and small firms; recruiter guides sample roles placed through recruiters, which skew to Milan, large firms and multinationals; and MP26 bands are for under 5 years of experience, so the upper end is not reachable at 1-2 years. Bocconi and Polimi graduates (JobPricing EUR 41.4k and 38.2k average starting RAL) sit near the middle of the MP26 entry bands. In short: use the MP26 low end as a realistic starting figure outside Milan and the high end as a Milan, multinational or specialist-skills outcome.
