Finance is the work of moving, pricing, investing and protecting money for companies, governments, institutions and individuals. It splits into four worlds that feel very different from the inside: the **sell side** (investment banks that advise on deals, raise capital and make markets, paid through fees and trading spreads), the **buy side** (asset managers, hedge funds, private equity, venture capital and private credit funds that invest money and are paid management and performance fees), **corporate and risk finance** (finance teams inside companies, risk and compliance at financial institutions, paid a salary to plan, control and protect), and **quant and fintech** (mathematicians, programmers and technology firms that model markets or rebuild financial services as software). Pay at the top of finance is among the highest any graduate can reach, but it is concentrated in a few very selective, long-hours seats; most finance jobs (FP&A, risk, compliance, wealth management, corporate banking) are normal-hours, well-paid professional careers with far easier entry. Region matters more than in most branches: New York and London pay and recruit very differently from Milan, Hong Kong, Singapore, Tokyo or Dubai, and the reports below say where.

Scope notes: this branch was researched in four parts (investment banking and advisory; sales & trading, markets and research; the buy side; corporate finance, risk, quant and fintech). Accounting, management consulting and economics careers have their own branch reports.
