### (a) Ratings by background (whole branch)

Overall ratings combine the four parts; the matrix in (b) shows where a background is strong even if its overall rating is lower.

| Background | Overall fit | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Management | possible | Strong for corporate banking, distribution, private banking and fintech business roles; a stretch for modelling-heavy and quant seats without extra finance training. |
| Logistics & Supply Chain | stretch | Little overlap with banking or investing, with real exceptions: physical commodity trading houses (strong), trade finance, treasury and trade-sanctions compliance. |
| Finance | strong | The default feeder for almost every role family; quant research and quant development need extra maths and programming. |
| Accounting | strong | Standard route into transaction services, restructuring, equity and credit research, FP&A, treasury and compliance; weak for trading and quant. |
| Marketing | stretch | Realistic only for institutional sales, asset-management distribution, private banking or fintech growth roles, and usually with added finance proof. |
| Data Analytics | possible | SQL and analytics fit risk, financial crime, fintech and Big 4 deals; markets and investing seats need finance knowledge on top. |
| Economics | strong | Fits advisory, rates/FX and macro trading, research, allocators, FP&A and risk; a bridge to quant only with a strong mathematical track. |
| Computer Science | possible | Strong for quant developers, quant research at trading firms, electronic trading and fintech; fundamental banking and investing need finance study. |
| Cybersecurity | stretch | Front-office fit is minimal; realistic doors are technology and operational risk, fraud, and cyber-focused VC. |
| Data Science | possible | Strong for quant, risk modelling, structuring and commodity analytics; IBD and fundamental investing need accounting and valuation skills. |
| Artificial Intelligence | possible | Strong for quant research, bank quant and fintech models; most advisory and fundamental investing seats rate it a stretch. |
