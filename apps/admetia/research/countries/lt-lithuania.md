# Lithuania: country brief

Today is 3 October 2026. Tags: `[data]` official statistics or government pages, `[employer-stated]`, `[practitioner consensus]` (published indices), `[anecdotal]`.

## 1. Bottom line

- Vilnius is the graduate market. Eurostat counts 33,980 of Lithuania's 50,860 information-and-communication workers (67%) and 22,380 of its 30,530 finance-and-insurance workers (73%) in the Vilnius metropolitan region in 2021 [data]. Kaunas is a distant second: 9,890 ICT and 2,560 finance workers [data].
- The specialities are fintech and business-service centres. At the end of 2025 there were 248 licensed fintech companies with about 7,800 employees, 231 of them in Vilnius, and Lithuania ranks first in the EU by fintech licences issued (Invest Lithuania) [data]. Business-service centres employ more than 27,000 people in 100 companies, including Danske Bank, Nasdaq, Western Union, SEB and Moody's (Invest Lithuania, 2025) [data].
- Tech and finance pay well above the national mean: in 2025 the average gross monthly wage was €2,411 nationally, €4,168 in information and communication and €3,848 in finance; in Vilnius County €4,359 and €3,997 (Statistics Lithuania) [data].
- In standing: Vilnius is 5 nationally in finance, software and IT, 3 in Europe for finance (on its first place for fintech licences, a cross-country statistic) and 2 in Europe for software and IT; world scale is 1 in each. The Global Financial Centres Index 40 lists Vilnius only as an associate centre (too few assessments for a rank) [practitioner consensus], and Startup Genome values its ecosystem at $8 billion against a European average of $14.3 billion [practitioner consensus].
- Graduates find work: 92.7% of recent tertiary graduates aged 20-34 were employed in 2025 (EU 85.3%), though unemployment among 15-to-24-year-olds is 14.1% (Eurostat) [data].
- For non-EU students: 12 months after graduating to look for work; Bachelor 1st-2nd year work is capped at 20h/week while Master and PhD can work 40h/week under the 22 May 2026 amendments (see full rules in visas_immigration/lithuania/lithuania_visas_immigration_guide.md).
- For a European master's student: Vilnius for fintech, payments, shared-service finance and tech product roles; Kaunas for engineering and software through Kaunas University of Technology and Nord Security's Kaunas office; no deep corporate-finance or consulting market.

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size.** The population of the Vilnius metropolitan region was 848,724 and of Kaunas 579,903 in 2023; the Vilnius region's GDP was €24.4 billion in 2021 and Kaunas's €11.7 billion (Eurostat) [data]. Lithuania's overall unemployment rate was 6.9% in 2025 (Eurostat) [data].

**Structure.** ICT specialists were 5.7% of employment in 2025 (83,100 people), against 5.0% in the EU (Eurostat) [data]. The two clusters above (fintech, shared services) are the employers that hire in English.

**Graduates and youth.** 60.8% of 25-to-34-year-olds hold a tertiary degree (EU 44.8%); recent graduates' employment rate is 92.7%; youth unemployment 14.1% (2023: 13.8%) (Eurostat, 2025) [data].

**Pay.** County averages above; city averages for the second quarter of 2026 were €2,963 in Vilnius city and €2,734 in Kaunas city against €2,616 for the country (Statistics Lithuania, S3R0050) [data]. These are averages, not entry salaries; no entry-pay survey was read (gap).

**Hiring and language.** No graduate calendars were read. Recruiting through shared-service centres is typically in English, but no source was read to confirm language requirements (gap).

## 3. Hubs

### Vilnius

**What it is.** The capital: 848,724 residents in the metropolitan region (2023), €24.4 billion GDP (2021), average gross pay €2,963 a month in the city (Q2 2026) (Eurostat; Statistics Lithuania) [data]. Rent for a one-bedroom flat in the centre is not shown (the crowd-sourced source refused access on the day).

**Sectors and named employers.** Fintech and payments (231 of 248 licensed companies are in Vilnius) [data]; business-service centres (Danske Bank, Nasdaq, Western Union, SEB, Moody's) [data]; Swedbank, whose address is Konstitucijos pr. 20A [employer-stated]; Vinted, which says more than 2,000 people work from its Lithuanian headquarters and offices across Europe [employer-stated]; Nord Security, with its headquarters in Vilnius [employer-stated].

**Universities.** Vilnius University is 465th in the QS World University Rankings 2027, the highest in Lithuania (university news, 18 Jun 2026) [employer-stated].

**Start-up ecosystem.** Startup Genome 2026: ecosystem value $8 billion (European average $14.3 billion); the StartupBlink 2026 index puts Lithuania 22nd in the world, ten places behind Estonia (Invest in Estonia, May 2026) [practitioner consensus, via a state agency].

### Kaunas

**What it is.** Lithuania's second city: 579,903 residents in the metropolitan region, GDP €11.7 billion, average gross pay €2,734 in the city (Q2 2026) [data].

**Sector and employers.** 9,890 ICT and 2,560 finance workers in 2021 [data]. Kaunas University of Technology is described by Invest Lithuania as the country's largest provider of engineers, and 36% of the city's 50,000 students study engineering, manufacturing or construction; the free economic zone is described as a hub of logistics and advanced manufacturing [employer-stated]. Nord Security lists a Kaunas office [employer-stated].

**Standing:** IT 3 / 1 / 1. National 3: second in the country by ICT jobs; the GFCI lists Kaunas as an associate centre with 15 assessments.

## 5. Hypotheses tested

1. **"Lithuania is Europe's fintech capital."** Partly supported: it ranks first in the EU by licences issued, but the Global Financial Centres Index does not rank Vilnius (associate centre), and the sector is about 7,800 employees (Invest Lithuania) [data, practitioner consensus]. Holds as a licensing centre; fails as a deep finance market.
2. **"Shared-service centres are a way in for graduates."** Supported on size (27,000 employees, 36% in tech roles), insufficient on entry terms: no graduate scheme or pay was read.
3. **"Vilnius is enough; Kaunas is irrelevant."** Mostly supported for finance (2,560 against 22,380 jobs) but not for engineering and software, where Kaunas has 9,890 ICT jobs, the leading technical university and a Nord Security office.

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

- **Fintech licences are not jobs.** 248 companies employ about 7,800 people.
- **2021 job counts are old.** Metropolitan employment is the latest Eurostat publishes, from 2021; treat the shares (67%, 73%) as indicative.
- **Average pay is not entry pay.** The ICT average of €4,168 includes senior staff.

## 7. Decision rules

1. If your target is payments, fintech compliance or product roles, prefer Vilnius.
2. If your target is engineering or embedded software, compare Kaunas with Vilnius: Kaunas has the main engineering university and an FEZ.
3. If you want a shared-service finance job, look at the named centres (Danske Bank, SEB, Western Union, Nasdaq, Moody's) rather than the open market.
4. If you are non-EU, plan for 12 months after graduating, noting that Bachelor 1st-2nd year students work max 20h/week while Master and PhD students work full-time 40h/week (see visas_immigration/lithuania/lithuania_visas_immigration_guide.md).
5. If you want pay data, use the sector and county averages as ceilings, not as offers.
