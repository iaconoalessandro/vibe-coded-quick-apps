# Romania: country brief

Today is 3 October 2026. Tags: `[data]` official statistics or government pages, `[employer-stated]`, `[practitioner consensus]` (published indices and trade-body reports), `[anecdotal]`.

## 1. Bottom line

- Bucharest is Romania's office economy. In 2021 the Bucharest metropolitan region held 108,840 information-and-communication jobs (56% of the country's 194,700) and 36,080 finance-and-insurance jobs (35% of 102,600) (Eurostat) [data]. No other Romanian metropolitan region has more than 17,390 ICT jobs or 8,120 finance jobs.
- Cluj-Napoca is second in both counts (17,390 ICT and 8,120 finance jobs), and Iași is third for ICT (16,810) (Eurostat, 2021) [data]. Cluj-Napoca is also where Banca Transilvania, the largest Romanian bank by assets, has its registered address, with 10,180 employees at the end of 2025 [employer-stated].
- Banking is a Bucharest career: Banca Comercială Română (4,809 employees at the end of 2025), BRD-Groupe Société Générale (4,965) and the National Bank of Romania have their seats there [employer-stated].
- Technology is real but narrow: ICT specialists were 2.7% of employment in 2025, the second-lowest share in the EU after Greece (EU 5.0%) (Eurostat) [data]. Named technology employers are Bolt (a key hub), Ubisoft Bucharest (the second largest Ubisoft studio worldwide) and Bosch (Cluj research centre, Timișoara technology centre) [employer-stated], and Endava in Iași, Cluj-Napoca and Timișoara [trade press].
- Pay: a junior specialist in a service centre earned a mean base of €1,625 a month in 2024, but a single worker keeps only 58.5% of gross at the average wage (ABSL citing Mercer; Eurostat, via the library) [data]. County wages could not be read this round.
- Standing: Bucharest is 5 nationally in finance, IT and software; 3 in Europe for IT (seventh of 25 capital regions for ICT jobs) and 2 for finance (fifteenth) and software, and 1 in the world. Bucharest is not among the 117 centres of the Global Financial Centres Index 40 [data, practitioner consensus].
- For a European master's student, Romania is a market for service-centre, bank and software jobs in Bucharest or Cluj-Napoca, where the saving comes from rent rather than pay, and where graduate unemployment is high (26.1% among 15-to-24-year-olds in 2025).

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size.** 19.04 million people in 2025 and GDP of €380.1 billion in 2025 (Eurostat nama_10_gdp and demo_pjan) [data]. Unemployment was 6.1% in 2025, close to the EU's 6.0% (Eurostat une_rt_a) [data].

**Structure.** More than 280,000 people work in business services; finance and accounting and IT services are each offered by 69% of the 70 companies surveyed, human resources by 61% and customer support by 57% (ABSL Romania with PwC, 2025 report) [data]. ICT specialists were 2.7% of employment in 2025 (207,800 people) (Eurostat) [data].

**Graduates and youth.** In 2025, 81.1% of tertiary graduates aged 20-34 who finished within three years were in work against 85.3% in the EU; unemployment among 15-to-24-year-olds was 26.1% (21.8% in 2023; EU 15.2%); 23.0% of 25-to-34-year-olds had a tertiary degree against 44.8% in the EU (Eurostat) [data].

**Entry pay.** No Romanian graduate pay survey by field was read. The Atlas holds the Mercer 2024 junior-specialist base of €1,625 a month in service centres (ABSL 2025, via the library) [data]. Net-to-gross at the average wage is 58.5% (Eurostat 2025, via the library) against 71.7% in Poland; the library's Numbeo reading of a central Bucharest one-bedroom leaves about €340 a month after rent [practitioner consensus, library]. I could not re-read rents on 3 October (Numbeo refused access).

**Language of work.** Not researched this round; ABSL's Romanian report was read only for its sector totals.

**How hiring works.** Not researched: no graduate programme calendars were read (gap).

**Permits.** Full official guide and single source of truth: `visas_immigration/romania/romania_visas_immigration_guide.md`. EU citizens register with the immigration office in their county after three months; non-EU students may work 6 hours a day without a permit (Law 28/2024) and graduates can extend their permit by 9 months to look for work (OUG 194/2002 art. 58(4)). EU Blue Card threshold is 9,192 RON gross/month (1.0x average gross salary, Law 44/2026 and Law 28/2024); statutory minimum wage is 4,325 RON gross/month (HG 146/2026). Sourced in `visas_immigration/romania/romania_visas_immigration_guide.md`.

## 3. Hubs

### Bucharest

**What it is.** 2,290,125 residents in the metropolitan region (2023) and GDP of €67.6 billion (2021) (Eurostat) [data]. Wage by county: not read (the National Institute of Statistics site answers automated requests with a browser check, which I did not bypass). Rent: not read.

**Sectors and named employers.**
- Banking: Banca Comercială Română (registered office at 15D Orhideelor avenue; 4,809 employees at 31 December 2025), BRD-Groupe Société Générale (Bld Ion Mihalache 1-7; 4,965 employees at the end of 2025) and the National Bank of Romania (Strada Lipscani 25) [employer-stated].
- Software and technology: Bolt (named among four key hubs around its Tallinn headquarters) [employer-stated]; Ubisoft Bucharest (Jiului 8 street, a studio since 1992 and, in Ubisoft's words, the second largest worldwide) [employer-stated]; Bosch (Romanian headquarters in Bucharest since 1994, about 9,900 staff in Romania) [employer-stated].
- Business services: ABSL's national totals; no Bucharest-only count was read.

**Start-ups.** Startup Genome's 2026 report values the Bucharest ecosystem at $2 billion, with $123 million of early-stage funding in H2 2023-2025, against a European average of $14.3 billion [practitioner consensus].

### Cluj-Napoca

**What it is.** 688,930 residents in the metropolitan region (2023), GDP €12.5 billion (2021) (Eurostat) [data].

**Sectors and employers.** Information and communication 17,390 jobs and finance and insurance 8,120, both second in the country (Eurostat, 2021) [data]. Banca Transilvania (registered address 30-36 Calea Dorobanților; 10,180 bank employees and 13,361 in the group at 31 December 2025) [employer-stated]. Bosch runs a research and development centre and an automotive production unit in Cluj [employer-stated]. Evozon describes itself as a software agency located in Cluj-Napoca [employer-stated]; Endava has an office there [trade press].

**Standing:** IT 4 / 1 / 1, software 4 / 1 / 1, finance 4 / 1 / 1. National 4 because second in the country; no wider because the counts are small beside Bucharest's.

### Iași

**What it is.** 774,025 residents in the metropolitan region (2023), GDP €8.4 billion (2021) (Eurostat) [data].

**Sectors and employers.** Information and communication 16,810 jobs, third in the country; finance 3,270 (Eurostat, 2021) [data]. A trade-press report of 1 September 2025 says Endava has grown in Iași from 25 to over 700 specialists since 2010 [employer-stated, via press]; another of 13 November 2025 says VOIS Romania opened a new office there and plans to hire 150 people by the end of 2026 [employer-stated, via press].

**Standing:** IT 3 / 1 / 1. Software is rated *present* on Endava alone.

### Timișoara

**What it is.** 658,607 residents in the metropolitan region (2023), GDP €11.1 billion (2021) (Eurostat) [data]. A western manufacturing city.

**Sectors and employers.** Information and communication 8,460 jobs and finance and insurance 3,340 (Eurostat, 2021) [data]. Bosch has a centre for business and technology solutions in Timișoara [employer-stated]; Endava has an office there [trade press].

**Standing:** IT 3 / 1 / 1; IT and software are rated *present*.

## 5. Hypotheses tested

1. **"Romania is a large IT nation."** Not supported by the national share: 2.7% of employment, second-lowest in the EU, though Bucharest holds 56% of ICT jobs and is seventh among 25 capital regions [data].
2. **"Cluj-Napoca is the second capital of Romanian tech."** Partly supported: second in ICT and finance jobs, but its 17,390 ICT jobs are about one-sixth of Bucharest's; Iași is close behind with 16,810 [data].
3. **"A Romanian service-centre job is cheap to live on."** Partly supported: gross pay of €1,625 looks close to Poland's €1,744, but only 58.5% is kept at the average wage, against 71.7% [data, via the library].

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

- **Romania's banks are not all in Bucharest.** The largest, Banca Transilvania, has its registered address in Cluj-Napoca, though its report also lists a head office in Bucharest.
- **Metropolitan job counts date from 2021.** Treat the shares as indicative.
- **Trade-press employer figures are not audited.** The Endava and VOIS items rest on press reports quoting the companies.

## 7. Decision rules

1. If you want banking or finance in Romania, start with Bucharest (BCR, BRD, the National Bank) and add Banca Transilvania in Cluj-Napoca.
2. If you want software, compare Bucharest (Ubisoft, Bolt) with Cluj-Napoca (Bosch, Evozon, Endava) and check each firm's careers page for your city.
3. If you compare a Romanian and a Polish offer, compare net pay, not gross.
4. If you want investment banking or capital markets, do not choose Romania for entry.
5. If you are non-EU, use the nine-month permit after graduating, and have offers lined up.
