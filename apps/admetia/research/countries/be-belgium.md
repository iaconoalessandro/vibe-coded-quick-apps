# Belgium: country brief

Today is 3 October 2026. Tags: `[data]` official statistics or government pages, `[employer-stated]`, `[practitioner consensus]` (published indices and specialist synthesis), `[anecdotal]`.

## 1. Bottom line

- Belgium is a one-city country for white-collar graduate jobs. Brussels has 725,600 jobs in its arrondissement (Eurostat, 2023), 48,900 of them in finance and insurance and 33,800 in information and communication; Antwerp, the next city, has 12,600 and 18,500 [data].
- Brussels is first in Belgium (5 of 5) in finance and IT but a mid-sized centre in Europe (2 of 5): the Global Financial Centres Index 40 (September 2026) ranks it 82nd in the world and outside the top 15 Western European centres [practitioner consensus]. It is where the EU institutions are (the European Commission has about 33,000 staff [employer-stated]), not where the banks are.
- Antwerp is the one place where Belgium is a European top-five: Antwerp-Bruges handled 244.2 million tonnes in 2024, second in the EU after Rotterdam (Eurostat) [data]; the port authority counts about 164,000 direct and indirect jobs and over 1,400 companies [employer-stated]. Go there for logistics, port and chemicals roles (BASF's Antwerp site is its second largest in the world).
- Ghent is a third city for finance and IT jobs (6,000 and 12,000 in the metropolitan region, third in Belgium for both [data]); Leuven is a university and chip-research town (KU Leuven, imec); Liège is Wallonia's largest region and has a cargo airport, but no family is rated beyond logistics.
- Entry pay is good for the euro area but not exceptional: employees under 30 in firms with 10 or more staff earned a mean of €38,114 gross a year in 2022, and under-30 professionals €52,290 (Eurostat SES) [data]. Brussels pays about 17% more at the median than Antwerp province (SD Worx via VRT) [anecdotal].
- Graduates find work: 90.2% of Belgians aged 20 to 34 with a tertiary degree were employed in 2025, but 15-to-24 unemployment is 17.4% [data]. The language of the job (Dutch, French, German, English) matters more than the city; the language of work was not researched here.
- For a European master's student: Brussels for EU affairs, consulting and finance roles that use French or English; Antwerp for logistics and chemicals; Leuven for engineering and research. Non-EU graduates of Belgian institutions get up to 12 months to search (KU Leuven) [employer-stated].

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size.** Population 11,883,495 on 1 January 2025; GDP €642.0 billion in 2025; unemployment 6.2% overall and 17.4% among 15-to-24-year-olds (Eurostat demo_pjan, nama_10_gdp, une_rt_a) [data].

**Structure.** Three clusters matter. The first is the capital: EU institutions, NATO, lobbying, consulting and law, banks and the Belgian head offices (Proximus, for example, gives its address as Boulevard du Roi Albert 2, Brussels [employer-stated]). The second is the port economy around Antwerp and Ghent: chemicals, energy, logistics. The third is research and life sciences in Flanders (Leuven, Ghent). In Brussels 37% of jobs (266,500) are public administration, defence, education and health [data].

**Graduates.** In 2025 the employment rate of 20-to-34-year-olds with a tertiary degree was 90.2%, and 88.5% for those who finished within the last five years (Eurostat edat_lfse_24) [data]. Lower than the Netherlands (92.7%), but a graduate who looks finds a job; the question is which one.

**Entry pay.** Eurostat's Structure of Earnings Survey 2022 (firms with 10 or more staff, public administration excluded): mean annual gross earnings of €38,114 for all employees under 30 and €52,290 for under-30 professionals, against €53,642 for all ages (earn_ses22_28) [data]. Monthly medians by place (SD Worx payroll data of 400,000 full-time employees, reported by VRT NWS on 13 March 2026): Brussels €4,200; Antwerp province €3,605; East Flanders €3,595; Flemish Brabant €3,580 [anecdotal: one private payroll provider]. The 2022 figures are four years old.

**How hiring works.** The EU institutions recruit through EPSO: the 2026 graduate competition (AD5) drew 174,727 applicants for 1,490 places, about 117 per place, so traineeships and contract-agent posts are the realistic first doors (careers/public-policy-and-academia.md) [data]. No graduate-scheme calendar was found for Belgian banks or port employers.

**Language and permits.** Not researched beyond the record's route: EU students register at the commune with proof of enrolment and health insurance (City of Brussels, updated 10 Jun 2026); non-EEA students may work 20 hours a week in term (Study in Flanders); non-EEA graduates can stay up to 12 months to search (KU Leuven) [data, employer-stated].

## 3. Hubs

Population and GDP are Eurostat NUTS 3 regions, which for Belgium are arrondissements (`demo_r_pjangrp3`, population 1 January 2025; `nama_10r_3gdp`, GDP 2023), `area: region`. Pay is the SD Worx median for the region or province the city is in (`area: region`). Rent is Numbeo's average for a one-bedroom flat in the city centre, read through the Internet Archive's snapshots (crowd-sourced, `anecdotal`).

### Brussels

**What it is.** The capital region and the EU's seat: 1,271,709 residents in the arrondissement, GDP €103.6 billion (2023), median pay €4,200 a month, a one-bedroom flat about €1,209. Of 725,600 jobs, 158,000 are in professional, scientific, technical and administrative services [data].

**Employers.** European Commission (about 33,000 staff), the EU graduate competition (EPSO), Proximus (telecoms head office). Startup Genome's 2026 report values the ecosystem at $20 billion against a European average of $14.3 billion, with $800 million of seed and Series A funding in H2 2023 to 2025 [practitioner consensus].

**Standing.**

| Family | Steps | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Finance | 5 / 2 / 1 | National 5: ninth-most finance and insurance jobs of the 152 metropolitan regions in Eurostat's table (63,000), against 13,000 in Antwerp. Europe 2: GFCI 40 puts it 82nd in the world and outside the top 15 Western European centres; Luxembourg and Amsterdam rank above it. World 1. |
| IT | 5 / 2 / 1 | National 5: 68,000 information and communication jobs, 16th in the table, against 18,000 in Antwerp. Europe 2: the table omits German, British and Swiss regions, and although Startup Genome values Brussels above the European average ($20 billion against $14.3 billion), no ranking puts it among Europe's top five for IT. World 1. |

**Re-rating.** Finance and IT: not rated → strong, on Eurostat's job counts (be-bru-emp, be-rank).

### Antwerp

**What it is.** Port and chemicals: 1,101,687 residents in the arrondissement, GDP €66.8 billion (2023), median pay in the province €3,605, rent about €910. 514,700 jobs, of which 46,000 in manufacturing and 142,300 in professional and administrative services [data].

**Employers.** Port of Antwerp-Bruges (over 1,400 companies, around 164,000 direct and indirect jobs, €21 billion in added value [employer-stated]); BASF Antwerp, the largest chemical production site in Belgium and BASF's second largest in the world [employer-stated].

**Standing.** Logistics 5 / 4 / 2. National 5: the port authority calls the port Belgium's most important economic engine. Europe 4: second-busiest EU port (244.2 million tonnes, 2024) behind Rotterdam (Eurostat), so top five by a published statistic. World 2: no global port ranking was read. Logistics demand stays strong (be-port).

### Ghent

**What it is.** Flanders' university and port city: 583,606 residents, GDP €36.9 billion, median pay in East Flanders €3,595, rent about €1,020. 327,700 jobs: 95,300 in public administration, defence, education and health, 39,000 in manufacturing, 11,700 in information and communication, 5,500 in finance and insurance [data].

**Employers.** Ghent University (about 15,000 staff and 50,000 students, 2022-23; ranked 84 in ARWU and 115 in THE per its own fact sheet [employer-stated]); North Sea Port (105,912 direct and indirect jobs at the start of 2024 [practitioner consensus: trade press]).

**Standing.** IT 3 / 2 / 1 and finance 3 / 2 / 1: third in Belgium for both on Eurostat's metropolitan counts, behind Brussels and Antwerp, both small beside the European leaders. Demand strong for both (be-ghent, be-gnt-emp), rated on 2021-22 counts of at least 5,000 jobs.

### Liège

**What it is.** Wallonia's largest region: 637,220 residents, GDP €25.5 billion; 252,500 jobs, 91,200 in public administration, defence, education and health, 21,600 in manufacturing. No pay or rent figure (the SD Worx article gave none for Liège; Numbeo's archived page had no usable price).

**Employers.** Liège Airport handled 1,325,000 tons of cargo in 2025 and says 1.35 billion e-commerce packages passed through it [employer-stated].

**Standing.** Logistics 3 / 2 / 1; demand present, on the airport. Finance and IT are not rated: 3,800 and 5,200 jobs.

### Leuven

**What it is.** A university town and chip-research centre, added as a hub on 3 October 2026: 530,971 residents in the arrondissement, GDP €25.7 billion, median pay in Flemish Brabant €3,580, rent about €969. 206,100 jobs: 75,200 in public administration, defence, education and health, 52,800 in professional and administrative services, 14,000 in manufacturing, 7,000 in information and communication [data].

**Employers.** KU Leuven (66,306 students, 22,799 staff including the university hospital; 46th in THE 2026, 59th in QS 2027, 76th in ARWU 2025, as the university states [employer-stated]); imec, whose headquarters and biggest campus are in Leuven [employer-stated].

**Standing.** Computer science 3 / 2 / 1: a recognised centre on imec and KU Leuven's rankings, but no source compares European cities in computer-science hiring, so no 4. Demand present: both are research employers and entry hiring volume was not read.

## 5. Hypotheses tested

1. **Brussels is a finance centre because it is a capital.** Not supported. Brussels has the ninth-most finance and insurance jobs of 152 metropolitan regions, but GFCI 40 ranks it 82nd in the world, below Luxembourg and Amsterdam. Holds as a national centre; fails as a European one.
2. **Belgium's high youth unemployment (17.4%) means graduates struggle.** Partly supported. 90.2% of tertiary graduates aged 20 to 34 are in work, so the graduate rate is far better than the 17.4% headline for all 15-to-24s; recent graduates (88.5%) are slightly lower. The sources read do not split the headline further.
3. **Brussels pays more than Flanders.** Supported, on one provider: median €4,200 against €3,585 in Flanders [anecdotal]. Fails if the 2022 official figures (not split by city) are what you need.
4. **Antwerp is a top-five European logistics hub.** Supported by one statistic (second EU port by tonnage, 2024); not tested against Rotterdam's jobs or any ranking of port employers.

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

- Treating "Brussels" as one labour market: EU institutions recruit through competitions with about 117 applicants per place, while the private market is separate [data].
- Assuming Dutch or French is optional: language of work was not researched; do not assume English suffices.
- Reading one payroll provider's medians as official statistics: SD Worx covers full-time employees of its clients [anecdotal].

## 7. Decision rules

1. If you target EU institutions, public affairs or consulting around them, choose Brussels; apply for traineeships first (EPSO's graduate competition is about 117 to one).
2. If your family is finance or IT and you read French, Brussels is the Belgian answer; for a European finance career, rank it below Luxembourg, Amsterdam and Paris.
3. If your family is logistics, shipping or chemicals, choose Antwerp over every other Belgian city.
4. If you are an engineer or computer scientist, add Leuven (imec, KU Leuven) to a Brussels search.
5. If you are non-EU and study in Belgium, plan for the 12-month search year and the 20-hour work limit (full guide and verified rules in `visas_immigration/belgium/belgium_visas_immigration_guide.md`).
6. If pay decides, remember Brussels pays about 17% more at the median than Antwerp, but rent is about 33% higher too (Numbeo) [anecdotal].
