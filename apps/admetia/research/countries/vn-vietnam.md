# Vietnam: where a European business or computing graduate should look

Tags: `[data]` official statistics; `[employer-stated]` an employer's own report; `[practitioner consensus]` a published ranking or a specialist summary; `[anecdotal]` crowd-sourced. All pages read on 3 October 2026 (some on 4 October).

## 1. Bottom line

- **Hanoi hosts the biggest corporate and computing employers in the sources read.** FPT Corporation is headquartered at 10 Pham Van Bach Street and had 54,110 employees in more than 30 countries (Annual Report 2025) `[employer-stated]`; Techcombank's head office is Techcombank Tower, 6 Quang Trung Street, with 12,705 employees at 31 December 2025 (Annual Report 2025) `[employer-stated]`.
- **Ho Chi Minh City is the largest economy and the finance-ranking leader.** Its output was VND1,778,271 billion in 2024 (preliminary, pre-merger boundaries; National Statistics Office) `[data]`; the GFCI 40 ranks it 67th in the world, up 17 places, with Da Nang entering at 71st (Z/Yen, Sep 2026) `[practitioner consensus]`. Vinamilk's headquarters is in the city, with nearly 10,000 employees (Annual Report 2025) `[employer-stated]`.
- **Hai Phong is the northern port.** The port handled 2 million TEU for the first time in 2025 and over 104 million tonnes of cargo in the first 11 months, up about 8% (Hai Phong city portal) `[data]`; its economy grew 11.81% in 2025 (VietnamPlus, on NSO data) `[data]`. Logistics is the one family rated strong anywhere in the country.
- **Da Nang is a small, fast-growing centre**: GRDP up 9.18% in 2025 to an estimated VND316.1 trillion (VnEconomy, on city statistics) `[data]`; FPT describes a semiconductor R&D centre there `[employer-stated]`.
- **Work rights are easier for active student interns than for experts.** Under Decree 219/2025/ND-CP (in force since 7 August 2025), foreign students or trainees currently enrolled in studies with an internship agreement or job offer from a Vietnamese entity are exempt from the work permit upon formal DOLISA certification, while a foreign expert needs a university degree plus at least 2 years of relevant experience (1 year in technology, digital transformation and finance) `[data]`.
- **For a European the practical order is** Hanoi (corporate and tech) and Ho Chi Minh City (largest market) first, Hai Phong only for logistics, Da Nang for a smaller tech and quality-of-life option.

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size and structure.** Ho Chi Minh City's economy grew 8.03% in 2025; outstanding credit exceeded VND5 quadrillion (about US$190 billion), up more than 13%, foreign direct investment passed US$8.1 billion, up more than 21%, and the digital economy was about 25% of output (city People's Committee figures, Saigon Giai Phong, 6 Jan 2026) `[data]`. Hanoi's GRDP grew 8.16%, 16th among localities; Hai Phong's 11.81%, contributing 8.58% of national growth (NSO via VietnamPlus, 6 Jan 2026) `[data]`. Since 1 July 2025 the country has 34 provincial-level units: Ho Chi Minh City absorbed Binh Duong and Ba Ria-Vung Tau (a 2025 merger described by Vietnamese media; the merged population is reported at about 14 million, not read on an official page here).

**Entry pay.** NSO gives average monthly compensation of enterprise employees by city (above); no graduate salary survey was read.

**How hiring works, language.** Not researched.

**Visa and permit headline.** Citizens of 18 EU countries (including Italy, France, Germany and Spain) and the UK can visit for up to 45 days without a visa under Resolutions 44/NQ-CP and 229/NQ-CP, while 9 EU countries require a 90-day e-visa ($25 single, $50 multiple) via `evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn`. For comprehensive visa, work permit and residence regulations, see the single source of truth: `visas_immigration/vietnam/vietnam_visas_immigration_guide.md`.

## 3. Hubs

Metric method: **population, output and pay** are the National Statistics Office's "Socio-economic Statistical Data of 63 Provinces and Cities 2020-2024" (published November 2025), for each centrally run city **within its boundaries before the 1 July 2025 mergers** (`city`): population 2024 preliminary, GRDP at current prices 2024 preliminary, and average monthly compensation of employees in enterprises 2023 (the latest year in the book). **Rent** is left out (Numbeo HTTP 429). The merged units used by the growth claims are larger than these, notably Ho Chi Minh City, Hai Phong and Da Nang.

### Ho Chi Minh City

- **What it is.** 9,543,600 people, GRDP VND1,778,271 billion (2024, old boundaries), average enterprise pay VND14.3 million a month (2023) `[data]`.
- **Employers.** Vinamilk, 10 Tan Trao Street, Tan My Ward `[employer-stated]`; foreign investors with over US$8.1 billion in 2025 `[data]`.
- **Standing** (national / Asia-Pacific / world): finance 5 / 2 / 1 (largest credit market and 67th in the GFCI; no cross-city table lifts it in Asia-Pacific).
- **Ratings:** business not rated → present (one headquarters; no source on graduate hiring).

### Hanoi

- **What it is.** 8,717,600 people, GRDP VND1,425,521 billion (2024), pay VND12.8 million (2023) `[data]`.
- **Employers.** FPT Corporation (headquarters; 54,110 employees) and Techcombank (head office; 12,705 employees) `[employer-stated]`.
- **Standing:** IT 4 / 2 / 1; finance 4 / 2 / 1 (judgements: a national 5 would need a statistic).
- **Ratings:** IT, software and finance not rated → present; banking → present.

### Hai Phong

- **What it is.** 2,124,500 people, GRDP VND445,995 billion (2024), pay VND12.2 million (2023) `[data]`.
- **Port.** 2 million TEU handled in 2025; Hateco Hai Phong International Container Terminal opened in February 2025 `[data]`.
- **Standing:** logistics 4 / 2 / 1. **Rating:** logistics not rated → strong (a statistic and the growth claim).

### Da Nang

- **What it is.** 1,276,000 people, GRDP VND151,307 billion (2024, old boundaries), pay VND10.9 million (2023) `[data]`; GFCI 71st (new entry) `[practitioner consensus]`.
- **Employers.** FPT's semiconductor R&D centre `[employer-stated]`.
- **Standing:** IT 3 / 2 / 1. **Rating:** IT present.

## 5. Hypotheses tested

**H1. "Vietnam is open to European graduates because interns need no permit."** Partly supported: the work-permit exemption under Decree 219/2025/ND-CP applies only to currently enrolled students with a formal internship agreement certified by DOLISA; an expert work permit requires a degree plus at least 2 years of experience (1 year in tech/finance). A fresh graduate without prior certified work experience cannot qualify for a standard work permit.

**H2. "Ho Chi Minh City is the finance capital."** Partly supported: it is the largest economy and the best-ranked in the GFCI (67th), yet the two named banks in the sources are headquartered in Hanoi.

**H3. "Hai Phong's growth means graduate logistics jobs."** Insufficient evidence: port volume is read as a statistic, not as hiring.

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

(a) Comparing the pre-merger pay and output figures with the merged areas in the growth claims; (b) reading enterprise average pay as a graduate starting salary; (c) treating a legal-news summary of a decree as the decree.

## 7. Decision rules

1. If the target is IT services or a bank head office, prefer Hanoi (FPT, Techcombank).
2. If the target is consumer goods or a broad corporate role, prefer Ho Chi Minh City (Vinamilk and the largest market).
3. If the target is port and shipping, look at Hai Phong.
4. If you have an internship agreement while an active student, you may be exempt from the work permit (Decree 219/2025/ND-CP, art. 7); see `visas_immigration/vietnam/vietnam_visas_immigration_guide.md`.
5. Compare hubs on the same basis: pre-merger city figures, not merged units.

## 8. Gaps and claims to verify

1. Decree 219/2025/ND-CP text verified on 2026-10-05: expert requires degree + 2 years of experience (1 year in tech/digital/finance); intern exemption requires active student enrollment and DOLISA certification.
