# South Korea: country brief

Today is 3 October 2026 (Numbeo rent for Seoul was read on 4 October). Tags: `[data]` official statistics, `[employer-stated]`, `[practitioner consensus]` (published indices), `[anecdotal]` (crowd-sourced). Won (₩) unless stated.

## 1. Bottom line

- Seoul is the hub for finance and corporate roles: it ranks 7th in the Global Financial Centres Index 40 (September 2026) and 5th in Asia/Pacific [practitioner consensus]; it produced ₩575.0 trillion in 2024, 22.5% of national GDP, and 33.0% of the country's services output [data].
- Seoul pays most: ₩4,852,000 a month per regular worker in April 2026, 113.0% of the national ₩4,294,000, and ₩10,170,000 in finance and insurance (Ministry of Employment and Labor) [data]. These are all-age averages including overtime and bonuses, not entry pay.
- Start-ups and AI: Startup Genome 2026 ranks Seoul 9th in the world and 3rd in Asia, 4th in the world for funding momentum, with a top-ten global rank for talent strength [practitioner consensus].
- Busan is the logistics hub: the Port of Busan handled 24.4 million TEU in 2024, 76.6% of Korea's container cargo and 96.8% of its transhipment, and is the world's seventh-largest container port (KOTRA / Invest Korea) [data]. Busan is also 22nd in the GFCI (Z/Yen).
- Pangyo (Seongnam) is the software cluster: about 64,000 employees, with Kakao, NHN, NCSOFT, Nexon and AhnLab (KOTRA, 2019 figures) [data].
- Korean graduate employment is not easy: 69.5% of the 2024 graduating class were counted as employed, and 62.8% of university graduates (Ministry of Education and KEDI) [data].
- The entry door is recent: from 29 October 2025 graduates of Korean universities can hold the job-seeker visa (D-10) a year at a time for up to three years, and graduates aged 29 or under from a top-200 foreign university skip its points test (Ministry of Justice) [data]. Language needs were not researched.

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size.** Nominal regional GDP in 2024 (Statistics Korea): national ₩2,560.8 trillion; Gyeonggi 651.4, Seoul 575.0, Incheon 125.6, Busan 121.1; the capital region (Seoul, Gyeonggi, Incheon) is 52.8% of the total [data]. Real national growth was 2.0%.

**Pay.** April 2026, total monthly pay per regular worker (all industries): Seoul ₩4,852,000, Gyeonggi 4,309,000, Incheon 3,899,000, Busan 3,741,000; national 4,294,000. Seoul's finance and insurance pay ₩10,170,000 (Ministry of Employment and Labor, 30 Sep 2026) [data]. Nationally the monthly wage per worker in July 2026 was ₩4,316,000 across all workers and ₩8,386,000 in finance and insurance [data]. These figures are for all ages and include overtime and bonuses.

**Graduate labour market.** 2024 class: employment rate 69.5% (70.3% a year earlier); universities 62.8%, junior colleges 72.1%, graduate schools 82.1%; by field 69.0% in social sciences and 70.4% in engineering. The counted employed are mostly those on a health-insurance payroll (87.0%) plus freelancers (7.4%) (Ministry of Education and KEDI) [data]. No starting-pay figure or recruiting calendar was read.

**Language.** Not researched. A university tells students that proof of Korean ability is needed for off-campus work unless the job is in English (Yonsei) [practitioner consensus].

**Visa headline.** British, Italian and EU citizens can visit 90 days and are K-ETA exempt until 31 December 2026 (Ministry of Justice). Degree students may work up to 30 hours a week on weekdays with TOPIK 3/4 and permission (10-15h without TOPIK); after graduating the student visa lasts at most 30 days, so switch to the D-10 before it lapses (fee KRW 135,000 in person / KRW 115,000 online). D-10: a year at a time, up to three years, for Korean graduates; top-200 foreign graduates (QS or THE rankings) aged 29 or under skip the points test (Ministry of Justice, 27 Oct 2025). Full visa rules across all 10 cases are consolidated in [visas_immigration/south_korea/south_korea_visas_immigration_guide.md](../../visas_immigration/south_korea/south_korea_visas_immigration_guide.md).

## 3. Hubs

Metrics in the Atlas: population is the city (register-based census, 1 November 2025); GDP is the region, which for Seoul, Busan and Incheon is the city itself and for Pangyo is Gyeonggi Province; wage is the region's April 2026 regular-worker pay.

### Seoul
- **What it is.** 9.32 million residents (down 0.2% in a year); GRDP ₩575.0 trillion; services 92.4% of output, finance and insurance 7.0% [data].
- **Employers.** Shinhan Financial Group (20 Sejong-daero 9-gil, Jung-gu) [employer-stated]; the Seoul Fintech Lab at Yeouido supports growth-stage fintechs (Seoul government). No other bank, chaebol or platform page loaded (KB, Hana, Hyundai).
- **Standing.** Finance 5-4-4: first in Korea; GFCI puts it 5th in Asia/Pacific (top handful) and 7th in the world (New York and London are the benchmark 5s). Business 5-3-2: a third of national services output but no cross-country comparison read.
- **Start-ups.** 9th in the world, 3rd in Asia (Startup Genome 2026) [practitioner consensus].
- **Rent.** ₩1,187,802 a month, one-bedroom in the centre (Numbeo, crowd-sourced, 4 Oct 2026) [anecdotal].

### Busan
- **What it is.** 3.24 million (down 0.7%); GRDP ₩121.1 trillion; wage ₩3,741,000, 87.1% of the national figure [data].
- **Employers.** Port of Busan (24.4 million TEU, 2024) [data]. Busan is 22nd in the GFCI, ninth in Asia/Pacific.
- **Rating and standing.** Logistics dominant (one data claim: 76.6% of national container cargo); standing 5-4-3: first in Korea, seventh in the world by container volume. Finance 4-3-2 (GFCI).
- **Start-ups.** 71st to 80th among emerging ecosystems, 26th to 30th in Asia [practitioner consensus].
- **Rent.** ₩965,714 (Numbeo) [anecdotal].

### Seongnam (Pangyo)
- **What it is.** 894,000 in Seongnam city; Gyeonggi Province GRDP ₩651.4 trillion, the largest of the regions; Gyeonggi wage ₩4,309,000 [data].
- **Employers.** Pangyo Techno Valley: about 64,000 employees, tenant sales of ₩107.2 trillion in 2019; Kakao, NHN, NCSOFT, Nexon, AhnLab (KOTRA) [data].
- **Rating and standing.** IT and software strong; standing 4-3-2 (no ranking comparing Pangyo with other Asian clusters was read).
- **Rent.** Not available: Numbeo has no Seongnam page.

### Incheon (Songdo)
- **What it is.** 3.09 million (up 1.2%, the fastest-growing region); GRDP ₩125.6 trillion; wage ₩3,899,000 [data].
- **Employers.** Samsung Biologics (300 Songdobio-daero) [employer-stated]; Songdo has the world's largest biopharmaceutical production capacity of any city, according to KOTRA (2021); Celltrion and Samsung Bioepis are based there [data].
- **Standing.** Business 3-2-1. No family rated: the sources give capacity, not hiring by role. Incheon is a GFCI associate centre.
- **Rent.** ₩804,679 (Numbeo) [anecdotal].

## 5. Hypotheses tested

1. **"Seoul is Korea's only finance hub."** Supported: GFCI ranks Seoul 7th and Busan 22nd, no other Korean city listed; Incheon is an associate centre [practitioner consensus]. Busan holds second place only in the ranking, with no employer data read.
2. **"The capital region offers the better pay."** Supported for Seoul (113.0% of national) and Gyeonggi (100.4%); Incheon (90.8%) and Busan (87.1%) are below [data].
3. **"Korean graduates find it easy to get hired, so foreigners will too."** Not supported: the employment rate of university graduates is 62.8% and the rate fell for the 2024 class [data].
4. **"Pangyo pays like Seoul."** Insufficient evidence: only Gyeonggi-wide pay (₩4,309,000, 11% below Seoul) was read [data].

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

- Reading Seoul's finance pay (₩10.17 million a month) as entry pay. It is an average for all ages with bonuses.
- Treating the job-seeker visa as instant: after graduating the student visa lasts at most 30 days, so the D-10 application has to be ready before it lapses (Ajou).
- Comparing Gyeonggi's output (₩651.4 trillion) with Seoul's as city figures; Gyeonggi is a province of 14 million.
- Assuming the K-ETA exemption is permanent: for British citizens it runs only to 31 December 2026 (FCDO).

## 7. Decision rules

1. If you want finance, consulting or a corporate head-office role and can learn Korean, prefer Seoul: finance 5-4-4 and the highest pay.
2. If you want logistics or shipping, go to Busan: it handles 76.6% of Korea's container cargo.
3. If you want software or games, look at Pangyo, within commuting distance of Seoul.
4. If you graduate from a Korean university, plan the D-10 switch before the 30 days lapse, and note it now runs a year at a time for up to three years.
5. If you graduate from a top-200 foreign university and are 29 or under, use the points-test exemption.
6. If your goal is biopharma operations, Songdo (Incheon) is the cluster, but no hiring evidence by role was found.
