# Greece (Atlas brief, round 5, 3 October 2026)

Tags: [data], [employer-stated], [practitioner consensus], [anecdotal]. The record is `data/atlas/gr.js`; the quotes behind each claim are in `research/verification/round-5b.md`.

## 1. Bottom line

- **The graduate job market is the EU's weakest.** In 2025, 64.0% of Greek tertiary graduates aged 20 to 34 who had left education up to three years earlier were in work, the lowest of the 36 countries Eurostat lists (EU 85.3%); among all 20-to-34-year-old graduates the figure is 80.3% (EU 88.4%) [data]. Youth unemployment under 25 is 15.6% (August 2026), close to the EU's 15.4% [data].
- **Athens is the market for IT and finance.** Eurostat counts 76,220 jobs in information and communication in the Athens metropolitan region (70% of Greece's) and 46,230 in finance and insurance (59%), in 2021; it is 13th and 16th of 152 European metropolitan regions on those counts [data]. Thessaloniki has about 9% of each.
- **The country has fewer such jobs than the EU.** Information and communication were 2.5% of Greek employment in 2025 (EU 3.5%) and finance and insurance 1.7% (EU 2.3%) [data]. Athens is a regional centre of a small sector, not a large one.
- **Shipping is the world-scale industry.** Greek-owned ships number 5,691 and hold about 20% of world capacity; the Union of Greek Shipowners (Piraeus) says shipping accounts for 7% to 8% of GDP and about 160,000 jobs, at pay about three times the private average [employer-stated, industry estimate].
- **Graduate programmes ask for Greek.** HELLENiQ ENERGY's two-year Empowering Future Leaders has commercial and corporate routes for graduates in economic sciences but requires Greek and English at C2; METLEN's Engineers in Action requires excellent Greek and English [employer-stated].
- **Pay is low against the EU.** In 2022 mean monthly earnings in information and communication were €2,011 against €4,026 in the EU, and in finance and insurance €2,719 against €4,234 [data].
- **For non-EU readers**: one-year type H.11 job-search permit after study (Law 5038/2023 art. 119), convertible in-country to a work permit without quotas [data].

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size and structure.** Greece's GDP was €206.6 billion in 2022, and 5,226,000 people were in employment in 2025 (Eurostat) [data]. The Athens metropolitan region produced €82.6 billion in 2021, 46% of the country's €181.5 billion, and Thessaloniki €16.0 billion [data]. Beyond shipping and tourism, the headquarters read are in banking (National Bank of Greece, Piraeus Bank, Alpha Bank), telecoms (OTE), energy (HELLENiQ ENERGY, Motor Oil, METLEN) and cement (Titan) [data].

**Labour market.** Unemployment was 7.4% in August 2026 (EU 6.1%) and 15.6% for under-25s (EU 15.4%) (Eurostat) [data]. Graduate employment is the weak point: 64.0% (within three years), 66.9% (one to three years) and 80.3% for all aged 20 to 34. The library records tertiary unemployment of 14.8% at ages 25 to 29 and a clear push to emigrate (places/origin-countries-eu.md §7) [data].

**Entry pay.** No entry-pay figure by field was found. Eurostat's 2022 structure of earnings survey (firms with ten or more employees, all employees) gives mean monthly gross earnings of €2,011 in information and communication (EU €4,026), €2,719 in finance and insurance (EU €4,234) and €1,824 in professional, scientific and technical activities (EU €3,876); annual means €27,043 and €37,468 [data]. It is older than 2023 and not graduate pay.

**How hiring works.** HELLENiQ ENERGY: Empowering Future Leaders, two years, four routes (technical, commercial, digital transformation, corporate: human resources, finance, procurement), for graduates in engineering or economic sciences with up to two years' experience; the page shows no dates [employer-stated]. METLEN: Engineers in Action, a one-year paid internship; applications ran from 15 September to 20 October 2025; more than 260 engineers have taken part in Greece, 125 still with the company [employer-stated]. Pfizer's Thessaloniki Center for Digital Innovation recruited 15 young professionals from a software and cloud bootcamp in 2023 [employer-stated]. The banks' graduate pages (Eurobank, National Bank, Alpha) could not be read; the Piraeus Bank programme found is from 2018 to 2020.

**Language.** Greek is needed for most programmes read: C2 Greek and English at HELLENiQ ENERGY, excellent Greek and English at METLEN [employer-stated].

**Tax.** Article 5C of the Income Tax Code halves income tax on Greek employment income for up to seven years for people who move their tax residence to Greece after not being resident for five of the previous six years; see places/origin-countries-eu.md §10 [data, library].

**Visa headline.** EU citizens over three months register in person at the police for a certificate costing €0.50 that does not expire; non-EU students may work part-time up to 20 hours a week (Law 5038/2023 art. 118); graduates get a 1-year job-search permit (type H.11, art. 119) [data]. Detail: visas_immigration/greece/greece_visas_immigration_guide.md.

**Metric basis.** Population: Eurostat metropolitan regions, 1 January 2023 (area: metro). GDP: Eurostat metropolitan regions, 2021, the latest year published (metro). Pay and rent: not shown, because no regional pay table was found and Numbeo refused automated access.

## 3. Hubs

### Athens and Piraeus

**What it is.** The capital region and the main concentration of Greek head offices, with the port of Piraeus. 3,626,216 residents (2023), €82.6 billion of GDP (2021) and 1,733,940 jobs (2021) (Eurostat) [data].

**Sectors and named employers.** Headquarters read in the Global LEI Index [data]: National Bank of Greece (86 Eolou Street), Piraeus Bank (Amerikis 4), Alpha Bank (40 Stadiou Street), the Piraeus Port Authority (10 Akti Miaouli), OTE (99 Kifissias Avenue, Marousi), HELLENiQ ENERGY (Cheimarras 8A, Marousi), Motor Oil Hellas (12A Irodou Attikou, Marousi), METLEN Energy & Metals (8 Artemidos, Marousi), Titan Cement Company (Chalkidos 22A) and Aegean Airlines (Athens International Airport, Spata). Deloitte Greece's companies are registered at Marousi [employer-stated]. Graduate programmes: HELLENiQ ENERGY and METLEN above.

**Universities and schools.** None ranked here: no university ranking was read.

**Start-ups.** Startup Genome's GSER 2026 gives Athens an Ecosystem Value of $4 billion (global average $25 billion, European average $14.3 billion) and $124 million of early-stage funding in the second half of 2023 to 2025 (global average $554 million) [practitioner consensus]. The Global Financial Centres Index 40 puts Athens 114th of 117 centres, and 113th for fintech [practitioner consensus].

**Cost.** Not shown (see metric basis).

**Standing.**
- *IT (information and communication):* national 5, regional 3, world 1. First in Greece (70% of jobs); 13th of 152 European metropolitan regions on jobs, so a notable centre; GSER funding and value are well below global averages.
- *Finance:* national 5, regional 2, world 1. First in Greece (59% of jobs; three bank headquarters); 16th of 152 on jobs but 114th in the GFCI 40, so the lower reading is taken.
- *Business:* national 5, regional 2, world 1. Large energy, telecoms and banking groups with headquarters and a graduate scheme; a judgement from named employers.
- *Logistics:* national 5, regional 4, world 3. Greek owners hold about 20% of world fleet capacity, the largest of any country, and the shipowners' union and the port authority sit in Piraeus; the family is wider than shipowning and no ranking of maritime cities was read, so the world step is held at 3.

### Thessaloniki

**What it is.** Greece's second city and northern port: 1,089,819 residents (2023), €16.0 billion of GDP (2021) and 489,300 jobs (2021) (Eurostat) [data].

**Sectors and named employers.** 10,160 jobs in information and communication (68th of 152 European metropolitan regions) and 7,400 in finance and insurance (74th) [data]. The Thessaloniki Port Authority (Pier A, inside the port) is headquartered there [data]. Pfizer's Center for Digital Innovation says it has about 500 employees [employer-stated]; METLEN lists Thessaloniki among its Engineers in Action sites and a Deloitte company is registered in the port zone [employer-stated].

**Start-ups and universities.** Startup Genome has no Thessaloniki page; no university ranking was read.

**Standing.**
- *IT:* national 4, regional 2, world 1. Second in Greece, one-seventh of Athens's count (68th of 152).
- *Finance:* national 4, regional 2, world 1. Second in Greece (74th of 152).

## 5. Hypotheses tested

**H1. "Greece's problem is unemployment among the young."** Not supported on 2026 data. Under-25 unemployment is 15.6%, about the EU's 15.4%, while graduate employment within three years is the EU's lowest (64.0% against 85.3%) [data]. The weakness is in converting degrees into jobs for the recent graduate, not in headline youth unemployment.

**H2. "Athens is Greece's only market for IT and finance."** Supported. 70% and 59% of the national counts; Thessaloniki has 9% of each (Eurostat, 2021) [data]. It fails for engineering and pharma-digital jobs, where METLEN and Pfizer name Thessaloniki sites.

**H3. "Shipping is a graduate-hiring industry."** Insufficient evidence. The shipowners' union claims 160,000 jobs and pay three times the private average, but no graduate programme or intake from a shipping company was read [employer-stated, industry estimate].

**H4. "Greek graduate schemes are open to English speakers."** Not supported for the two read: HELLENiQ ENERGY requires Greek and English at C2 and METLEN requires excellent Greek and English [employer-stated].

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

1. **"Greece has a big finance sector because it has big banks."** Finance and insurance are 1.7% of employment against 2.3% in the EU (Eurostat, 2025) [data].
2. **"Shipping jobs are Athens graduate jobs."** The shipowners' figures count direct and indirect jobs and are an industry estimate, with no entry-level split [employer-stated].
3. **The 50% tax cut applies to anyone who moves.** It needs five of the previous six years as a non-resident and Greek-source employment (library, art. 5C) [data].
4. **"A Greek master's leads to deportation after graduating."** False: Law 5038/2023 art. 119 grants a 1-year job-search permit (type H.11) convertible directly to employment without quotas [data].

## 7. Decision rules

1. If you want IT or finance in Greece, aim at Athens: it holds 70% and 59% of the country's jobs (Eurostat, 2021).
2. If you speak no Greek, do not plan around Greek graduate schemes: the two read ask for C2 or excellent Greek.
3. If you are an engineering graduate, METLEN's Engineers in Action lists Athens, Thessaloniki, Viotia, Corinth and Volos; apply from mid-September to October.
4. If you want a digital job outside Athens, look at Pfizer's Thessaloniki centre (about 500 staff, bootcamp hires).
5. If you are a non-EU student, apply for the type H.11 permit (art. 119) at least 30 days before your student permit expires to look for work or start a business [data].
6. If you compare offers with the EU, remember mean pay in finance and in information and communication was about 64% and 50% of the EU mean in 2022 (€2,719 against €4,234; €2,011 against €4,026) [data; arithmetic].
7. If you consider returning to Greece after years abroad, check article 5C's five-of-six-years test before relying on the tax cut.
