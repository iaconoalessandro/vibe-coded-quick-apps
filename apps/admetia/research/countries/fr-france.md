# France: country brief

Today is 3 October 2026. Tags: `[data]` official statistics or government pages, `[employer-stated]`, `[practitioner consensus]` (published indices and specialist synthesis), `[anecdotal]`. Pay figures are means for **all** full-time-equivalent private-sector employees of a département, not graduate starting salaries.

## 1. Bottom line

- France has one graduate market and nine small ones. Paris (with the neighbouring Hauts-de-Seine) holds 39% of French jobs in information and communication and 28% of those in finance and insurance (Eurostat, 2023), and is first and second of 697 EU regions at that level for information-and-communication jobs [data]. The next département has 65,800 ICT jobs (Rhône) and 31,600 finance and insurance jobs (Nord).
- Paris is 5 of 5 nationally in business, management, finance, IT, software and AI. In Europe it is 5 for business and management (Europe's highest concentration of Fortune Global 500 headquarters), 4 for finance, IT, software and AI; at world scale it is 3 for business, management, software and AI (Kearney's 2025 Global Cities Index ranks it third; Startup Genome puts it 13th) and 2 for finance and IT (GFCI 40: 23rd) [practitioner consensus].
- The anchor employers are the big banks, insurers and energy groups: TotalEnergies, EDF, BNP Paribas, Société Générale, Crédit Agricole, Christian Dior, Carrefour, AXA, ENGIE and BPCE are the ten largest Paris Region headquarters on Fortune's Global 500 of July 2024, with 75,000 to 305,000 employees each worldwide [practitioner consensus].
- Entry is through the final internship: 42.3% of grande-école graduates in work were hired by their final internship or apprenticeship host (CGE, library-verified), and about 4% of French job postings say French is not required [data]. English-only entry is realistic mainly in investment banking, trading and tech.
- Outside Paris the useful rule is one hub per family: Toulouse for aerospace and space (CNES, Thales Alenia Space, Airbus) and ICT; Lyon and Lille for ICT, finance and retail headquarters; Marseille for the port; Sophia Antipolis for technology; Grenoble for semiconductors.
- Pay is lower outside the capital by a third or more: mean gross private-sector pay per full-time equivalent in 2022 was about €4,903 a month in Paris, €5,521 in Hauts-de-Seine, €3,661 in Rhône (Lyon), €3,549 in Haute-Garonne (Toulouse) and €3,218 in Nord (Lille), against €3,466 for France (INSEE, from annual figures ÷ 12) [data].
- Rent is the other side: about €1,380 a month for a one-bedroom flat in central Paris against €724-994 in the regional cities (Numbeo, crowd-sourced) [anecdotal].
- Youth unemployment is high: 19.8% for 15-to-24-year-olds in 2025 (17.3% in 2023), and 8.7% for tertiary-educated 25-to-29-year-olds against 7.7% overall (Eurostat) [data]. A graduate who finds a first job does well; finding it takes longer than in Germany or the Netherlands.

## 2. The economy and the graduate labour market

**Size and structure.** France's GDP was €2,991 billion in 2025 (€2,935 billion in 2024), behind Germany's €4,530 billion (Eurostat, nama_10_gdp, updated 2 October 2026) [data]. The Paris Region alone produced €783 billion in 2022, 30% of France's GDP and 5% of the EU's, with 12.3 million inhabitants (CCI Paris Île-de-France and Choose Paris Region, Facts & Figures 2025) [data]. The record's sectors are corporate headquarters, banking and insurance, luxury and fashion, aerospace, start-ups and life sciences.

**Graduates and youth.** Unemployment among 15-to-24-year-olds was 17.3% in 2023, 18.8% in 2024 and 19.8% in 2025; overall unemployment was 7.4%, 7.4% and 7.7%; tertiary-educated 25-to-29-year-olds had 7.1%, 7.5% and 8.7% (Eurostat, une_rt_a and lfsa_urgaed) [data]. The grandes écoles' net employment rate six months after graduation was 76% in the Conférence des grandes écoles' 2026 survey, against 90.5% in 2023 (library-verified; response rates fell to 36.6% for the latest cohort, so compare with care) [data].

**Pay.** INSEE's private-sector tables for 2022 (published 29 August 2024) give mean gross pay per full-time equivalent of €41,594 a year in France (about €3,466 a month). By sector: information and communication €59,881, finance and insurance €66,925. In Paris département the same measures are €68,381 and €104,003; in Hauts-de-Seine €71,791 and €88,253 (INSEE tables T401B and T402) [data]. Finance pay in Paris is therefore 55% above the national sector mean, and all of these include experienced staff. Starting pay by field was not found (gap).

**How hiring works.** The final-year internship or apprenticeship is the main door: 42.3% of employed grande-école graduates were hired by that host (CGE 2026, library) [data]. This brief did not source a recruiting calendar for named employers (see the library's recruiting-calendar file).

**Language.** About 4% of French job postings say French is not required (Indeed Hiring Lab, October 2024); English-only entry is realistic mainly in investment banking, trading and tech [data].

**Permits.** EU, EEA and Swiss citizens need no permit; non-EU students need proof of funds of €877.50/month (from 1 Aug 2026, décret 2026-526) and may work up to 964 hours a year; post-master RECE card lasts 1 year; Talent card (salarié qualifié) requires €39,582/yr, and Carte Bleue Européenne €59,373/yr [data]. Full verified guide: [`visas_immigration/france/france_visas_immigration_guide.md`](../../visas_immigration/france/france_visas_immigration_guide.md).

## 3. Hubs

Method. **Population and GDP** are Eurostat metropolitan regions (population on 1 January 2023; GDP at current prices for 2021). They are larger than the cities: Paris's metropolitan region has 12.4 million people; Sophia Antipolis has no region of its own and uses Nice's. **Pay** is INSEE's mean gross pay per full-time equivalent in the private sector for 2022, by département (a département is not a city: Paris département is the city, Hauts-de-Seine is not counted in Paris's figure), annual ÷ 12. **Rent** is Numbeo's crowd-sourced one-bedroom flat in the city centre, 2026 [anecdotal]. **Jobs by sector** are Eurostat employment by NUTS 3 region (the départements), 2023. **National steps** for a family are set by rank among French urban areas for the family's jobs, with the Paris region counted once: 5 first; 4 second to fifth; 3 sixth to fifteenth; 2 sixteenth to fortieth. Regional and world steps need a published ranking or an EU-wide statistic.

### Paris

**What it is.** The capital and the only deep market. Metropolitan population 12.39 million; GDP €757.6 billion (2021); Paris département mean private-sector pay €4,903 a month; centre flat about €1,380. Paris has 2,237,200 jobs, 245,500 in information and communication and 139,400 in finance and insurance; Hauts-de-Seine has 1,267,900, 204,200 and 87,100 (Eurostat, 2023) [data].

**Named employers.** The ten largest Paris Region headquarters on Fortune's Global 500 (July 2024), as quoted by Choose Paris Region: TotalEnergies (102,579 employees), EDF (171,863), BNP Paribas (182,656), Société Générale (124,089), Crédit Agricole (75,125), Christian Dior (197,141), Carrefour (305,333), AXA (94,705), ENGIE (97,297) and Groupe BPCE (97,835) [practitioner consensus]. AXA's own page gives 156,000 employees and distributors in 52 countries [employer-stated]. Paris La Défense has 200,000 employees and more than 2,800 companies, 75% of them headquarters (Paris La Défense guide, March 2026), and an EY-ULI ranking of 2025 placed it first among Europe's business districts [data]. STATION F calls itself the world's biggest start-up campus, with more than 1,000 start-ups on site [employer-stated].

**Universities and business schools.** The record lists HEC, ESSEC, ESCP and INSEAD programmes for Paris and SKEMA and EDHEC for Sophia Antipolis; no ranking was sourced in this brief (gap).

**Start-ups and finance.** Startup Genome (2026): 13th in the world, second in Europe, second in Europe for talent, top ten in the world for funding momentum and for its AI-native cluster; ecosystem value $169 billion against Europe's average of $14.3 billion and $47 billion of venture funding in 2021-2025 [practitioner consensus]. May 2025's Choose France Summit secured 53 investment projects worth $46.9 billion, AI infrastructure and data centres a substantial share [practitioner consensus]. GFCI 40: 23rd in the world, sixth in Western Europe behind London, Zurich, Geneva, Luxembourg and Lugano [practitioner consensus]. EY: Paris drew 30 foreign finance investment projects in 2025 (23 in 2024) and investors ranked it the most attractive city for financial-services investment [data, library].

### Toulouse

**What it is.** Airbus's home and Europe's space centre. Metropolitan population 1.47 million; GDP €59.0 billion (2021); Haute-Garonne mean pay €3,549 a month; centre flat about €788. Haute-Garonne has 751,800 jobs, 74,300 in manufacturing, 47,400 in information and communication (fourth of 101 départements) and 176,400 in finance, real estate and professional and business services (Eurostat, 2023) [data].

**Employers.** Airbus has its headquarters and the final assembly lines for all its commercial aircraft in France, where it employs more than 56,000 people [employer-stated]. Thales Alenia Space's French headquarters is in Toulouse, the largest of its nine industrial facilities in Europe, with more than 2,800 people (25 September 2023) [employer-stated]. Invest in Toulouse counts 16,000 space-sector jobs, a quarter of European space jobs by its reckoning, and 1,700 CNES employees in the city [data]. Startup Genome values the ecosystem at $2 billion [practitioner consensus]. Mean gross pay in Haute-Garonne was €50,562 in ICT and €54,540 in finance (2022) [data].

**Standing.** IT 4 / 2 / 1: fourth of 101 départements for ICT jobs (third urban area after Paris and Lyon), ecosystem value well under Europe's average; management 3 / 2 / 1 on the Airbus and Thales Alenia Space headquarters. Ratings: IT strong, management present.

### Lyon

**What it is.** France's second ICT market. Metropolitan population 1.92 million; GDP €97.3 billion (2021); Rhône mean pay €3,661; centre flat about €777. Rhône has 1,100,000 jobs (fourth in France), 65,800 in information and communication (third), 31,000 in finance and insurance (fourth) and 93,400 in manufacturing (second) (Eurostat, 2023) [data]. Startup Genome values the ecosystem at $9 billion [practitioner consensus]; the library flags Lyon's 66,200 life-science jobs. Pay in ICT was €53,320 and in finance €60,271 (2022) [data].

**Standing.** IT 4 / 2 / 1 and finance 4 / 2 / 1 on second place among urban areas for ICT jobs and third or fourth for finance. Ratings: IT and finance strong.

### Marseille

**What it is.** Port city and second-largest finance market among the regions in the 2022 metropolitan data. Metropolitan population 3.18 million; GDP €125.4 billion (2021); Bouches-du-Rhône mean pay €3,420; centre flat about €803. The département has 996,400 jobs (fifth), 23,800 in finance and insurance (sixth) and 222,400 in finance, real estate and professional services (fifth) [data]. The port handled 66.0 million tonnes in 2024, eighth among the EU's 20 largest ports and second in France after HAROPA (76.7 million) [data]. Startup Genome: ecosystem value $2 billion [practitioner consensus].

**Standing.** Logistics 4 / 3 / 1: second French port, eighth in the EU, far below Rotterdam; finance 4 / 2 / 1. Ratings: finance and logistics strong.

### Lille

**What it is.** Retail and e-commerce headquarters on the Belgian border. Metropolitan population 2.61 million (the Lille-Dunkerque-Valenciennes region); GDP €85.9 billion (2021); Nord mean pay €3,218, the lowest of the hubs; centre flat about €821. Hello Lille says the metropolitan area has 558,000 employees, 136,000 students, more than 80 headquarters of retail brands and 40 distributors (including Auchan, Decathlon and Leroy Merlin) and the first concentration of international headquarters in France outside Paris [data]. Nord has 1,123,400 jobs (third), 106,900 in manufacturing (first) and 31,600 in finance and insurance (third) [data]. Startup Genome: $4 billion [practitioner consensus].

**Standing.** Business, finance, IT 4 / 2 / 1. Ratings: business, finance and IT strong.

### Bordeaux, Nantes, Strasbourg, Grenoble, Sophia Antipolis

**Bordeaux.** Metropolitan population 1.69 million; GDP €61.3 billion; Gironde pay €3,251; flat about €828. Gironde has 812,700 jobs (sixth), 24,900 in finance and insurance (fifth) and 35,500 in ICT (seventh) [data]. Finance 4 / 2 / 1; rated strong.

**Nantes.** Population 1.49 million; GDP €54.8 billion; Loire-Atlantique pay €3,267; flat about €724. 728,400 jobs, 42,900 in ICT (fifth) and 74,300 in manufacturing (fifth) [data]. IT 4 / 2 / 1; rated strong.

**Strasbourg.** Population 1.16 million; GDP €41.4 billion; Bas-Rhin pay €3,327; flat about €888. 532,900 jobs, 68,500 in manufacturing (seventh), 16,100 in ICT (sixteenth) and 14,600 in finance (eleventh) [data]. IT and finance 3 / 2 / 1; no family rated. The seat of European institutions, but no employer figure was read (gap).

**Grenoble.** Population 1.30 million; GDP €44.8 billion; Isère pay €3,469; flat about €735. 561,400 jobs, 74,500 in manufacturing (third) and 19,600 in ICT (fifteenth) [data]. Soitec reports more than 2,100 employees and about €600 million of turnover [employer-stated]. IT and computer science 3 / 2 / 1; no family rated.

**Sophia Antipolis.** Technology park on the Côte d'Azur: "over 1,000 new jobs a year in AI, biotechnology and connected vehicles" (technopole's own page) [employer-stated]. Population, GDP and rent are Nice's (1.11 million; GDP €39.6 billion; flat about €994); Alpes-Maritimes pay €3,348 and 515,100 jobs, 23,700 in ICT (twelfth) [data]. IT 3 / 2 / 1; software and AI present.

## 5. Hypotheses tested

1. **"Paris is the only French graduate market."** Largely supported for finance, IT and corporate headquarters: two départements hold 39% of ICT and 28% of finance jobs. It fails for logistics (Marseille), aerospace and space (Toulouse) and manufacturing, where Nord, Rhône and Isère lead the country.
2. **"A regional salary is a regional discount."** Supported in gross terms (Paris €4,903 against €3,218 in Lille) but partly offset by rent (€1,380 against €724-994); no cost-of-living comparison after tax was made here.
3. **"English is enough in Paris finance."** Partly supported. Only about 4% of postings drop French, concentrated in investment banking, trading and tech; most Paris bank headquarters hire in French.
4. **"Paris is Europe's second finance centre."** Not supported by GFCI 40, which ranks it sixth in Western Europe behind London, Zurich, Geneva, Luxembourg and Lugano; its stronger claim is Eurostat's count of finance and insurance jobs (first of 697 EU regions).
5. **"Lille is a retail town."** Supported on headquarters (Auchan, Decathlon, Leroy Merlin) and 80+ retail-brand headquarters, but Nord also has the country's largest manufacturing job count and the third-largest finance count.

## 6. Common mistakes and myths

- Treating a département as a city: Paris département has 2.24 million jobs, Hauts-de-Seine another 1.27 million [data].
- Reading the Fortune list as "jobs in Paris": the workforce figures are worldwide group totals [practitioner consensus].
- Assuming the grande-école employment rate is the national graduate rate: the CGE response rate was 36.6% for the latest cohort (library).
- Comparing gross pay across cities without rent: Paris centre flats cost about 1.7 to 1.8 times Lyon's or Lille's [anecdotal].
- Expecting English-only entry outside investment banking, trading and tech [data].

## 7. Decision rules

1. If you want corporate, finance or consulting work in France, prefer Paris; no other hub is within a fifth of its jobs.
2. If you want aerospace, space or defence-adjacent business roles, prefer Toulouse (Airbus, Thales Alenia Space, CNES).
3. If you want a regional ICT or finance job with lower rent, prefer Lyon first, then Lille, Nantes and Bordeaux.
4. If you want logistics, prefer Marseille for the port (second in France, eighth in the EU).
5. If you want semiconductors, prefer Grenoble or the Dresden hub in Germany: Isère has the third-largest manufacturing job count in France.
6. If you cannot speak French, rule out most of the above except investment banking, trading and tech roles in Paris.
7. If you are non-EU, treat the final internship as the entry route and keep within the 964-hour limit.
8. If you compare offers, compare after rent: gross pay gaps between Paris and the regions are about a third; flat prices differ by up to 1.8 times.
