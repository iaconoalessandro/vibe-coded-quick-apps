**What the data can say.** (a) London-track pay is a long climb from a low base: median UK-domiciled business master's pay rises from £35.1k at one year to £53.4k at ten (1.52x on different cohorts; 1.33x for the single 2012/13 cohort over years 5 to 10), with a wide upper tail (UQ £80.9k at ten years). (b) In Switzerland the age-band medians for business professionals rise 1.45x to 1.54x from "up to 29" to "30-49", which is similar in proportion to the UK curve, so there is **no evidence in these two series that Swiss percentage growth is slower**; the earlier "slower growth after year three" half of H1 is not supported by these data, nor refuted. (c) In level terms the Swiss business-professional median for ages 30-49 (CHF 116.5k, about EUR 123k at CHF 0.9461 per euro) is about twice the UK ten-year master's median (GBP 53.4k, about EUR 62k at GBP 0.85718 per euro); the UK upper quartile at ten years (GBP 80.9k, about EUR 94k) is below the Swiss 25th percentile for ages 30-49 (CHF 7,793 x 12 = CHF 93.5k, about EUR 99k). **This is directional only**: the populations (a job-based occupation group vs all graduates, including part-time and non-graduate jobs), the pay definition (LSE standardised monthly full-time wage, bonuses reported separately in the BFS release, vs tax-record annualised pay) and the time axis (age band vs years since graduation) differ. (d) London's premium sectors (IB, MBB, PE) sit in the LEO upper quartile and above, so the "London overtakes later" story can only apply to that tail, which neither series isolates.

**What the data cannot say.** Ten-year Swiss pay by field or by degree (BFS's five-year survey press release has no levels; the full BFS tables were not located); Swiss versus UK pay for the *same person type* (EU graduate who studied a one-year MiM and works in each country); anything for international graduates at ten years (the LEO international file stops at 5 years); alumni-survey data: HSG and LBS publish no 5+ year alumni salary series that I could find (the LBS MiM employment reports are at graduation; an Emolument self-reported figure for HSG alumni "5 to 10 years" of CHF 201,000 appeared only in a search summary and is [anecdotal]).
