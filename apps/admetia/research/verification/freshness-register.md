# Freshness register

## How to use it

- Every record on the site that depends on one of these facts carries the fact's `checked` date and shows a stale banner when `review_by` passes (the calculator already does this for deadlines: 120 days).
- **Cadence key:** E = event-driven (check when the authority announces); Q = quarterly; Y = yearly; M = monthly during application season (Sept–Mar).
- **Status key:** V = read on the official page by the lead; S = seen only in a secondary source or snippet (verify before use).
