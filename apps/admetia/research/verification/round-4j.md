Same method and status vocabulary as rounds 4a to 4i. Neither country had coverage in the research library. ## P77 Turkey (data/atlas/tr.js)

**Rating notes.** Istanbul finance and banking are *dominant* on the Banks Association of Türkiye's yearbook (Banks in Türkiye 2025, May 2026, table 17): 83,582 of 189,636 bank employees (44%, Admetia's arithmetic) against 18,621 in Ankara, the next province; the bank directory in the same PDF gives Ziraat, Halkbank and Vakıfbank head offices in the Istanbul Finance Centre (Ümraniye) and Akbank's in Levent. The association is an industry body publishing sector statistics, tagged data as CompTIA was in round 4f. Read today: Study in Türkiye (Council of Higher Education), work opportunities; METU international students office, work permits (tagged practitioner consensus); FCDO Turkey entry requirements and warnings (21 Sep 2026); US State Department advisory for Türkiye (Level 2, 9 Jun 2026), read through travel.state.gov's advisories RSS feed because the page returned 403.
