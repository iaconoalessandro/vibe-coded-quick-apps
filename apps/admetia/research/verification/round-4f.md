# Verification round 4f (2–3 October 2026)

> Note (6 Oct 2026): this log is kept as history. For the United States, visa and immigration rules are now consolidated in `visas_immigration/united_states/united_states_visas_immigration_guide.md`; where the two differ, the guide prevails.

Same method and status vocabulary as rounds 4a to 4e. Lithuania, Malta and Iceland had no coverage in the research library; their records rest only on pages read in this round.

Two rule changes found here matter beyond the Atlas. Iceland cut the graduate job-search permit from three years to 18 months and replaced the student work permit with a 60%-of-full-time allowance on 8 July 2026. Lithuania capped first- and second-year bachelor's students at 20 hours a week from 22 May 2026.

## P57 Lithuania (data/atlas/lt.js)

**Rating notes.** Vilnius finance is *strong* on Invest Lithuania's count (data; state agency) that 231 of 248 Lithuanian fintechs are in Vilnius; not *dominant* because fintech is a slice of finance and no statement covers banking or other finance roles. Business, IT and banking are *present* on the business-services report (27,000+ staff; Danske Bank, SEB and others named). Read: Study in Lithuania, work in Lithuania (3-month renewal, then a 12-month job-search permit) and FAQ (20 h a week, dated 14 May 2025); TRINITI law-firm note (25 May 2026) on the 22 May 2026 amendments (first- and second-year bachelor's students capped at 20 h); Invest Lithuania fintech overview 2025–2026 and business services report 2025. **Conflict:** a search summary said master's students may work 40 h a week after May 2026; the law-firm page read does not say so, and the record does not repeat it. The Migration Department (migracija.lrv.lt) returned 403 and migracija.lt rendered empty; not bypassed, so the EU three-month registration step is a gap and the EU route shows only free movement.
