# Verification round 4h (3 October 2026)

Same method and status vocabulary as rounds 4a to 4g. None of the five had coverage in the research library; every claim was read in this round. All five records have one hub; Beijing, Shenzhen, Hsinchu and Hanoi are not mapped.

Two rule changes found here matter beyond the Atlas. Taiwan now lets graduates of its universities work in any job without a permit for up to two years after graduating. Malaysia raised the lowest Employment Pass salary floor from RM3,000 to RM5,000 a month on 1 June 2026.

## P68 Taiwan (data/atlas/tw.js)

**Rating notes.** Taipei finance and banking are *dominant*: the Central Bank's list of domestic banks (updated 1 Oct 2026) gives head-office addresses for 40 banks, of which 35 are in Taipei (34 written 'Taipei City', plus Citibank Taiwan, 'XinYi District, Taipei'); the other five are in Taichung (3), Kaohsiung and Tainan. All 14 domestic property and casualty insurers on the companion list are in Taipei. The count was made by Admetia from the HTML table, and the record says so. Read today: Workforce Development Agency news (15 Jul 2020: 20 hours a week in term, relaxed in breaks; fines up to NT$150,000 and NT$750,000); EZ Work Taiwan, work permit exemption during the extended residence of new graduates (updated 4 Feb 2026: up to two years, any job, no work permit); EZ Work Taiwan, new scoring criteria (updated 5 Sep 2024: 70 points); FCDO Taiwan entry requirements (28 Aug 2026). Secondary sources say the two-year exemption began in 2026; the WDA page read gives no start date, so the record does not state one.
