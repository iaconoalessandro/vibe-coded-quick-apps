# Verification round 4d (2 October 2026)

Same method and status vocabulary as rounds 4a to 4c. CONFIRMED means the page was read on the day; LIBRARY means the claim is carried from a research brief that was itself verified, with that brief's source named.

Two rule changes found in this round matter beyond the Atlas. Sweden cut student work to 15 hours a week in term for permits granted from 11 June 2026 (Migrationsverket, 25 May 2026). Denmark's student limit is 90 hours a month from September to May and full-time in summer (New to Denmark, updated 1 Oct 2026). Neither is yet in places/visas-and-work-rights.md; both belong in its next refresh.
