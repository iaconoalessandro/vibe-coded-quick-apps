# Verification round 4g (3 October 2026)

Same method and status vocabulary as rounds 4a to 4f. New Zealand and South Korea had no coverage in the research library; Japan had a brief, mostly secondary section, now replaced in the record by primary pages. Australia's Department of Home Affairs and Tax Office refused automated reads (403) and were not bypassed.

One rule change found here matters beyond the Atlas. South Korea's job-seeker visa (D-10) became one year at a time for up to three years from 29 October 2025, and graduates aged 29 or under of a foreign top-200 university skip its points test.

## P63 New Zealand (data/atlas/nz.js)

**Rating notes.** Auckland finance is *dominant*: the Auckland Economic Monitor (July 2025; Auckland Council's economic development office with PwC) calls the city centre "the country's commercial hub" and says 37% of New Zealand's GDP in financial and insurance services comes from it. IT is *strong* on the same source (28% of national information media and telecommunications GDP; nearly 40 of the TIN200 tech firms in or around the centre); not dominant, because 40 of 200 is a fifth. ## P64 Japan (data/atlas/jp.js)

**Rating notes.** Tokyo finance is *dominant* on the Tokyo Metropolitan Government's 2023 overview (PDF, extracted locally): 43.5% of the loans of domestically licensed banks (end March 2022) against 15.8% of national employment (2016). Business is *strong* on the same table: 2,391 of 3,174 foreign-affiliated companies (75.3%, 2022, from Toyo Keizai's directory). Banking is *strong*. Read today: Immigration Services Agency, J-Find outline (PDF; top 100 in two of three rankings, within five years, ¥200,000, two years in total including earlier similar stays); Study in Japan (JASSO) pages on part-time work (28 hours a week, eight a day in long holidays), employment (Designated Activities for job hunting: six months plus, as a rule, one six-month extension) and status of residence (Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services; Designated Activities 46 with JLPT N1 or BJT 480); FCDO Japan entry requirements (28 Aug 2026). The 2019 edition of the Tokyo overview was also downloaded; its figures did not extract cleanly and it was not used. ## P65 South Korea (data/atlas/kr.js)

**Rating notes.** No family is rated. Seoul figures by sector were found only behind Statista (Statistics Korea data, 296,873 finance employees in 2022) and were not used; Invest Seoul, the Seoul statistics hub and Invest Korea pages read gave no sector shares. Read today: Ministry of Justice press release, 27 Oct 2025 (Korean, PDF from immigration.go.kr): D-10 one year at a time up to three years, internships with one company up to a year, effective 29 Oct 2025; graduates aged 29 or under of a foreign top-200 university exempt from the D-10 points test and funds and from E-7 field and experience requirements. Yonsei graduate school part-time page (30 hours on weekdays; tagged practitioner consensus because it is a university's summary); Ajou University FAQ (9 Jun 2025; 30 days after graduation; KRW 130,000). **Conflict:** secondary guides (jobploy, kowork) say the 30 weekday hours need TOPIK 4 and are 15 hours otherwise; no government page confirming either was read, so the record states only the Yonsei wording and lists the conflict as a gap. Seoul Metropolitan Government release on the GFCI (27 Mar 2026); FCDO South Korea entry requirements (14 May 2026; K-ETA exemption to 31 Dec 2026).

## P66 Hong Kong (data/atlas/hk.js)

**Rating notes.** Finance is *dominant* on the Census and Statistics Department's Four Key Industries article (Monthly Digest, Dec 2025, PDF extracted locally): 26.2% of GDP and 265,000 jobs in 2024; Hong Kong is a single-hub territory, as with Singapore. Logistics is *strong* (trading and logistics 555,200 jobs, 15.0% of employment; logistics alone 170,700). Banking is *strong* (96,100 staff, 18.9% of GDP). Read today: HKSAR Government press release, 26 Oct 2023 (postgraduate part-time exemption from 1 Nov 2023, two-year trial; old rule 20 hours on campus); CUHK careers office staff briefing, updated 4 Feb 2026 (undergraduates and postgraduates exempt until further notice); FCDO Hong Kong entry requirements (1 Apr 2026). IANG, TTPS and the HKUST survey are carried from places/beyond-europe.md §5, verified 1 Oct 2026. A search summary said the government would review the exemption; that was not on a page read and is not used.
