# Verification round 4e (2 October 2026)

Same method and status vocabulary as rounds 4a to 4d. CONFIRMED means the page was read on the day; LIBRARY means the claim is carried from a research brief that was itself verified, with that brief's source named.

One correction matters beyond the Atlas: Romania raised the student work limit from 4 to 6 hours a day on 8 March 2024 (Law 28/2024). The EU Immigration Portal and several law-firm pages still show 4 hours.

## P52 Poland (data/atlas/pl.js)

**Rating notes.** Business is *strong* in Warsaw, Kraków and Wrocław on ABSL's centre counts (data; employers' association survey), with Kraków also on ABSL's press figure of nearly 108,000 centre jobs. The family is *business* rather than accounting or IT because ABSL's split by function and city was not read. Kraków IT and finance (risk) are *present* on HSBC's service-delivery page; Warsaw software is *present* on Goldman Sachs (trade press) and Bending Spoons. **Conflict:** the library reads 324 Kraków centres from the full ABSL PDF; ABSL's press page says 312. The record keeps the library's figure (full report) and does not repeat the press count. Read: Ministry of the Interior, entry and residence rules (register with the voivode after three months; no deadline stated on that page); Study in Poland (NAWA): full-time students need no work permit; Office for Foreigners: 9-month graduate permit, once only; ABSL press release (24 Jun 2025). Two voivodeship pages failed their TLS certificate checks; not bypassed.

## P53 Czech Republic (data/atlas/cz.js)

**Rating notes.** Prague IT and finance are *strong* on the Czech Statistical Office's Labour Force Survey (Q4 2024): information and communication is 12.0% of Prague main jobs against 4.0% nationally, finance and insurance 6.9% against 2.4%. They are not *dominant*: the source gives shares, not a statement that Prague is the national centre, though by author calculation Prague holds about 42% of Czech ICT jobs (721,200 × 12.0% against 5,204,000 × 4.0%). Business is *present* on the library's Mercer pay figure. Read: gov.cz, reporting the place of residence (30 days) and the EU registration certificate (optional); ipc.gov.cz, free access to the labour market and the 9-month job-search permit; CZSO First hand figures Prague 2024 (PDF, July 2025, extracted locally); Eurostat news 10 Feb 2026 (Praha 191.8% of EU GDP per head in PPS).

## P56 Greece (data/atlas/gr.js)

**Rating notes.** Logistics (shipping) is *present* for Athens and Piraeus on the Union of Greek Shipowners' fleet figures; the record does not say the industry is run from Piraeus beyond the union's own address. No other family is rated. The art. 5C relief is carried from places/origin-countries-eu.md, which read AADE's FAQ; it is listed as tax context, not as a graduate benefit, because a student resident in Greece during the degree may not meet the 5-of-6-years test. Read: mitos.gov.gr registration certificate (PD 106/2007; €0.50; does not expire); EU Immigration Portal, Greece (part-time work; return home after studies); UGS Greek shipping and economy 2025. The Ministry of Labour page on third-country workers does not cover students; no official page with an hours limit was found.
