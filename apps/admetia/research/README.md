# Research library: choosing a business master's and a first career

About 410,000 words of sourced research (in 58 Markdown files; the Italian edition adds about 14,000 words in 3 files) on choosing a pre-experience master's in Management, Finance or Marketing and getting the first job after it. It was written between 30 September and 2 October 2026 for the Admetia site. The primary reader is a 20–25-year-old European, often Italian, bachelor's student or recent graduate. Non-EU readers are covered where rules differ. An Italian edition of the three core guides is in [it/](./it/README.md).

## How to read the evidence

Every non-obvious claim carries a source, a date and one tag:

| Tag | Meaning | How much to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| **[data]** | Official statistics, school employment reports, government portals, survey microdata | High, but check the year, the coverage and who published it (schools publish their own outcomes) |
| **[employer-stated]** | An employer's own page, press release or an executive on the record | High for what the employer does; lower for claims about the market |
| **[practitioner consensus]** | Several independent recruiters, career services or specialist press agree | Medium |
| **[anecdotal]** | Forums, single blogs, self-reported salary sites | Signal only |

Other markers:
- **"snippet only"** means the figure was seen in a search result but the page couldn't be opened. Such figures sit in each file's "Claims to verify" section, not the body.
- **†** in evidence/hypotheses.md means the lead re-checked the figure against the primary source.
- **"author calculation"** means the number was computed in the file, with the method shown (e.g. net pay after tax and rent).

## How current is it?

- Researched 30 September – 2 October 2026. Each file has a `review_by` date; verification/freshness-register.md lists the volatile facts with re-check URLs and dates.
- **The fastest-moving facts:** visa rules (UK Graduate visa, US H-1B, Swiss quotas, UK–EU youth scheme), application deadlines for the 2026–27 cycle, Big 4 and consulting intake, Mediobanca after the MPS takeover. Treat anything in these areas as needing a check after early 2027.
- **Method limits:**
  - The session's web-search budget ran out early, so most evidence was gathered by fetching primary pages directly. That favoured primary sources, but some pages were blocked or script-rendered.
  - About 40 key figures were re-checked by the lead against primary sources in Rounds 1–2, and two errors were found and fixed (see _working/progress.md). In Round 3 the 32 priority claims (P1–P32) went through a dedicated verification pass; the final status of each is in verification/claims-to-verify.md §4.
  - Gaps are listed honestly in verification/claims-to-verify.md.
