# Employer Prestige & Internship Tier Reference

This guide helps you place employers accurately for both the Master's and MBA admissions calculators.
*(Note: These examples are also accessible directly inside the calculator through the "See some examples" dropdowns and company search).*

---

## Master's vs. MBA Employer Questions

| | Master's Calculator | MBA Calculator |
|---|---|---|
| **Question** | "Where those internships were" | "Employer prestige" |
| **Context** | Student **internships** (pre-experience) | Full-time **career / employer** |
| **Input** | One of 4 tiers | Score from **0.0 to 4.0** |
| **Weight** | ~3 points out of 100 | Up to **4 points** direct addition |

> [!WARNING]
> On the master's calculator, leaving the internship tier blank scores **0** (same as "Not applicable"). If you completed internships, always select your tier.

## 3. Important Contextual Distinctions

### Investment Banking: Front-Office vs. Commercial/Support
- **Front-Office Investment Banking / Global Markets** at Bank of America (BofA Securities), Citigroup (CIB), Barclays, Deutsche Bank, or BNP Paribas qualifies as **Score 2.0** (or 3.0 at JPM/MS and 4.0 at Goldman).
- **Commercial Lending, Branch Banking, Back-Office Operations, or IT Support** at those same banks qualifies as **Score 1.0**.

### Big Four: Strategy vs. Audit & Tax
- **Dedicated Strategy Arms** (Strategy& at PwC, EY-Parthenon, Monitor Deloitte) operate with separate hiring, compensation, and case interview processes, scoring **2.0**.
- **Audit, Assurance, Tax, and Risk Advisory** within the Big Four scores **1.0**.

### Private Equity: Investment Team vs. Value Creation vs. Fund-of-Funds
- **Direct Investment Professionals** (Associates conducting LBO modeling, due diligence, and deal execution) at mega-funds score **4.0** (Blackstone, KKR) or **3.0** (Apollo, Carlyle, EQT, CVC).
- **Portfolio Operations / Value Creation / Capital Markets** teams score **3.0** or **2.0**.
- **Fund-of-Funds, Secondaries, and Co-investment** platforms score **2.0** to **3.0**.

### Quant Trading & Prop Shops: Research vs. Trading vs. Development
- All three tracks (Quantitative Research, Quantitative Trading, and Core Software/Systems Engineering) at tier-1 firms like Citadel Securities, Jane Street, Optiver, and Jump Trading command the highest selectivity ceiling (**Score 4.0 or 3.0**).

## Decision Flowchart for Unlisted Companies

1. **Would a business professional in London, New York, Frankfurt, or Singapore recognise the company unprompted?**
   - **Yes** → Global / Score 2.0–4.0 (match with closest listed peer).
   - **No** → Go to Step 2.
2. **Is it a major household name or dominant market leader across its home country (e.g. FTSE 100, DAX 40, CAC 40, FTSE MIB constituent)?**
   - **Yes** → National / Score 1.0.
   - **No** → Go to Step 3.
3. **Is it a well-funded venture-backed scale-up (Series B+ with top VC backing like Sequoia, Index, Accel) or a mid-market leader?**
   - **Yes** → Score 1.0–2.0.
   - **No** → Small/Local / Score 0.0.
