# Employer Prestige & Internship Tier Reference

This guide helps you place employers accurately for both the Master's and MBA admissions calculators.
*(Note: These examples are also accessible directly inside the calculator through the "See some examples" dropdowns).*

---

## Master's vs. MBA Employer Questions

| | Master's Calculator | MBA Calculator |
|---|---|---|
| **Question** | "Where those internships were" | "Employer prestige" |
| **Context** | Student **internships** | Full-time **career / employer** |
| **Input** | One of 4 tiers | Score from **0.0 to 4.0** |
| **Weight** | ~3 points out of 100 | Up to **4 points** direct addition |

> [!WARNING]
> On the master's calculator, leaving the internship tier blank scores **0** (same as "Not applicable"). If you completed internships, always select your tier.

## 1. Master's Internship Tiers

| Tier | Guideline | Representative Peers |
|---|---|---|
| **Global** | Recognised worldwide unprompted by business professionals in any country. | Goldman Sachs, BCG, Google, Coca-Cola, Mercedes-Benz, LVMH |
| **National** | Household name in its home country, but less known abroad. | UniCredit, Intesa Sanpaolo, Deutsche Börse, Eni, Enel, Revolut, BBVA |
| **Small / Local** | Local firms, startups, university research, family businesses, town councils. | Standard default for first student internships (~1.5 pts below National) |
| **Not Applicable** | No internships completed. | — |

## 2. MBA Employer Prestige Scores (0–4 Scale)

| Score | Meaning | Representative Peers |
|---|---|---|
| **4.0** | Top-tier global selectivity ceiling | MBB (McKinsey, Bain, BCG), Goldman Sachs, Google, Amazon, Apple, Meta, OpenAI, Blackstone, KKR, Citadel, Jane Street |
| **3.0** | Elite, globally renowned | Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Lazard, Rothschild, Evercore, BlackRock, Carlyle, Apollo, Bridgewater, Stripe, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Netflix, Tesla, P&G, Nike, LVMH, Disney |
| **2.0** | Large global corporates & top industrials | Tier-2 consultancies (Kearney, Roland Berger, Strategy&), Barclays, UBS, Mediobanca, Visa, Mastercard, ASML, SAP, Spotify, Uber, Unilever, L'Oréal, Coca-Cola, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Ferrari, Airbus, Siemens, World Bank, IMF, UN |
| **1.0** | Major national institutions & Big 4 audit | Big Four audit/tax, Bank of America, Citi, BNP Paribas, HSBC, UniCredit, Intesa Sanpaolo, Deutsche Börse, LSEG, Revolut, Inditex, VW, Bosch, BP, Eni, Enel, Central Banks, Government Ministries |
| **0.5** | Regional or smaller national presence | N26, Infosys, TCS, Barilla |
| **0.0** | Small, local, or unlisted firms | General benchmark default |

## Decision Flowchart for Unlisted Companies

1. **Would a business professional in another country recognise the company unprompted?**
   - **Yes** → Global / Score 2.0–4.0 (match with closest peer)
   - **No** → Go to Step 2
2. **Is it a major household name across its home country?**
   - **Yes** → National / Score 1.0
   - **No** → Small/Local / Score 0.0
