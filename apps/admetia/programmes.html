A reality check on bars, fees and dates

Every requirement here is what the school publishes, tagged with where it was read. Three things are ours, and are labelled as such:

  • “Our bar” is calibration. No school publishes a points requirement. The number is the calculator’s own threshold, shown with its rank in the track so you can see how the models order programmes — not an admission rate.
  • Fees are never converted. They are copied in the school’s currency from our research, for the intake shown. The fee bands compare the number in its own currency (a semester counts twice), so a £ and a € fee in the same band can differ by a fifth. Programmes without a fee in the research say “Not in our data”.
  • Deadlines move. Every date was read on the school’s own page on the day shown beside it. Confirm on the official admissions page before you plan around one.
