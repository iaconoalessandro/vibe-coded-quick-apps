## Building the cube (Assignment 14)
- **Dimensions** come from your dimension tables. **Hierarchies** are drill paths: Date as Year → Quarter → Month → Day, and time of day as Day part → Hour.
- **Measures** come from fact columns: count of events, sum of likes, and so on. Add only what the MDX queries need.
- Seasons, categories and anything else the queries slice by must exist as **attributes**. If they're missing, you go back to the dimension tables, so check the Part 2 queries now.
